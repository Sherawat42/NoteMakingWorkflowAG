4.1 INTRODUCTION  
You may be aware that sample survey has many advantages (lower cost, lesser 
time, limited resources) over a population census. Therefore, we study a sample 
instead of the universe. We find out the sample characteristics andgeneralise the 
sample results to the population. This process is called estimation of parameters. 
Thus, estimation is the process of recovering the unknown parameters that were 
used to generate the sample data. You should have a thorough knowledge of 
inferential statistics, particularly estimation and hypothesis testing given in Unit 
30 and Unit 31 of the course MEC 203: Quantitative Methods. 
The relationship between variables in a population is given by the population 
regression function (PRF). The same relationship in a sample is given by the 
sample regression function (SRF). In simple linear regression, we estimate the 
population regression function (PRF) from the sample regression function (SRF). 
There are three methods of estimation: (i) least squares method, (ii) maximum 
likelihood method, and (iii) method of moments.
