## Section: Course Title & Institutional Information

### Core Idea

This is the cover page and credit section for **MEC-102: Macroeconomic Analysis**, a postgraduate course offered by the School of Social Sciences at Indira Gandhi National Open University (IGNOU), New Delhi. The course provides a comprehensive treatment of macroeconomic theory from classical foundations to modern policy analysis.

### Key Concepts

#### MEC-102 Macroeconomic Analysis

MEC-102 is structured as a graduate-level macroeconomics course covering both traditional and contemporary approaches. It spans 7 blocks and 19 units, progressing from the Classical and Keynesian models through expectations, intertemporal decision-making, business cycle theories, labour market analysis, monetary policy, and fiscal policy. The course is designed for students of the Master of Arts (Economics) programme at IGNOU.

## Section: Expert Committee & Course Preparation Team

### Core Idea

The course was prepared by an expert committee of distinguished economists from leading Indian academic institutions including JNU, University of Mumbai, IIM Ahmedabad, IGIDR, Pondicherry University, University of Delhi, TISS Mumbai, and IGNOU itself. The course coordinator is Prof. Kaustuva Barik of IGNOU.

### Key Concepts

#### Course Contributors

The unit writers and their institutional affiliations span India's leading economics departments. Block 1 (which this textbook covers) was authored by:

Other blocks involve contributors from Delhi School of Economics, St. Stephen's College, Ramjas College, and TISS Mumbai.

#### Block and Unit Structure Overview

The course is organized into 7 thematic blocks containing 19 units total:

- **Block 1 (Units 1-5)**: Traditional Approaches to Macroeconomics — Classical, Keynesian, IS-LM, Open Economy
- **Block 2 (Units 6-7)**: Expectations and Macroeconomics — Phillips Curve, Rational Expectations
- **Block 3 (Units 8-10)**: Intertemporal Decision-Making — Consumption theory, Ramsey-Cass-Koopmans, OLG
- **Block 4 (Units 11-12)**: Business Cycle Theories — Traditional and Real Business Cycles
- **Block 5 (Units 13-14)**: Labour Markets — Rigidities and Search Theory
- **Block 6 (Units 15-17)**: Monetary Policy — Central banks, conduct, and theory
- **Block 7 (Units 18-19)**: Fiscal Policy — Theory and sustainability

### Definitions

- **Block 1 — Traditional Approaches to Macroeconomics**: Covers 5 units spanning the Classical Approach, Keynesian Model, Neoclassical Synthesis (IS-LM), and Open Economy Macroeconomics (I and II). This is the focus of the current textbook.
