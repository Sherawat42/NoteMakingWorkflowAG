## Section: Classical Dichotomy (1.7) — AD-AS Equilibrium (1.7.1) & Neutrality of Money (1.7.2)
<!-- This section starts here and continues into Chunk 008 -->

### Core Idea
<!-- placeholder: Real variables (GDP, capital, employment) are determined by supply-side factors; nominal variables (prices, money wages) are determined by money supply; changes in money supply do NOT affect real variables -->

### Key Concepts
#### Real vs Nominal Variables
<!-- placeholder: Real = physical quantities (GDP, capital, employment); Nominal = monetary values (price level, wages in rupees) -->

#### Classical Dichotomy Definition
<!-- placeholder: Independence of real economic variables from changes in money supply and nominal variables -->

#### AD-AS Equilibrium in Classical Model
<!-- placeholder: AS vertical at full employment; changes in AD (from money supply) only affect price level -->

#### Neutrality of Money (1.7.2)
<!-- placeholder: Money supply expansion → AD shifts right → price ↑ → nominal wage ↑ proportionally → real wage unchanged → employment unchanged → output unchanged -->

### Definitions
- **Classical Dichotomy**: <!-- placeholder: separation of real and nominal variables; money supply changes affect only nominal side -->
- **Neutrality of Money**: <!-- placeholder: money supply changes have no effect on real output/employment -->
- **Real GDP**: <!-- placeholder: quantity of goods and services produced -->
- **Nominal Variables**: <!-- placeholder: expressed in monetary value (price level, money wages) -->
