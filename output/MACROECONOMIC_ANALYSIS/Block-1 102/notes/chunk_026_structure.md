#### Nominal Exchange Rate
<!-- placeholder: e = units of domestic currency per unit of foreign currency; e↑ = depreciation of domestic currency; e↓ = appreciation -->

<!-- placeholder: r = (e × pf) / pd; measures relative price of foreign goods in terms of domestic goods; r↑ = real depreciation; r↓ = real appreciation -->

<!-- placeholder: Real depreciation → domestic goods cheaper → boosts exports; real appreciation → domestic goods expensive → reduces exports -->

<!-- placeholder: If prices constant, nominal and real move together; but prices can change simultaneously → e and r may move in opposite directions -->
