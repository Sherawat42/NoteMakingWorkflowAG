# Running Context

## Key Concepts Introduced
- **Exchange rate regimes** (Chunk 001): The rulebook governing a currency's relative value.
- **Spectrum of regimes** (Chunk 002): Ranging from fixed pegs to managed floats to free floating.
- **India's regime** (Chunk 002): A managed float with fully convertible current account.
- **BoP vs BoT** (Chunk 003): Payments include visible, invisible and capital; Trade is only visible goods.
- **Autonomous vs Accommodating** (Chunk 003): Autonomous causes disequilibrium, Accommodating fixes it.
- **Crisis Mechanics** (Chunk 004): Triggered by current account deficit, leading to capital flight and currency crash.
- **Policy Measures** (Chunk 004): Expenditure-reducing (dampening demand) and expenditure-switching (devaluation).
- **Impossible Trinity / Trilemma** (Chunk 005): Cannot have fixed config, free capital, and independent monetary policy simultaneously.
- **Trilemma in Practice** (Chunk 006): Real-world examples (China, UK, India) showing how countries navigate the required trade-offs.
- **The US Exception** (Chunk 006): The US avoids the trilemma constraint because the dollar is a global reserve currency.
- **Four Approaches to BoP** (Chunk 007, 008): Elasticity (micro prices), Absorption (macro spending), Keynesian (income), and Monetary (money stock) approaches explain balance adjustments complementarily.

## Definitions (⭐ exam-important)
- **Exchange rate** (Chunk 001): The rate at which a currency exchanges for another. ⭐ 
- **Exchange rate regime** (Chunk 001): The process by which a country manages its currency. ⭐ 
- **Devaluation / Revaluation** (Chunk 001): Deliberate adjustments to a fixed currency's value. ⭐ 
- **Managed Floating Rates** (Chunk 002): Intervention to smooth short-term currency fluctuations. ⭐ 
- **Special Drawing Rights (SDRs)** (Chunk 002): International reserve asset by IMF. ⭐ 
- **Convertibility on current account** (Chunk 002): Ability to freely convert for trade. ⭐
- **Balance of payments (BoP)** (Chunk 003): Complete record of a country's economic transactions with the world. ⭐
- **Balance of trade (BoT)** (Chunk 003): Net receipts purely from merchandise trade. ⭐
- **Autonomous transactions** (Chunk 003): Spontaneous independent trade above-the-line. ⭐
- **Accommodating transactions** (Chunk 003): Government transactions to correct autonomous imbalances. ⭐
- **Capital flight** (Chunk 004): Sudden withdrawal of capital investments out of fear. ⭐
- **Expenditure reduction policy** (Chunk 004): Reduces domestic aggregate demand to lower imports. ⭐
- **Expenditure-switching policy** (Chunk 004): Devaluation to switch demand towards home goods. ⭐
- **Impossible trinity / Mundell-Fleming trilemma** (Chunk 005): Cannot have fixed forex, free capital, and independent monetary policy at the same time. ⭐
- **Mundell-Fleming model** (Chunk 005): Open economy extension of IS-LM model proving the trilemma. ⭐
- **Asian financial crisis** (Chunk 006): Evidence of crisis from violating the impossible trinity. ⭐
- **Tinbergen target-instrument framework** (Chunk 006): A policy instrument can only be effective for a single objective. ⭐
- **Capital account convertibility (CAC)** (Chunk 006): The ability to freely conduct capital transactions across borders. ⭐
- **Capital control** (Chunk 006): Measures to limit the flow of foreign capital. ⭐
- **Elasticities approach** (Chunk 007): Analysis relying on import/export demand responsiveness to prices. ⭐
- **Marshall-Lerner condition** (Chunk 007): Devaluation works only if the sum of export and import demand elasticities is >1. ⭐
- **Absorption** (Chunk 007): Total real expenditure (C + I + G) of domestic economy. ⭐
- **Monetary approach to BoP** (Chunk 007): Examines BoP as a symptom of domestic money market disequilibrium. ⭐
- **The Asset Approach to BoP** (Chunk 008): Monetary approach under flexible rates focused on international investor securities. ⭐
- **Sterilization policy** (Chunk 008): Central bank actions to offset the domestic money supply impact of forex interventions. ⭐

## Named Models / Laws / Theories
- **Mundell-Fleming Model** (Chunk 005): Analyzes short-run relationship between nominal exchange rate and an open economy's output.
