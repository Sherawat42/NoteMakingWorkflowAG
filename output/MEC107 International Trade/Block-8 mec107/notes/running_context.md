# Running Context

## Key Concepts Introduced
- **Inward-Looking Strategy** (Chunk 001): State-led, protectionist economic approach used by India from 1947–1990 to foster self-reliance.
- **Trade Policy Framework** (Chunk 001): The codified laws and diplomatic practices regulating a nation's global commerce.
- **Tariff vs. Non-Tariff Measures** (Chunk 001): Price-based taxes versus quantity/standard-based restrictions on international trade.
- **The License Raj** (Chunk 001): The highly complex bureaucratic system of permissions required for imports and investments in pre-1990 India.
- **Structural Trade Liberalization** (Chunk 001): Post-1991 reforms focused on reducing tariffs, cutting QRs, and opening to foreign investment.
- **Export Promotion Schemes** (Chunk 001): Distinct initiatives like AEZs, ASIDE, and MAI to boost Indian export competitiveness.
- **Developmental vs Maintenance Imports** (Chunk 003): Developmental imports build new capacity (machinery); maintenance imports sustain existing capacity (raw materials).
- **Intra-Industry Trade** (Chunk 004): India imports raw goods (precious stones, crude oil) to process locally and re-export as finished goods.

## Definitions (⭐ exam-important)
- **Trade Policy** (Chunk 001): A national framework of laws, regulations, decisions, practices, and negotiating positions that governments adopt to regulate the exchange of goods and services. ⭐
- **Quantitative Restrictions (QRs)** (Chunk 001): Strict limits on the quantity of a specific good that can be imported or exported, implemented via quotas or licensing policies. ⭐
- **Import Substitution** (Chunk 001): An economic theory and practice aimed at replacing foreign imports with domestic production to foster a self-reliant economy. ⭐
- **Autarky** (Chunk 003): An economic system of complete self-sufficiency and limited or zero international trade. ⭐
- **Duty Credit Scrips** (Chunk 003): Paper authorisations allowing the holder to import inputs without paying duty equivalent to the printed value. ⭐
- **Anti-Dumping Duty** (Chunk 004): A protectionist tariff that a domestic government imposes on foreign imports which it believes are priced artificially low or below fair market value. ⭐
- **SPS and TBT Agreements** (Chunk 004): Key non-tariff barriers under the WTO regulating food safety/animal-plant health (SPS) and labeling/technical standards (TBT). ⭐
- **WTO (World Trade Organization)** (Chunk 004): The primary intergovernmental organization that regulates international trade, replacing GATT in 1995. ⭐

## Named Models / Laws / Theories
- **Agri Export Zones (AEZ)** (Chunk 001): Set up in 2002-07 to provide comprehensive services for exporting specific agricultural products.
- **ASIDE Scheme** (Chunk 001): Assistance to States for Development of Export Infrastructure, introduced in 2002.
- **Market Access Initiative / FPS / FMS** (Chunk 001): Schemes introduced to offset freight costs and promote Indian products in specific markets.
- **WTO Trade Facilitation Agreement (TFA)** (Chunk 001): A 2013 agreement India committed to for reducing procedural complexities in trade.

## Key Data & Numbers
- **Anti-Dumping Duties** (Chunk 001): India is the second-highest user globally.
- **Tariff Reduction** (Chunk 001): Post-1991 simple average tariffs fell to about one-tenth of the 1990-91 level.
- **Trade Demographics** (Chunk 003): India's share in global exports was 0.4% in 1980 and rose marginally to 1.6% in 2020.
- **Export Documentation** (Chunk 004): An average Indian export transaction has historically required about 25 documents.
