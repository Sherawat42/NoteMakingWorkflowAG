# Running Context

## Key Concepts Introduced
- **Globalization and Trade Dynamics** (Chunk 001): Interconnection of economies profoundly affecting alliances.
- **Structure of the Global Economy** (Chunk 001): Dictated by trade policies and multilateral systems.
- **Free Trade and Laissez-faire** (Chunk 002): Adam Smith's policy advocating minimal interference.
- **Gains from Specialization** (Chunk 002): Total global production rises when focusing on absolute advantages.
- **Ricardian Trade Law** (Chunk 002): Nations gain by exporting goods with comparative productivity advantage.
- **Factor Endowments** (Chunk 002): Relative resource supply determines comparative cost differences.
- **Price Criterion of Factor Abundance** (Chunk 003): Defines factor abundance using price ratios.
- **Magnification Effect** (Chunk 003): Determines magnitude of price changes on real factor returns.
- **H-O-S Model** (Chunk 003): Samuelson's extension combining four key trade theorems.
- **Sources of Trade Gains** (Chunk 004): Market expansion, division of labor, economies of scale.
- **Marginal Rate of Transformation (MRT)** (Chunk 004): Amount of commodity sacrificed to gain one unit of another.
- **Reciprocal Demand** (Chunk 004): Strength and elasticity of demand determining terms of trade (J.S. Mill).
- **Modern Measurement of Trade Gains** (Chunk 005): Total Gains = Gains from Exchange + Gains from Specialization.
- **Static vs. Dynamic Gains** (Chunk 005): Immediate structural benefits vs. evolving long-term growth and tech transfer.

## Definitions (⭐ exam-important)
- **Trade** (Chunk 002): Buying and selling goods and services.
- **International Trade** (Chunk 002): Exchange between buyers and sellers of two nations.
- **Capital-Abundant Country** (Chunk 003): A country with relatively cheap capital and dear labor. ⭐
- **Gains from Trade** (Chunk 004): Additional production and consumption effects achieved by trading. ⭐
- **Production Possibilities Curve (PPC)** (Chunk 004): Alternative combinations of two commodities a nation can produce. ⭐
- **Terms of Trade** (Chunk 004): Rate at which one commodity of a country is exchanged for another. ⭐
- **Autarky** (Chunk 005): The absence of trade; economic isolation. ⭐
- **Offer Curve** (Chunk 006): Reciprocal demand curve mapping willingness to exchange exports for imports.

## Named Models / Laws / Theories
- **Theory of Mercantilism** (Chunk 002): Accumulate wealth via exports.
- **Absolute Advantage Theory** (Chunk 002): Specialization based on absolute labor costs (Smith).
- **Comparative Advantage Theory** (Chunk 002): Specialization based on relative productivity (Ricardo).
- **Heckscher-Ohlin Theory** (Chunk 002): Trade driven by factor endowments.
- **Stolper-Samuelson Theorem** (Chunk 003): Return to intensive factor rises with good's price.
- **Factor-Price Equalization Theorem** (Chunk 003): Free trade equalizes global wages/rents.
- **Rybczynski Theorem** (Chunk 003): Increasing factor endowment heavily boosts its intensive industry.
- **Vent for Surplus** (Chunk 005): Adam Smith's concept of exporting domestic excess.
