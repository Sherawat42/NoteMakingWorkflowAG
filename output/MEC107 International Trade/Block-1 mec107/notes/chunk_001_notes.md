## Section: Course Introduction 🟢

### Core Idea
The course "International Trade and Development" introduces the drivers of international trade and finance, exploring how technological advancements, trade policies, and geopolitical shifts dynamically shape the global economy. It covers various perspectives on global trade patterns including free trade, protectionism, trade agreements, and India's evolving role in the global market.

> **In Simple Terms:** This course is a guide to understanding how countries trade with each other, why they use different currencies, and how global events and agreements affect businesses and economies worldwide.

### Key Concepts

#### Globalization and Trade Dynamics
Globalization interconnects economies through cross-border trade, investment, and mobility of resources. Geopolitical dynamics, such as political stability and regional conflicts, profoundly affect these cross-border flows and international business alliances. Understanding these factors provides skills to navigate the complexities of the global economy.

#### Structure of the Global Economy
The global economic environment is shaped by a variety of trade policies (free trade vs. protectionism), instruments (tariffs and non-tariff barriers), exchange rate regimes (fixed, floating, or managed), and multilateral systems like the World Trade Organization (WTO). The course systematically addresses these domains across eight blocks.

> **Quick Recall:**
> - International trade is influenced by technology, geopolitics, and trade policies.
> - The debate between free trade and protectionism drives how countries regulate trade flows.
> - Exchange rates and balance of payments are crucial for macroeconomic stability.
