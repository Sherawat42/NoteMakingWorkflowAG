## Key Terms Glossary
| Term | Definition | First Appears |
|------|-----------|---------------|
| Free Trade | Trade policy where trade barriers among member countries are eliminated. | Chunk 001 |
| Protected Trade | Restrictions on international trade to support domestic industries using tariffs, quotas, subsidies. | Chunk 001 |
| Protectionist Policies | Government controls/measures to restrict international trade. | Chunk 001 |
| Tariffs | Taxes or duties levied on imported goods. | Chunk 001 |
| Import Quotas | Limit on the quantity of a product imported during a specific period. | Chunk 001 |
| Subsidies | Direct or indirect financial assistance from governments to companies for making them competitive. | Chunk 001 |
| Technical Trade Barriers (TBT) | Discriminatory technical regulations, standards, and conformity procedures for health, safety, etc. | Chunk 001 |
| Voluntary Export Restraints (VERs) | Limitation on the maximum volume of exports for the exporting country. | Chunk 001 |
| Infant Industry Argument | Protecting small & young domestic industries from established foreign competition until they mature. | Chunk 002 |
| Dumping | Price discrimination policy across markets; selling lower overseas than at home. | Chunk 002 |
| Balance of Trade (BOT) Disequilibrium | Using tariffs to correct excess imports over exports and create favorable trade balance. | Chunk 002 |
| Specific Tariff | Fixed sum levied on each unit of the commodity imported or exported. | Chunk 002 |
| Effective Rate of Protection (ERP) | Measures change in value added in domestic industry due to tariff protection, considering tariffs on final and intermediate goods. | Chunk 003 |
| Revenue vs. Protective Tariff | Categorization based on whether the intention is income generation or shielding domestic firms. | Chunk 003 |
| Ad Valorem Tariff | Duty levied as a fixed percentage of the value of the traded commodity. | Chunk 003 |
| Compound Tariff | A combination of specific and ad valorem tariffs. | Chunk 003 |
| Export Subsidies | Government policies implemented to encourage the export of goods by incentivizing local producers. | Chunk 003 |
| Import Quota vs Monopoly | Quotas under monopoly lead to greater welfare losses than under perfect competition, preserving the monopolist's price-setting power. | Chunk 004 |
| Rent-seeking behavior | Dissipating quota rents through unproductive activities like bribery, worsening social loss. | Chunk 004 |
| Consumer / Producer Surplus | The gain to consumers/producers from the difference between willing price and actual price. | Chunk 004 |
| Terms of Trade | The ratio of the price received for a country's exports to the price paid for its imports. | Chunk 004 |
