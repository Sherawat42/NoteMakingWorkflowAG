# Running Context

## Key Concepts Introduced
- **Free Trade** (Chunk 001): Trade policy where trade barriers among member countries are eliminated.
- **Protected Trade** (Chunk 001): Restrictions on international trade to support domestic industries using tariffs, quotas, subsidies.
- **Protectionist Policies** (Chunk 001): Government controls/measures to restrict international trade.
- **Infant Industry Argument** (Chunk 002): Protecting small & young domestic industries from established foreign competition until they mature.
- **Dumping** (Chunk 002): Price discrimination policy across markets; selling lower overseas than at home.
- **Balance of Trade (BOT) Disequilibrium** (Chunk 002): Using tariffs to correct excess imports over exports and create favorable trade balance.
- **Effective Rate of Protection (ERP)** (Chunk 003): Measures change in value added in domestic industry due to tariff protection, considering tariffs on final and intermediate goods.
- **Revenue vs. Protective Tariff** (Chunk 003): Categorization based on whether the intention is income generation or shielding domestic firms.
- **Import Quota vs Monopoly/Perfect Competition** (Chunk 004): Quotas under monopoly lead to greater welfare losses than under perfect competition, preserving the monopolist's price-setting power.
- **Rent-seeking behavior** (Chunk 004): Dissipating quota rents through unproductive activities like bribery, worsening social loss.

## Definitions (⭐ exam-important)
- **Tariffs** (Chunk 001): Taxes or duties levied on imported goods. ⭐
- **Import Quotas** (Chunk 001): Limit on the quantity of a product imported during a specific period. ⭐
- **Subsidies** (Chunk 001): Direct or indirect financial assistance from governments to companies for making them competitive. ⭐
- **Technical Trade Barriers (TBT)** (Chunk 001): Discriminatory technical regulations, standards, and conformity procedures for health, safety, etc. ⭐
- **Voluntary Export Restraints (VERs)** (Chunk 001): Limitation on the maximum volume of exports for the exporting country. ⭐
- **Specific Tariff** (Chunk 002): Fixed sum levied on each unit of the commodity imported or exported. ⭐
- **Ad Valorem Tariff** (Chunk 003): Duty levied as a fixed percentage of the value of the traded commodity. ⭐
- **Compound Tariff** (Chunk 003): A combination of specific and ad valorem tariffs. ⭐
- **Export Subsidies** (Chunk 003): Government policies implemented to encourage the export of goods by incentivizing local producers. ⭐
- **Consumer / Producer Surplus** (Chunk 004): The gain to consumers/producers from the difference between willing price and actual price. ⭐
- **Terms of Trade** (Chunk 004): The ratio of the price received for a country's exports to the price paid for its imports. ⭐

## Named Models / Laws / Theories
- **Adam Smith (1776) / David Ricardo (1816) on free trade** (Chunk 001): Doctrine that welfare is enhanced with free trade.
