# Running Context

## Key Concepts Introduced
- **Horizontal IIT** (Chunk 001): Trade of similarly high-quality, but differentiated products due to scale economies.
- **Vertical IIT** (Chunk 001): Trade of differentiated products of different qualities due to factor endowment differences.
- **Love-of-Variety Approach** (Chunk 001): Consumer desire for many different product choices.
- **Ideal Variety Approach** (Chunk 001): Consumer desire for one specific preferred product variant.
- **Increasing Returns to Scale** (Chunk 003): Output scaling disproportionately faster than inputs, reducing per-unit cost.
- **Tariff Jumping** (Chunk 003): Firms producing overseas to avoid tariffs.

## Definitions (⭐ exam-important)
- **Intra-Industry Trade (IIT)** (Chunk 001): Simultaneous export and import of very close substitute products in terms of factor inputs and consumption.
- **Horizontal IIT** (Chunk 001): Simultaneous export and import of differentiated products of a similar quality.
- **Vertical IIT** (Chunk 001): Exchange of differentiated products of different qualities.
- **Love-of-Variety Approach** (Chunk 001): Concept that consumers maximize utility by consuming many different varieties.
- **Product Life Cycle Theory** (Chunk 003): Vernon's theory explaining the dynamic shifting of comparative advantage through innovation, maturation, and standardization of a product.
- **Increasing Returns to Scale** (Chunk 003): A situation where output grows proportionally more than the increase in inputs/factors of production.
- **Economies of Scale** (Chunk 003): The reduction of per-unit production costs as the scale of production rises.
- **Overlapping Demand Hypothesis** (Chunk 003): Trade in manufactures is largest among countries with similar tastes and income levels.

## Named Models / Laws / Theories
- **Krugman's Model of Horizontal IIT** (Chunk 001): Explains horizontal IIT using monopolistic competition, Love-of-Variety, and internal economies of scale.
- **Technological Gap Model** (Chunk 003): Temporary export monopolies arise from technological innovations.
- **Product Life Cycle Theory** (Chunk 003): Comparative advantage shifts from innovators to low-wage countries as products standardize.
- **Overlapping Demand Hypothesis** (Chunk 003): Large domestic demand creates export advantages to similar-income/taste countries.
