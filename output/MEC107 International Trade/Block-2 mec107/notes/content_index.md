## Key Terms Glossary
| Term | Definition | First Appears |
|------|-----------|---------------|
| Intra-Industry Trade (IIT) | Simultaneous export and import of very close substitute products in terms of factor inputs and consumption. | Chunk 001 |
| Horizontal IIT | Simultaneous export and import of differentiated products of a similar quality. | Chunk 001 |
| Vertical IIT | Exchange of differentiated products of different qualities. | Chunk 001 |
| Love-of-Variety Approach | Concept that consumers maximize utility by consuming many different varieties. | Chunk 001 |
| Reciprocal Dumping | Oligopoly firms dump identical products into each other's domestic markets. | Chunk 002 |
| Product Life Cycle Theory | Comparative advantage shifts horizontally over time from innovators to low-wage countries through standardization. | Chunk 003 |
| Increasing Returns to Scale | Output grows proportionally more than the increase in inputs/factors of production. | Chunk 003 |
| Economies of Scale | Reduction of per-unit production costs as the scale of production rises. | Chunk 003 |
| Gravity Model of Trade | Bilateral trade is proportional to size (GDP) and inversely proportional to geographic distance. | Chunk 004 |
| Footloose Industry | Industries relatively unconstrained by transport weights, locating based on input costs like labor. | Chunk 004 |
