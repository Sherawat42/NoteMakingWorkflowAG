# Running Context

## Key Concepts Introduced
- **Multilateralism** (Chunk 001): Understanding between 3+ countries for trade liberalization.
- **GATT Phases** (Chunk 001): Four historical periods tracing the evolution of GATT from 1947 to Uruguay Round.
- **Singapore Issues** (Chunk 001): Four contentious topics (Investment, Govt procurement, Competition, Trade facilitation) introduced in 1996.
- **The Semaphore System** (Chunk 002): Colour-coded classification of agricultural support based on trade distortion.
- **Stumbling Blocs vs Stepping Stones** (Chunk 002): Debate on whether RTAs hinder or help multilateral trade.
- **Types of RTAs** (Chunk 003): Five progressive stages of economic integration (PTA, FTA, CU, Common Market, EMU).
- **Static vs Dynamic gains** (Chunk 003): Immediate reallocation gains (trade creation) vs long term growth gains (economies of scale).
- **Equilibrium Structure of RTAs** (Chunk 004): RTAs expand horizontally (members), vertically (cooperation), or both simultaneously.
- **The Address Game** (Chunk 004): Game theory model showing sequential RTA formation halts before global free trade is reached.
- **India's Trade Shift** (Chunk 005): India's historical transition from a passive GATT bystander to an aggressive developing-bloc leader in the WTO.
- **NAMA & TRIPS stance** (Chunk 006): India's defense of industrial tariffs and successful adaptation of IP laws for public health (generic drugs).

## Definitions (⭐ exam-important)
- **Multilateralism** (Chunk 001): An understanding between multiple countries (three or more) to achieve trade liberalization.
- **Special and Differential Treatment** (Chunk 001): Exception excusing developing-nations from reciprocally cutting tariffs.
- **Swiss formula** (Chunk 001): A mathematical formula cutting high tariffs greater than lower tariffs.
- **GATS** (Chunk 001): General Agreement on Trade in Services for free/fair service trade.
- **TRIPS** (Chunk 001): Agreement on Trade-Related Intellectual Property Rights.
- **Amber/Blue/Green box** (Chunk 002): Classifications of agricultural subsidies based on trade distortion levels.
- **Special Safeguard Mechanism (SSM)** (Chunk 002): Tool allowing developing nations to temporarily hike agricultural tariffs against import surges.
- **Domino effect** (Chunk 002): Phenomenon pushing non-members to optionally join an existing RTA due to rising exclusion costs.
- **Juggernaut effect** (Chunk 002): Phenomenon where reciprocal tariff cuts empower exporter lobbies, driving further liberalization.
- **Regional Trading Agreements (RTAs)** (Chunk 003): Reciprocal agreements between partners determining economic integration extent.
- **Common External Tariff (CET)** (Chunk 003): A single unified tariff rate applied by Customs Union members to non-members.
- **Transshipment** (Chunk 003): Exploiting differing FTA external tariffs to import via the lowest-tariff member.
- **Rules of Origin (RoO)** (Chunk 003): Rules to firmly verify the sourcing country of an FTA product.
- **Trade Creation / Diversion** (Chunk 003): Welfare-enhancing shift to an efficient member vs welfare-decreasing shift to an inefficient member.
- **Horizontal / Vertical Expansion** (Chunk 004): Increase in RTA members vs deepening of economic cooperation among existing members.
- **Open / Unanimous Regionalism** (Chunk 004): Joining without existing member consent vs requiring consent from all members.
- **APTA** (Chunk 005): Asia-Pacific Trade Agreement, generating heavy trade deficits for India due to China.
- **SAFTA** (Chunk 005): South Asian Free Trade Area, replacing SAPTA.
- **MERCOSUR PSA** (Chunk 005): India's trans-continental trade agreement with South America.
- **NAMA** (Chunk 006): Non-Agricultural Market Access; deals strictly with industrial products.

## Named Models / Laws / Theories
- **General Agreement on Tariffs and Trade (GATT)** (Chunk 001): Post-WWII multilateral framework established in 1947.
- **Blair House accord** (Chunk 001): 1992 US-EU agreement settling agricultural differences.
- **Marrakesh Agreement** (Chunk 001): 1994 treaty that officially replaced GATT with the WTO.
- **Geneva Package** (Chunk 002): 2022 agreement focusing on food security, vaccine IP waivers, and fisheries.
- **The Quad (QSD)** (Chunk 005): Strategic security dialogue between Australia, India, Japan, USA securing Indo-Pacific free trade routes.
