## Key Terms Glossary
| Term | Definition | First Appears |
|------|-----------|---------------|
| Multilateralism | An understanding between multiple countries (three or more) to achieve trade liberalization. | Chunk 001 |
| Special and Differential Treatment | Exception excusing developing-nations from reciprocally cutting tariffs. | Chunk 001 |
| Swiss formula | A mathematical formula cutting high tariffs greater than lower tariffs. | Chunk 001 |
| GATS | General Agreement on Trade in Services for free/fair service trade. | Chunk 001 |
| TRIPS | Agreement on Trade-Related Intellectual Property Rights. | Chunk 001 |
| Amber/Blue/Green box | Classifications of agricultural subsidies based on trade distortion levels. | Chunk 002 |
| Special Safeguard Mechanism (SSM) | Tool allowing developing nations to temporarily hike agricultural tariffs against import surges. | Chunk 002 |
| Domino effect | Phenomenon pushing non-members to optionally join an existing RTA due to rising exclusion costs. | Chunk 002 |
| Juggernaut effect | Phenomenon where reciprocal tariff cuts empower exporter lobbies, driving further liberalization. | Chunk 002 |
| Regional Trading Agreements (RTAs) | Reciprocal agreements between partners determining economic integration extent. | Chunk 003 |
| Common External Tariff (CET) | A single unified tariff rate applied by Customs Union members to non-members. | Chunk 003 |
| Transshipment | Exploiting differing FTA external tariffs to import via the lowest-tariff member. | Chunk 003 |
| Rules of Origin (RoO) | Rules to firmly verify the sourcing country of an FTA product. | Chunk 003 |
| Trade Creation / Diversion | Welfare-enhancing shift to an efficient member vs welfare-decreasing shift to an inefficient member. | Chunk 003 |
| Horizontal / Vertical Expansion | Increase in RTA members vs deepening of economic cooperation among existing members. | Chunk 004 |
| Open / Unanimous Regionalism | Joining without existing member consent vs requiring consent from all members. | Chunk 004 |
| APTA | Asia-Pacific Trade Agreement, generating heavy trade deficits for India due to China. | Chunk 005 |
| SAFTA | South Asian Free Trade Area, replacing SAPTA. | Chunk 005 |
| MERCOSUR PSA | India's trans-continental trade agreement with South America. | Chunk 005 |
| NAMA | Non-Agricultural Market Access; deals strictly with industrial products. | Chunk 006 |
