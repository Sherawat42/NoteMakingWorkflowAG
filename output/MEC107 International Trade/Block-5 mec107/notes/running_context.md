# Running Context

## Key Concepts Introduced
- **Intra-Industry Trade (IIT)** (Chunk 001): The exchange of similar or closely related goods and services within the same industry between countries.
- **Globalisation of Financial Markets** (Chunk 002): An ongoing process of increasing interdependence driven by cross-border capital flows.
- **Foreign Exchange Market** (Chunk 002): A 24/5 global market for currency conversion and risk management.
- **Eurocurrency Market** (Chunk 003): A wholesale market for transactions in a currency outside its country of origin.
- **Leading / Lagging Strategies** (Chunk 003): Altering payment and collection timings to mitigate foreign exchange risks.
- **Systemic vs. Non-Systemic Crises** (Chunk 004): A systemic crisis breaks down the entire financial system, whereas a non-systemic applies to isolated sectors.
- **Subprime Lending & RMBS** (Chunk 004): The core drivers of the 2008 Global Financial Crisis through the pooling of high-risk mortgages.
- **Currency Crisis & Capital Flight** (Chunk 005): Sharp decline in currency value driven by investor panic and rapid withdrawal of capital.
- **Maturity Transformation** (Chunk 006): Banks funding long-term loans with short-term deposits, creating inherent vulnerability.

## Definitions (⭐ exam-important)
- **Foreign Exchange Market** (Chunk 002): The organizational setting where businesses, governments, and banks buy/sell currencies. ⭐
- **Foreign Exchange Risk** (Chunk 002): Adverse consequences of unpredictable changes in exchange rates. ⭐
- **Financial Instruments** (Chunk 002): Legal agreements acting as payment, store of value, and risk transfer tools. ⭐
- **Eurocurrency Market** (Chunk 003): Transactions denominated in a currency other than the domestic currency. ⭐
- **LIBOR** (Chunk 003): London Inter-bank Offer Rate; benchmark short-term interest rate. ⭐
- **Transaction Risk** (Chunk 003): Exposure due to currency exchange rate fluctuations between commitment and payable time. ⭐
- **Translation Risk** (Chunk 003): Accounting exposure from converting foreign assets/liabilities values to domestic currency. ⭐
- **Economic Risk** (Chunk 003): Long-run risk of changing macroeconomic conditions on future operations. ⭐
- **Financial Crisis** (Chunk 004): Severe situation where asset prices plummet, widespread defaults occur, and liquidity vanishes. ⭐
- **Systemic Crisis** (Chunk 004): Risk of an entire financial system breakdown caused by cascading failures. ⭐
- **Subprime Lending** (Chunk 004): Loans distributed to borrowers with low creditworthiness. ⭐
- **Credit Derivatives** (Chunk 004): Off-balance-sheet contracts allowing lenders to transfer credit risk to a third party. ⭐
- **Residential Mortgage-Backed Securities (RMBS)** (Chunk 004): A funded credit derivative formed by pooling various residential mortgages. ⭐
- **Currency Crisis** (Chunk 005): Occurs when the value of a country's currency sharply declines. ⭐
- **Syndicated Loans** (Chunk 005): Large loans provided by a group of lenders to a corporate borrower. ⭐
- **Liquidity Trap** (Chunk 005): An economic situation where interest rates are near zero, rendering conventional monetary policy ineffective. ⭐
- **Maturity Transformation** (Chunk 006): Turning short-term deposits into long-term borrowings. ⭐
- **Collateralised Debt Obligation (CDO)** (Chunk 006): Derivative product grouping loans into a single investment package. ⭐
- **Securitisation** (Chunk 006): Pooling assets and repackaging them into interest-bearing securities. ⭐
