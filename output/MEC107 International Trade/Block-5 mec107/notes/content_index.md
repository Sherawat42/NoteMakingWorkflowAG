| Term | Definition | First Appears |

| Financial Instruments | Legal agreements involving monetary value, acting as payment, store of value, and risk transfer tools. | Chunk 002 |

| LIBOR | London Inter-bank Offer Rate; a benchmark for short-term and floating-rate loans worldwide. | Chunk 003 |
| Systemic Crisis | The risk of cascading failure that breaks down the entire financial system. | Chunk 004 |
| Subprime Lending | Making loans to people who may have difficulty maintaining the repayment schedule. | Chunk 004 |
| Credit Derivatives (CDs) | Off-balance-sheet financial contracts that allow a lender to transfer credit risk to a third party. | Chunk 004 |
| Currency Crisis | A sharp decline in the value of a country's currency due to volatile markets and lack of faith in the economy. | Chunk 005 |
| Liquidity Trap | An economic situation where interest rates are near zero, rendering monetary policy ineffective. | Chunk 005 |
| Maturity Transformation | The process whereby banks take short-term deposits and turn them into long-term borrowings. | Chunk 006 |
