## Section: Course Overview 🟢

### Core Idea
The course "International Trade and Development" covers the drivers of international trade, finance, and globalization. It explores trade theories, protectionism, exchange rates, and the role of international organizations like the WTO.

> **In Simple Terms:** This course is a comprehensive guide to understanding how countries trade with each other, how money flows across borders, and the rules that govern this global economic system.

### Key Concepts

#### Scope of International Trade and Finance
The macroeconomy is influenced by cross-border trade, capital flows, geopolitical dynamics, and trade agreements. Understanding these is essential for navigating the complexities of the global economy and international business.

### Definitions
- **Intra-Industry Trade (IIT)**: The exchange of similar or closely related goods and services within the same industry between countries or regions. 

### Mechanisms / Processes
1. Technological advancements → Increased cross-border trade & investment → Integration of national economies
