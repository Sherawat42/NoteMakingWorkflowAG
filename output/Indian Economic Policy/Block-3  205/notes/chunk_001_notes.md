## Section: Course Structure and Block 3 Overview 🟢

### Core Idea
This block introduces the fundamental macroeconomic policies that guide economic development: Monetary Policy and Fiscal Policy. It outlines the specific topics covered in subsequent units, establishing the foundational structure for studying inflation, capital markets, and fiscal management.

> **In Simple Terms:** This section acts as a roadmap for the course, telling you exactly what overarching economic tools the government uses to steer the economy and what each upcoming unit will discuss.

### Key Concepts

#### Monetary and Fiscal Policies as Macroeconomic Instruments
Economic policies are statements of aims and ideals to guide economic development. They are broadly classified into macroeconomic (aggregate) and microeconomic (sectoral) policies. Monetary and fiscal policies are the two primary instruments of macroeconomic policy.

| Policy Type | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| **Macroeconomic** | Aggregate policies dealing with the economy as a whole (e.g., Monetary, Fiscal). |
| **Microeconomic** | Sectoral policies dealing with specific segments of the economy. |

#### Block 3 Units Overview
The block is divided into four main units focusing on various aspects of macroeconomic policy:
1. **Unit 9:** Inflation and Monetary Policy (framework, factors, mechanisms).
2. **Unit 10:** Capital Market and its Regulations (structure, roles, performance of equity, currency, and debt markets).
3. **Unit 11:** Fiscal Policy and FRBM Act (government spending, taxation, IS-LM framework, Goods and Services Tax).
4. **Unit 12:** Major Development on Union State Relations (fiscal federalism, Finance Commission recommendations).

### Definitions
- **Economic Policies**: Statements of aims and ideals to be achieved through various policy instruments outlined by the government to guide the process of economic development.

> **Quick Recall:**

> - The course covers both macroeconomic foundations (Block 3) and sectoral issues (Block 4).
