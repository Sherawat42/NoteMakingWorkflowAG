## Key Concepts Introduced
- **Functions of Money** (Chunk 002): Store of value, unit of account, medium of exchange.
- **Inflation Measurement** (Chunk 002): Uses CPI for retail and WPI for wholesale.
- **Quantity Theory of Money** (Chunk 002): Money supply directly affects inflation.
- **Flexible Inflation Targeting** (Chunk 003): RBI targets 4% (+/- 2%) inflation rate.
- **Capital Market Structure** (Chunk 003): Divided into primary (new issues) and secondary (existing shares) markets.
- **Stock Market Performance Indicators** (Chunk 004): Market Capitalisation Ratio, Turnover Ratio, Value Traded Ratio, Volatility.
- **Types of Derivative Traders** (Chunk 004): Hedgers, Speculators, Arbitrageurs.
- **Corporate Debt Market Deficiencies** (Chunk 005): Highly illiquid, mostly private placements.
- **Components of Aggregate Demand** (Chunk 005): C (Consumption) + I (Investment) + G (Gov Spending) + NX (Net Exports).
- **IS-LM Fiscal Implications** (Chunk 006): Gov spending shifts IS right (expansionary), taxes shift IS left (contractionary).
- **Evolution of Indian Tax Policy** (Chunk 006 & 007): Moved from high rates to rationalized brackets. Introduced MAT and Service Tax in 1990s.
- **FRBM Act Genesis** (Chunk 007): Enacted in 2003 to enforce fiscal discipline; targets were derailed by the 2008 Global Financial Crisis.
- **GST Framework** (Chunk 008): Dual GST model (CGST, SGST, IGST); consumption/destination-based tax; GST Council makes decisions.
- **GST Framework** (Chunk 008): Dual GST model (CGST, SGST, IGST); consumption/destination-based tax; GST Council makes decisions.
- **Fiscal Federalism Imbalances** (Chunk 009): Vertical (Centre vs State capacity) and Horizontal (Rich state vs Poor state capacity).
- **Federal Institutions** (Chunk 009): Finance Commission (tax devolution), GST Council (indirect tax decisions), NITI Aayog (advisory).
- **14th & 15th Finance Commissions** (Chunk 010): 14th FC increased devolution to 42% and introduced forest cover weight; 15th FC adjusted to 41%.
- **Quasi-Federalism Trends** (Chunk 010): Increasing use of unshared Cesses by Centre and matching-grant requirements of CSS distort state finances.
- **Terms of Reference (ToR)** (Chunk 011): The specific directives given to Finance Commissions, setting the parameters for their recommendations.

## Definitions (⭐ exam-important)
- **Economic Policies** (Chunk 001): Statements of aims and ideals to be achieved through various policy instruments outlined by the government to guide the process of economic development.
- **Money** (Chunk 002): Anything generally acceptable as a medium of payment.
- **Fiat Money** (Chunk 002): Money with no intrinsic value, derived from government order.
- **Inflation** (Chunk 002): The rate of increase in the general price level.
- **Core Inflation** (Chunk 002): Long-term inflation excluding volatile components.
- **SLR & CRR** (Chunk 002): Ratios determining the reserves banks must maintain.
- **Repo / Reverse Repo Rate** (Chunk 002): Rates at which RBI lends to / borrows from banks.
- **Nominal Anchor** (Chunk 003): A variable used by policymakers to tie down the price level.
- **Monetary Policy Committee (MPC)** (Chunk 003): 6-member committee that determines the policy rate.
- **Capital Market** (Chunk 003): Market for financial assets with long or indefinite maturity.
- **Liquidity** (Chunk 003): How easily an asset can be converted to currency without loss of value.
- **Market Capitalisation Ratio (MCR)** (Chunk 003): Value of listed shares divided by GDP.
- **Turnover Ratio (TOR)** (Chunk 004): Value of total shares traded divided by market capitalisation.
- **Value Traded Ratio (VTR)** (Chunk 004): Total value of traded shares divided by GDP.
- **Derivative Security** (Chunk 004): A financial contract whose value is derived from an underlying asset.
- **Delivery Versus Payment (DVP) III** (Chunk 005): A settlement mechanism where funds and securities are settled on a net basis.
- **Fiscal Policy** (Chunk 005): Government policy regarding taxation and public spending to influence the economy.
- **Marginal Propensity to Consume (MPC)** (Chunk 005): The increase in consumer expenditure per unit increase in disposable income.
- **Crowding-Out Effect** (Chunk 006): The decline in private investment as a result of increasing government spending and borrowing.
- **Plan vs Non-Plan Expenditure** (Chunk 006): Plan is for long-term development; non-plan is for routine administration.
- **Minimum Alternative Tax (MAT)** (Chunk 007): Tax introduced to ensure "zero-tax companies" using exemptions pay a minimum percentage of book profits.
- **FRBM Act (2003)** (Chunk 007): Legislation mandating progressive reduction in fiscal and revenue deficits.
- **Goods and Services Tax (GST)** (Chunk 008): A comprehensive, destination-based tax levied on value addition.
- **Cascading Effect** (Chunk 008): Tax on tax, which the GST aimed to eliminate.
- **Fiscal Federalism** (Chunk 009): The financial relations between units of governments in a federal system.
- **Vertical Imbalance** (Chunk 009): The mismatch between revenue-raising capacity and expenditure responsibilities at different levels of government.
- **Horizontal Imbalance** (Chunk 009): The disparity in the fiscal capacity of government units at the same level.
- **Tax Devolution** (Chunk 009): The statutory sharing of central tax revenues with the states, as recommended by the Finance Commission.
- **Centrally Sponsored Schemes (CSS)** (Chunk 010): Schemes funded partially by the central government but implemented by states, requiring matching state funds.
- **Cess and Surcharge** (Chunk 010): Additional taxes levied by the central government that are not shared with the states.
- **Quasi-Federalism** (Chunk 010): A federal system with a strong unitary bias (Centre is stronger).
