## Section: Globalisation 🔴
<!-- This section starts here -->
<!-- Exam importance: 🔴 HIGH YIELD -->
<!-- Reason: Crucial pillar of the LPG framework -->

### Core Idea
Globalisation involves integrating the domestic economy with the world economy through the removal of barriers to international trade and capital movements. 

### In Simple Terms
Taking down the walls around India's economic garden so that foreign seeds, water, and gardeners can come in, and Indian flowers can be sold internationally.

### Key Concepts
#### Outsourcing
A key outcome of globalization for India. Companies internationally began hiring Indian firms to perform IT and BPO (Business Process Outsourcing) services due to cheap, skilled labor.

#### WTO Membership
India became a founding member of the World Trade Organization (WTO) in 1995, committing to a rule-based multilateral trading system.

### Definitions
- **Globalisation**: The process of interaction and integration among people, companies, and governments worldwide.
- **Outsourcing**: Getting a business task done from an outside agency, usually internationally.

### Quick Recall
- Globalisation = integration with the world economy.
- IT/BPO boom in India was a direct result.

### Core Idea
Since 1991, India's reform process has been gradual and is categorized into distinct "generations," shifting from basic industrial deregulation to complex institutional overhauls.

### Key Concepts
#### First Generation Reforms (1991-2000)
These were the "stroke of the pen" reforms. They were initiated by the central government and were relatively easy to pass. 
- Included: De-licensing, slashing tariffs, devaluing the rupee, and opening to FDI.

#### Second Generation Reforms (2000s onward)
These are much harder structural/institutional reforms requiring legislative changes and state-level cooperation.
- Included: Labor law flexibility, agricultural reforms, power sector reforms, and disinvestment. 

### Definitions
- **First Generation Reforms**: Early, rapid macro-level liberalisations.
- **Second Generation Reforms**: Complex, institution-building and factor-market reforms.

### Quick Recall
- 1st Gen: Easy, "stroke of the pen", Product market focus.
- 2nd Gen: Hard, legislative, Factor market focus (land, labor).
