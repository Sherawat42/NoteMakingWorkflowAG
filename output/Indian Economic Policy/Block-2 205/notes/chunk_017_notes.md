## Section: Privatisation and Restructuring of Public Sector 🟡
<!-- This section starts here and continues into chunk 018 -->
<!-- Exam importance: 🟡 MODERATE -->
<!-- Reason: Sets up the core theme of restructuring -->

### Core Idea
Prior to 1991, Public Sector Enterprises (PSEs) dominated the Indian landscape but suffered from gross inefficiencies. Privatisation was introduced to restructure this sector, improve efficiency, and reduce the financial burden on the state.

### In Simple Terms
The government realized it was very bad at running hotels, making bread, and managing airlines. Restructuring meant either selling these off to private experts or making them run like private companies.

### Key Concepts
#### Deregulation Focus
The 1991 policy restricted the public sector to essential infrastructure and strategic areas (like defense and atomic energy), opening all other areas to private capital.

### Quick Recall
- Post-1991, the public sector size was drastically reduced.
- The goal was efficiency and reducing the fiscal burden.
