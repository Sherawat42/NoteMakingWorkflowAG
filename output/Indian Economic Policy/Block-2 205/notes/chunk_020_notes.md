### Core Idea
Successful privatisation requires the proceeds to be used for retiring public debt or building social/physical infrastructure (as recommended by the Eleventh Finance Commission), rather than just funding current government consumption.

### Key Concepts
#### Eleventh Finance Commission Report
Advised that disinvestment receipts should form a separate fund (National Investment Fund) to be used for social sectors like education/health, not just for balancing the budget.

### Quick Recall
- Disinvestment money should be invested, not consumed.

## Section: Public Private Partnership (PPP) 🔴
<!-- This section starts here and continues into chunk 021 -->
<!-- Exam importance: 🔴 HIGH YIELD -->
<!-- Reason: Extremely important model for Indian infrastructure development -->

### Core Idea
PPP is a funding and operational model where the government and a private sector company collaborate to build public infrastructure (like highways or airports). The private sector brings efficiency and capital, while the government retains eventual ownership or provides land/clearances.

### In Simple Terms
The government wants a new highway but has no money or expertise. A private company says, "I'll build it with my money, but let me collect tolls for 20 years to make my profit. After 20 years, the highway belongs to you."

### Key Concepts
#### Objectives of PPP in India
To bridge the massive infrastructure deficit. Over 1 trillion USD was needed for infrastructure in recent 5-year plans, which the government could not fund alone.

#### PPP Appraisal
The government has established a PPP Cell under the Department of Economic Affairs (DEA), Ministry of Finance, to appraise and approve PPP projects.

### Definitions
- **Public Private Partnership (PPP)**: A long-term contract between a private party and a government entity, for providing a public asset or service, in which the private party bears significant risk and management responsibility.
- **Viability Gap Fund (VGF)**: A scheme by the Gol to provide financial support (usually up to 20% of project cost) as a grant to make commercially unviable PPP infrastructure projects viable (e.g., a rural road that won't collect enough toll).

### Quick Recall
- PPP combines private efficiency/capital with public goals.
- VGF = Free money from govt to make a project profitable for the private player.
