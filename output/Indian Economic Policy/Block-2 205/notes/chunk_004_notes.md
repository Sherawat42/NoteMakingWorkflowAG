<!-- Exam importance: 🔴 HIGH YIELD -->

### Core Idea
State interventions can be broadly classed into direct controls (like licensing and rationing) and indirect controls (like fiscal and monetary policies) to steer the economy.

### Key Concepts
#### Direct vs. Indirect Interventions
Direct interventions explicitly mandate behavior (e.g., License Raj), whereas indirect interventions use market incentives (like altering interest rates or taxes) to influence behavior.

### Quick Recall
- Direct limit choice explicitly.
- Indirect shift the cost/benefit incentives of choices.

## Section: Functions of State Intervention 🟡
<!-- This section starts here -->
<!-- Exam importance: 🟡 MODERATE -->
<!-- Reason: Categorizes *why* governments act into functional groups -->

### Core Idea
The State acts to fulfill three primary economic functions: Protection (maintaining order and property rights), Regulation (correcting market abuses), and Promotion (fostering development and welfare).

### In Simple Terms
The government must play bodyguard (Protection), referee (Regulation), and cheerleader/coach (Promotion) for the economy.

### Key Concepts
#### Protection
Ensuring internal and external security, and maintaining a legal framework (contracts, property rights).

#### Regulation
Overseeing market mechanics to prevent monopolies, ensure fair trade, and protect consumers/laborers.

#### Promotion
Actively stimulating economic growth and equitable distribution through planning, infrastructure building, and social policies.

### Definitions
- **Promotional Function**: State actions taken to actively stimulate economic and social development.

### Quick Recall
- The 3 Functions: Protection, Regulation, Promotion.

## Section: State Intervention in Market for Efficiency 🔴

### Core Idea
The primary economic rationale for State intervention is "Market Failure"—situations where the free market, left to its own devices, fails to allocate resources efficiently.

### In Simple Terms
Even the best sports games need referees when players find loopholes that ruin the game for everyone else. When the market ruins efficiency, the government steps in.

### Key Concepts
#### Concept of Market Failure
Market failure occurs when the price mechanism fails to account for all costs and benefits, leading to overproduction of bad things or underproduction of good things.

#### Causes of Market Failure (Preview)
Primarily driven by Public Goods, Externalities, Monopoly Power, and Information Asymmetries (elaborated in the next chunk).

### Definitions
- **Market Failure**: An economic situation defined by an inefficient distribution of goods and services in the free market.
- **Resource Allocation**: The assignment of available resources to various uses.

### Quick Recall
- State intervenes for Efficiency to correct Market Failures.
- Main failures: Public Goods, Externalities, Monopolies, Info Asymmetry.
