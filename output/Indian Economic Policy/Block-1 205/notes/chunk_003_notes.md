## Section: Course Introduction 🟢

### Core Idea

MEC-205 is designed to provide a comprehensive understanding of India's economic policy landscape. The course covers India's economic history from pre-independence through contemporary challenges, examining the policy frameworks that have shaped the economy.

> **In Simple Terms:** This section explains why this course exists — to help you understand how India's economy has evolved and what policies have shaped it over the decades.

### Key Concepts

#### Block 1 Overview
Block 1 establishes the foundation by examining India's economic situation before independence, how the economy has grown and structurally changed since 1947, the demographic challenges and opportunities, the status of natural resources, and the infrastructure supporting economic growth. The block emphasizes that understanding the **colonial economic legacy** is essential for appreciating post-independence policy choices.

#### Learning Approach
Each unit contains **Check Your Progress (CYP)** questions for self-assessment and **Term-End Exercises** for comprehensive review. Students are expected to engage with the material critically, not just memorize facts.
