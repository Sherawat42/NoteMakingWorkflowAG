## Key Themes Across Units
1. **Colonial Legacy → Post-Independence Growth** (Units 1-2)
2. **Structural Transformation**: Primary → Tertiary (skipping secondary) (Unit 2)
3. **Demographic Dividend**: Window closing ~2040 (Unit 3)
4. **Natural Resource Management**: Land, water, forests, minerals (Unit 4)
5. **Infrastructure as Growth Enabler**: Physical + Social (Unit 5)
