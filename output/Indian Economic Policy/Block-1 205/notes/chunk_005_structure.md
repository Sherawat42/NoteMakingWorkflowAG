### Core Idea

### In Simple Terms
<!-- placeholder: The British created three different tax systems across India, all designed to squeeze maximum money from farmers -->

### Key Concepts
#### Three Land Tenure Systems
<!-- placeholder: Zamindari (eastern), Ryotwari (southern/western), Mahalwari (western Gangetic) -->
#### Over-Assessment of Revenue
<!-- placeholder: Revenue demands exceeded paying capacity → dependence on moneylenders -->

### Definitions
- **Zamindari System (Permanent Settlement)**: <!-- placeholder: Property rights to zamindars, revenue fixed in perpetuity -->
- **Ryotwari System**: <!-- placeholder: Property rights to peasants, revenue revised every 30 years -->
- **Mahalwari System**: <!-- placeholder: Property rights to dominant kinship lineages, revenue revised every 30 years -->
- **Diwani**: <!-- placeholder: Taxation rights granted to East India Company for Bengal, Bihar, Odisha in 1765 -->

### Mechanisms / Processes
<!-- placeholder: Grant of Diwani (1765) → maximum revenue extraction → devastation of agrarian economy → famine of 1769-70 killed 1/3 of Bengal population -->
