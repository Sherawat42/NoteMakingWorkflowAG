## Section: Banking (NPAs and Resolutions) (5.3.4 continued) 🔴

### Key Concepts

#### Tackling Stressed Assets (NPAs) ⭐ (exam-important)
The most severe issue in Indian banking (especially Public Sector Banks) is mounting bad loans. Steps taken:
1. **Recapitalisation (Indradhanush Plan, 2015):** The government infused Rs. 70,000 crore into PSBs to meet capital norms.
2. **Merger of PSBs:** Consolidating weak banks into larger, competitive entities capable of absorbing shocks without depending completely on the state exchequer.
3. **Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC, 2016):** A revolutionary market-mechanism providing time-bound insolvency resolution for bankrupt companies, maximizing value recovery for banks.
4. **P.J. Nayak Committee (2014):** Focused heavily on enhancing the governance and management of PSBs.

## Section: Housing and Urban Infrastructure (5.3.5) 🔴

### Core Idea

Unlike the West, India's urbanization is not gradually syncing with industrial growth. Due to massive rural-to-urban migration, Indian cities are exploding under pressure. Urban India currently generates ~60% of GDP but suffers from severe pollution, waste crises, and housing shortages.

### Key Concepts

#### Urban Policy Initiatives ⭐ (exam-important)
1. **AMRUT & Smart Cities:** Upgrading water/sewerage networks and transforming 100 cities into digitally networked hubs.
2. **RERA (2016):** The Real Estate Regulation Act cleans up the historically corrupt real estate sector, making it transparent to protect home buyers.
3. **PMAY-U:** "Housing for All" scheme aiming to provide *pucca* houses to the urban poor by 2022.
4. **INDC Commitments:** Pledges to the UN to ensure sustainable green transport and clean energy in cities.

## Section: Social Infrastructure: Education (5.4.1) 🔴

### Core Idea

India will soon have the largest working-age population in the world. Merely providing literacy is not enough; providing job-ready skills is structurally imperative. While Higher Education enrolment has grown 4x, the system suffers from low employability and weak governance.

### Key Concepts

#### Higher Education Snapshot
- Target: Reach a **50% Gross Enrolment Ratio (GER)** by 2035 (currently ~26%).
- FDI in Education is allowed, attracting ~$2.47 billion.

#### Digital and Policy Interventions
- **SWAYAM:** Massive open online courses for Access, Equity, and Quality.
- **NDLI:** National Digital Library.
- **PMRF:** Fellowships for top undergraduates to do PhDs in IITs/IISc.

> **Quick Recall:**
> - The **Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC)** was introduced to handle the massive BAD LOAN (NPA) crisis in banks.
> - Urban areas generate **60%** of India's GDP.
> - **RERA** was established to regulate the real estate market and protect home buyers.
> - Main target for Higher Education: **50% GER by 2035**.
