## Section: Unit 4 Summary (Let Us Sum Up - 4.10) 🟡

### Core Idea

India has a vast potential of natural resources, but current utilization falls dangerously short of long-term sustainability requirements. 

#### Required Actions to Prevent Disaster
1. **Intensive Exploration:** Survey both renewable and non-renewable resources thoroughly.
2. **Efficient Use:** Adopt better technology, utilize by-products, and locate industries smartly to minimize transport costs.
3. **Revive Conservation Institutions:** Revive indigenous, community-led practices that historically ensured sustainable use against greedy, short-term discounting.
4. **Halt the Resource Mafia:** As globalization increases demand, mafias colluding with politicians/bureaucrats are grabbing land, water, and mines. Privatization alone isn't the answer; civil society oversight is mandatory to prevent the "loot" of the public exchequer.

## Section: Key Words (4.12) 🔴

### Crucial Definitions ⭐ (exam-important)

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Biodiversity** | The variety of all forms of life (flora, fauna, microorganisms) found on Earth. |
| **Emerging Economies** | Nations transitioning from a low-income, pre-industrial state towards a modern, industrial economy with higher living standards (e.g., India). |
| **Governance** | Structures and processes designed to ensure accountability, transparency, responsiveness, rule of law, equity, empowerment, and broad-based participation. |
| **Labour-intensive** | A production process where the relative proportion of human labor used is much higher than other factors of production (like machinery). |
| **Marginal Land** | Land with little or no agricultural or industrial value, often having poor soil, yielding little potential for profit. |
| **Unsustainable** | A practice or rate of exploitation that cannot be maintained over time without exhausting the resource. |

> **Quick Recall:**
> - The biggest threat to modern resource allocation is the nexus between mafias, politicians, and bureaucrats.
> - "Marginal land" refers to poor quality land that cannot yield profitable crops. 
> - Efficient resource use requires maximizing by-products and community-based conservation.
