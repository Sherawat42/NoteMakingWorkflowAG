## Section: Let Us Sum Up (2.6) 🟡

### Core Idea

Unit 2 traced India's growth from 3.5% (Hindu Rate of Growth) to 7.5%+ through five phases. The structural transformation from primary to tertiary (skipping secondary) is unique. Key reforms since 2014 include GST, IBC, demonetisation, JAM trinity, and infrastructure push. However, six challenges remain — notably employment generation and demographic dividend utilisation.

## Section: Key Words (2.7) 🟢

### Definitions

- **Hindu Rate of Growth**: GDP growth of ~3.5% during 1950-80; coined by Prof. Raj Krishna. ⭐ (exam-important)
- **GFCF (Gross Fixed Capital Formation)**: Investment in physical assets (machinery, buildings) less disposals; key measure of productive investment. ⭐ (exam-important)
- **GDP at Factor Cost**: Total value of goods/services at their production costs (not market prices).
- **Kuznet's Law**: As economies develop, structural shift from primary → secondary → tertiary sectors. ⭐ (exam-important)
- **Demographic Dividend**: Economic growth potential from favourable age structure (large working-age population). ⭐ (exam-important)
- **Digital Economy**: Economy based on digital computing/internet technologies.
- **GST**: Unified indirect tax replacing multiple taxes, creating single national market. ⭐ (exam-important)
- **IBC**: Time-bound insolvency resolution law (2016). ⭐ (exam-important)
- **JAM Trinity**: Jan Dhan + Aadhaar + Mobile — platform for direct benefit transfers. ⭐ (exam-important)

> **Quick Recall:**
> - Unit 2 covers: 5 growth phases + structural change + tertiary dominance + reforms + challenges
> - Key definitions to memorize: Hindu Rate, GFCF, Kuznet's Law, GST, IBC, JAM
> - Unique Indian pattern: Primary → Tertiary (skipped secondary)
> - Growth trajectory: 3.5% → 5.5% → 6-8% → slowdown → 7.5% → "pause"
