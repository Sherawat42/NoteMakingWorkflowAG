## Section: Prerequisites for Private Investment (continued - 5.2.2) 🟡

### Key Concepts

#### Additional Prerequisites for Commercialisation
1. **Demand Orientation:** Public infra used to be "supply-oriented" (build it and hope they come). Privatisation requires "demand-orientation" (finding market signals indicating where infrastructure is actually needed) so investments yield returns.
2. **Allocation of Risk:** Risk must be appropriately demarcated among stakeholders to prevent private companies from shifting the risk burden entirely onto the government.
3. **Government Participation:** Because infrastructure is a "natural monopoly," pure privatization causes public harm. Direct government participation and regulation are still needed.
4. **Regulatory Framework:** A healthy, guided competition regulated by a strong agency is mandatory.

## Section: Physical Infrastructure: Growth and Policy Issues (5.3) 🔴

### Core Idea

Transport is the indispensable backbone of regional economic development. Even rich natural resources are useless without roads and railways to move them.

### Key Concepts: Transport Sector – Roads (5.3.1.1) ⭐ (exam-important)

#### Indian Road Network Stats
- **Size:** ~59 lakh km (second largest globally).
- **Dominance:** Roads transport over **60% of total goods** and **85% of total passenger traffic**.
- **National Highways (NH):** Massive investment jump; length increased from 92,851 km (FY14) to 132,499 km (FY19).

#### Core Issues Facing the Road Sector ⚠️
1. High cost of financing and shortfall in maintenance funds.
2. Unavailability of land and severe increases in land acquisition costs.
3. **NPA Crisis:** Non-Performing Assets in infrastructure (including roads) rose dangerously to Rs. 2.6 lakh crore by 2016.
4. **Project Delays:** Only a tiny fraction of projects finish on time. Delays are caused by land acquisition hurdles, environmental clearances, and cash flow issues. These delays drastically inflate project costs, ruining viability.

#### Government Initiatives and Financing Challenges
- MoRTH lacks its own revenue source outside of budget support.
- **National Infrastructure Pipeline (NIP):** The roads sector is allotted 18% of the massive capital expenditure (2019-25).
- **PMGSY Phase III:** Aims to consolidate 1,25,000 km of rural roads connecting to markets (GrAMs) and hospitals.
- **Bharatmala Pariyojana:** A mega-project to construct 65,000 km of highways.
- **Revival Strategies:** One-time fund infusions, 100% FDI allowance, and a revamped dispute resolution mechanism.

> **Quick Recall:**
> - Without effective "Risk Allocation," private companies will just dump project risks on the government.
> - Roads carry **60% of goods and 85% of passengers** in India.
> - Major reasons for road delays: Land acquisition, environmental clearance, cash flow.
> - Bharatmala Pariyojana: Mega-project for 65,000 km of national highways.
