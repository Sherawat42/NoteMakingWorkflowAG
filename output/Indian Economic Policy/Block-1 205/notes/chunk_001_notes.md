## Section: Block Introduction 🟢

### Core Idea

Block 1 of MEC-205 (Indian Economic Policy) covers the foundations of India's economic development. It consists of **five units** spanning the colonial economy, post-independence growth trajectory, demographic transition, natural resources, and physical/social infrastructure.

> **In Simple Terms:** This is the introductory section of the textbook — it tells you what the book will cover across its five units, like a roadmap for the course.

### Key Concepts

#### Course Structure — MEC-205
The course MEC-205 "Indian Economic Policy" is divided into multiple blocks. **Block 1** titled "Indian Economic Development: An Overview" lays the foundation by covering:
- **Unit 1**: Indian Economy on the Eve of Independence
- **Unit 2**: Growth and Structure of the Indian Economy
- **Unit 3**: Demographic Transition and Its Implications
- **Unit 4**: Natural Resources
- **Unit 5**: Physical and Social Infrastructure

> **Quick Recall:**
> - Course code: MEC-205
> - Block 1 has 5 units
> - Covers: Colonial economy → Growth → Demographics → Resources → Infrastructure
