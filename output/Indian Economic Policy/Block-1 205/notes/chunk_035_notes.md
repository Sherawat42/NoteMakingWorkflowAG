## Section: Environment and Economic Development (4.9 & 4.9.1) 🔴

### Core Idea

The environment performs four functions for the economy: life-support, supply of resources, waste absorption, and amenity services. India's obsession with rapid mega-project growth is severely straining these functions. India is currently using 50% more ecological resources each year than nature can replenish.

> **In Simple Terms:** We are treating the environment like an unlimited bank account. But economic growth means more cars, more paper cups, and more mining in forests. According to the World Wildlife Fund, India is literally bouncing its environmental checks every year.

### Key Concepts

#### Global Footprints
- Mankind's global footprint is ~130% of global biocapacity. 
- India's per-capita footprint is 0.8 global hectares (compared to China's 1.6 and USA's 9.7). But due to population size, the aggregate impact is massive.

#### Cost of Environmental Degradation ⭐ (exam-important)
- Degradation and diseases cause **23% of child mortality**.
- Economic cost of environmental damage = **5.7% of India's GDP**.
- Reversing this through green growth strategies would cost merely 0.02% to 0.04% of the GDP growth rate. The current mode of development is wholly unsustainable.

#### Environmental Sustainability Index (ESI)
- India ranks a dismal 101st out of 146 countries (2005 data), and 125th in the 2012 EPI.
- **Best States:** Manipur, J&K, Tripura (sustaining natural stops).
- **Worst States:** Gujarat, Punjab, Haryana (severe depletion of air, water, and land quality).

#### The 5-Element Solution for Eco-Growth
To reconcile growth and environment, Mega-projects require: (1) Full data provision, (2) Early stakeholder engagement/public hearing, (3) Negotiated solutions, (4) Generous compensation, and (5) Independent watchdogs.

## Section: National Environment Policy, 2006 (NEP) (4.9.2) 🔴

### Core Idea

The NEP 2006 builds on all prior resource policies (Forest, Agriculture, Water) to establish the principle that environmental protection cannot be isolated from economic development. 

### Key Concepts

#### Major Features of NEP 2006 ⭐ (exam-important)
1. **Livelihood Security:** Securing the livelihood of poor people dependent on forest/natural products through conservation.
2. **Integration:** Environmental protection must be an integral part of the development process.
3. **Appraisal Tool:** **Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA)** remains the principal methodology for approving new projects.
4. **Decentralization:** Significant devolution of assessment powers to State/Union Territory levels.
5. **Coastal Regulation:** Revisiting Coastal Regulation Zones (CRZ) for a holistic approach against rising sea levels and extreme events.
6. **Local Governance:** Heavy involvement of Panchayati Raj Institutions and Urban Local Bodies.

> **Quick Recall:**
> - Economic cost of environmental damage = **5.7% of GDP**.
> - India's global ecological footprint = **0.8 hectares per person**.
> - Best ESI States: Manipur, J&K. Worst: Punjab, Gujarat.
> - NEP 2006 Core Principle: Protect the environment by securing the livelihoods of the poor who depend on it.
> - Mandatory tool for new projects: Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA).
