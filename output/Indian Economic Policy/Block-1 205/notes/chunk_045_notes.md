## Section: National Education Policy 2020 (5.4.1 continued) 🔴

### Key Concepts

#### NEP 2020 Highlights ⭐ (exam-important)
Approved in July 2020 to bring transformational reform:
1. **Financial Target:** Centre and States to increase public investment in education to **6% of GDP**.
2. **Structure:** Early Childhood Care/Education with new Curricular/Pedagogical structures.
3. **Language:** Heavy emphasis on multilingualism and Indian languages.
4. **Holistic Education:** Multidisciplinary higher education with multiple entry/exit options.
5. **Target:** 50% GER in higher education by 2035.

## Section: Health (5.4.2) 🔴

### Core Idea

India's primary health system is chronically underfunded and heavily understaffed, meaning the private sector dominates (doing 70% of bed expansion). Consequentially, citizens face devastatingly high "out of pocket" expenses.

### Key Concepts

#### Health System Structure and Shortfalls
- **Three Tiers:** Sub-Centres (SCs), Primary Health Centres (PHCs), Community Health Centres (CHCs), leading up to District Hospitals and Tertiary Hospitals (like AIIMS).
- **Labour Shortage:** Massive 46% shortage of doctors and 82% shortage of specialists due to poor rural working environments, low pay, and better private/foreign opportunities.
- **Underfunding:** Public spending is stuck at **1.2% - 1.5% of GDP**. As a result, **65% of all health expenditure in India is borne "out of pocket" by citizens**, plunging millions into poverty.

#### Government Initiatives ⭐ (exam-important)
1. **PMSSY:** Upgrading medical colleges and setting up new AIIMS.
2. **Mission Indradhanush:** Rapid, full immunization coverage for children.
3. **Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY):** The world's largest health scheme providing **Rs. 5 lakh cover per family/year** for 10.7 crore poor families.
4. **National Medical Commission Act 2019:** Replaced the corrupt Medical Council of India to regulate medical education.
5. **Target:** Increase public health spending to **2.5% of GDP by 2025**.

## Section: Infrastructure Challenges (5.5 intro) 🔴

### Core Idea

Despite mega-projects like "Smart Cities", over 20% of central sector projects are severely delayed.

#### Primary Roadblocks
1. **Land Acquisition:** The single largest roadblock. Faces mass resistance from displaced farmers due to poor rehabilitation plans.
2. **Environmental Clearances:** Endless bureaucratic delays spanning the entire project cycle.

> **Quick Recall:**
> - NEP 2020 spending target: **6% of GDP**.
> - Health spending target (National Health Policy): **2.5% of GDP by 2025** (currently ~1.5%).
> - Out of pocket health spending in India: **~65%** (Extremely high compared to world average).
> - Ayushman Bharat provides **Rs. 5L health cover** per poor family.
> - The #1 reason infrastructure gets delayed is **Land Acquisition**.
