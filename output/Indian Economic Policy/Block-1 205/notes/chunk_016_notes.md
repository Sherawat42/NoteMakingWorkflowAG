## Section: Need for Integrated Services Policy (2.4.5) 🔴

### Core Idea

Services contribute 57% of GDP, ~25% of employment, high FDI share, and 1/3 of exports. Yet reforms have been **ad hoc** with no coherent strategy. India needs a comprehensive integrated services policy — like existing agricultural and industrial policies — defining the **sequence and pace** of reforms.

> **In Simple Terms:** India has a farming policy and a factory policy, but no "services policy" — even though services are now the biggest part of the economy. That's like running a restaurant without a menu plan.

## Section: Medium and Long-Term Growth Prospects (2.5) 🟡

### Key Concepts

#### Two Reasons for Optimism
1. **Savings-investment momentum**: Demographic dividend yet to peak; working-age population (30s-40s) has high savings rate
2. **Rising infrastructure investment**: Government prioritising infra → sustaining growth requires productivity improvements, skill upgradation, and innovation

#### Key Warning
"There is nothing preordained about India's economic rise — the sustenance of the growth story needs nourishment, not merely sound bites."

## Section: Major Policy Initiatives (2.5.1) 🔴

### Key Concepts

#### Containing Inflation (2.5.1.1) ⭐ (exam-important)

| Policy | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Monetary Policy Committee (MPC)** | Constituted Feb 2015; headline inflation target: **4% ±2%** |
| **FRBM Act (2003)** | Revived since 2016; GFD/GDP target: **3%**; declined from 4.5% (2013-14) to 3.4% (2018-19) |

#### JAM Trinity and Targeted Delivery (2.5.1.2) ⭐ (exam-important)

**JAM = Jan Dhan + Aadhaar + Mobile**

- **Aadhaar Act 2016**: Unique ID to every individual; 90%+ coverage; enables targeted subsidies
- **PMJDY**: Financial inclusion — bank accounts linked to mobile + Aadhaar
- **JAM trinity**: Secured Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to intended beneficiaries
- **Key DBT schemes**: MGNREGS, NSAP, PMAY-G, scholarships, fertiliser subsidies
- **PMUY (2016)**: LPG connections for BPL families
- **Ayushman Bharat (2018)**: ₹5 lakh health insurance cover for 10 crore BPL families at ₹100/month

#### Infrastructure Push (2.5.1.3) 🟡
- Every village electrified (April 2018)
- 20%+ of 132,000 km national highway length constructed in last 4 years
- **UDAAN (2017)**: Regional air connectivity to Tier 3/4 towns
- Special focus on North-East connectivity

### Definitions

- **MPC (Monetary Policy Committee)**: A committee constituted by the Government and RBI to target a headline inflation rate of 4% (±2%). ⭐ (exam-important)
- **JAM Trinity**: Jan Dhan + Aadhaar + Mobile — the three-pronged platform enabling Direct Benefit Transfer to beneficiaries. ⭐ (exam-important)
- **FRBM Act**: Fiscal Responsibility and Budget Management Act (2003) — mandates fiscal deficit reduction targets. ⭐ (exam-important)

> **Quick Recall:**
