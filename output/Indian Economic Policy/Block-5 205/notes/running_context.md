# Running Context for Block-5 205

## Recurring Themes
- **Economic Liberalization (1991)**: The pivot from restrictive (import substitution, FERA) to liberal (export promotion, FEMA, automatic route) policies is central to both trade and foreign capital discussions.
- **Trade Deficit vs. Services Surplus**: A consistent pattern in India's BoP where massive merchandise trade deficits (driven by POL imports) are offset by surpluses in invisibles (IT services and remittances).
- **FDI over FPI**: Policy favors long-term Foreign Direct Investment over volatile Foreign Portfolio Investment to maintain macroeconomic stability.
- **Outward FDI (OFDI)**: The evolution of India from a mere host of FDI to an active global investor seeking new markets and strategic assets.
- **Prudent Debt Management**: India prioritizes long-term commercial borrowings and keeps short-term debt low to avoid BoP crises.

## Common Terminology
- **BoP**: Balance of Payments.
- **CAD**: Current Account Deficit.
- **FDI**: Foreign Direct Investment.
- **FPI**: Foreign Portfolio Investment.
- **OFDI**: Overseas Foreign Direct Investment.
- **FERA/FEMA**: Foreign Exchange Regulation Act (old) / Foreign Exchange Management Act (new).
- **POL**: Petroleum, Oil, and Lubricants (major import).
