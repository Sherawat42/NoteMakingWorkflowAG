## Section: Consolidated FDI Policy 2020 - Prohibited and Permitted Sectors 🟢
<!-- Exam importance: 🟢 CONTEXT -->
<!-- Reason: Specific regulatory caps for FDI by sector -->

### Core Idea
The 2020 FDI policy clearly outlines sectors where foreign direct investment is entirely prohibited and details the specific equity caps and entry routes (Automatic vs. Government) for permitted sectors.


### Key Concepts
#### Prohibited Sectors
FDI is completely prohibited in:
- Lottery Business (including government/private/online)
- Gambling and Betting (casinos)
- Chit funds and Nidhi companies
- Trading in Transferable Development Rights (TDRs)
- Real Estate Business or Construction of Farmhouses (excluding townships, commercial premises, roads, bridges, REITs)
- Manufacturing of cigars, cheroots, cigarillos, and cigarettes (tobacco)
- Atomic Energy and Railway Operations (other than permitted activities)

#### Permitted Sectors (Highlights)
- **100% Automatic Route**: Agriculture (floriculture, seeds, animal husbandry), Plantation (tea, coffee, rubber), Coal/Lignite mining for captive consumption, Manufacturing, E-commerce activities, Single Brand Product Retail Trading, Duty Free Shops, Railway Infrastructure (construction, operation, maintenance), Credit Information Companies.
- **Up to 74% Automatic Route**: Private Sector Banking, Defence Industry (beyond 74% requires government approval for access to modern technology).
- **Up to 49% Automatic Route**: Insurance Company, Pension Sector, Power Exchanges, Infrastructure companies in Securities Markets.
- **Government Route Required**: Multi Brand Retail Trading (51%), Print Media (26%), Terrestrial Broadcasting FM (49%), Up-linking of News & Current Affairs (49%), Petroleum refining by PSUs (49%).

### Definitions
- **Automatic Route**: FDI allowed without prior approval of the Government or Reserve Bank of India.
- **Government Route**: Prior approval of the Government is required.
### Common Mistakes
- ❌ Mistake: Believing FDI is allowed in Real Estate speculation. → ✅ Correct: FDI is prohibited in Real Estate Business, but allowed in the development of townships, roads, and REITs.

### Edge Cases & Caveats
- Foreign technology collaboration (franchise, trademark, etc.) is also prohibited for Lottery and Gambling/Betting.
- Infusion of fresh foreign investment beyond 49% in a broadcasting company resulting in a change of ownership pattern requires Government approval.

### Quick Recall
> **Quick Recall:**
> - FDI is strictly prohibited in Lottery, Gambling, Chit Funds, Real Estate Business, and Cigarettes.
> - Most manufacturing and services fall under the 100% Automatic Route.
