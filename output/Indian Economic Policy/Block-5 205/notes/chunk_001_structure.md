### Core Idea
This section introduces Block 5, which covers External Sector and Trade Policy, including Trade Policy, Foreign Trade and Balance of Payment, and Foreign Capital.

### In Simple Terms

### Key Concepts
#### Block 5 Overview
The block comprises three units that highlight the characteristics of international trade, the status of Indian trade before and after 1991 reforms, instruments of trade policy, balance of payment, and the role of foreign capital in economic development.

### Definitions
- **External Sector**: The portion of a country's economy that interacts with the economies of other countries, primarily through international trade and foreign capital flows.
