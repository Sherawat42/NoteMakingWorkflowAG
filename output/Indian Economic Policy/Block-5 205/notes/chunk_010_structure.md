## Section: Statistical Tables on Foreign Capital and OFDI 🟢
<!-- Exam importance: 🟢 CONTEXT -->
<!-- Reason: Statistical data supporting trends discussed in previous chunks -->

### Core Idea
A comprehensive statistical review of India's foreign capital inflows and outflows from 2000 to 2020, demonstrating the exponential growth of FDI to India, the volatility of portfolio investments, and the rising prominence of India as an outward investor (OFDI).

### In Simple Terms
> **In Simple Terms:** The data shows that India is getting a lot more foreign investment than before, mostly from countries like Singapore and Mauritius into our service sectors. Also, Indian companies aren't just sitting at home; they are increasingly taking their money and investing it in other countries.

### Key Concepts
#### Foreign Investment Inflows (Tables 20.1 to 20.6)
- **FDI Growth**: Gross inflows grew from $4.03 billion in 2000-01 to $74.39 billion in 2019-20.
- **Portfolio Volatility**: Net portfolio investment saw massive fluctuations, with significant outflows during the 2008 global financial crisis and in 2015-16.
- **External Assistance & NRI Deposits**: Steady flow of external assistance (peaking in 2010-11 at $4.7 billion). NRI deposits grew consistently, reaching ~$130.5 billion by March 2020.
- **FDI by Sector**: Services (communication, retail, financial) dominate at 69.5% of inflows from 2015-2020. Manufacturing holds ~22.8%.
- **FDI by Country**: Singapore (29.1%) and Mauritius (25.3%) are the overwhelming top sources.

#### Overseas Foreign Direct Investment Outflows (Tables 20.7 to 20.9)
- **OFDI Components**: Mostly funded by Equity (66.7%) and Loans (30.6%) cumulatively from 2000-2020.
- **OFDI by Sector**: Dominated by Financial, Insurance, and Business Services (34.8%) and Manufacturing (22.1%).
- **OFDI by Country**: Top destinations are Singapore (19.2%), Mauritius (16.3%), USA (13.1%), and the UK (7.7%). This indicates a strong preference for developed nations and strategic hubs.

### Quick Recall
> **Quick Recall:**
> - Top FDI sources: Singapore, Mauritius.
> - Top FDI sectors: Services, Manufacturing.
> - Top OFDI destinations: Singapore, Mauritius, USA, UK.
