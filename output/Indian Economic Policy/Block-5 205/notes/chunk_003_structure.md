## Section: India and the Changing Nature of World Trade 🔴
<!-- Exam importance: 🔴 HIGH YIELD -->
<!-- Reason: Contemporary trade issues in services, IP, and agriculture -->

### Core Idea
World trade now involves Global Value Chains (GVCs), trade in services, intellectual property, and agricultural subsidies, presenting new challenges and opportunities for India.

### In Simple Terms
> **In Simple Terms:** Trade isn't just about shipping physical goods anymore. It's about global supply chains, call centers (services), patents (intellectual property), and arguments over farming subsidies.

### Key Concepts
#### Trade in Services
Recognized under WTO GATS in modes like cross-border supply (Mode 1), consumption abroad (Mode 2), and movement of natural persons (Mode 3). India strongly participates via BPO and ITES.

#### Trade in Intellectual Property (TRIPS)
Requires member countries to have similar IP protection. India had to allow product patents, not just process patents, affecting its pharmaceutical 'reverse engineering' practices.

#### Trade in Agriculture
Developed countries (USA, EU) provide large subsidies to their farmers, driving down global prices. India resists opening its agricultural markets to protect the livelihood of its millions of small farmers.

### Definitions
- **Global Value Chain (GVC)**: A chain of separate but inter-linked activities across multiple firms/geographies to bring a product from conception to delivery.
- **GATS**: General Agreement on Trade in Services.
- **TRIPS**: Trade Related Intellectual Property Rights.
