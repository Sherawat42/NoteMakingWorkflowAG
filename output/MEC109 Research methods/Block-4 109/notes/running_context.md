# Running Context

## Key Concepts Introduced
- **Factor Analysis Scope** (Chunk 001): Maps unknown concepts, classifies data, and identifies unobserved systematic patterns.
- **Determinate vs Indeterminate Models** (Chunk 001): Determinate models are for data reduction; indeterminate models account for co-variation through unobserved common factors.
- **Factor Loadings, Specific Variance, Communality** (Chunk 001): Core concepts in the orthogonal factor model detailing variance components.

## Definitions (⭐ exam-important)
- **Factor Analysis** (Chunk 001): Models representing observed variables as linear functions of latent or hypothetical variables. ⭐
- **Common Factors** (Chunk 001): Unobserved latent variables that control data variation. ⭐
- **Factor Loadings** (Chunk 001): Regression coefficients denoting variable-factor loading. ⭐
- **Communality** (Chunk 001): Sum of squared loadings representing variance explained. ⭐

## Named Models / Laws / Theories
- **Two-Factor Theory of Mental Tests** (Chunk 001): Charles Spearman's (1904) theory that scores have a general intelligence and test-specific part.
- **Orthogonal Factor Model** (Chunk 001): Formulates observable variables as linear functions of independent standard unobservable factors + errors.

## Key Data & Numbers

- **Principal Component Method vs Maximum Likelihood Method** (Chunk 002): Different methods for estimating factor parameters.
- **Factor Rotation** (Chunk 002): Improves interpretability by rotating axes to achieve simple structure.
- **Oblique Rotation** (Chunk 003): Allows factors to be correlated.
- **Factor Scores** (Chunk 003): Estimated values of factors for observations.
- **Canonical Correlation Analysis** (Chunk 004): Investigates relationship between two sets of variables.
- **Cluster Analysis** (Chunk 005): Classifies objects into internally homogenous groups.
- **K-Means vs Hierarchical Clustering** (Chunk 005): Partitioning vs Tree-building clustering methods.
- **Correspondence Analysis** (Chunk 006): Dimension reduction explicitly for categorical cross-tabular data.
- **Inertia and Chi-square Distance** (Chunk 007): Statistical measures of dispersion in correspondence analysis matrices.
- **Structural Equation Modeling (SEM)** (Chunk 008): A method examining causal links among observed and latent variables mapping theoretical relationships.
- **Latent vs Manifest Variables** (Chunk 009): Unobserved true constructs versus measured indicator variables.
- **Reflective vs Formative Indicators** (Chunk 009): Directions of causality between latent and manifest variables in a model. 
- **Model Evaluation and Modification** (Chunk 010): The process of checking and adjusting SEM models for optimal statistical fit.
