## 🗂️ Block Overview (Block Introduction Text)

> *"For undertaking any meaningful research in terms of situational assessment, testing of models, development of theory, evolving economic policy, assessing the impact of such policy etc., data is crucial."*

- **Block 6** is titled **"Data Base of Indian Economy"**
- It comprises **4 Units** covering different data domains of the Indian economy.

### Units at a Glance

| Unit | Title | Topic |
|------|-------|--------|
| Unit 21 | Macro-Variable Data | National Income, Saving & Investment |
| Unit 22 | Agricultural & Industrial Data | Agricultural Census, ASI, IIP |
| Unit 23 | Trade and Finance | DGCI&S, RBI Handbook, SEBI |
| Unit 24 | Social Sector | Employment, Education, Health, Environment |
