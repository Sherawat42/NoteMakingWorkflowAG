# Running Context — Block-6 109 (MEC109 Research methods)

## Book Theme
This book is about the **Database of the Indian Economy** — a comprehensive guide to the major statistical sources, agencies, and publications providing data across various sectors of the Indian economy: National Accounts, Agriculture, Industry, Trade, Finance, and the Social Sector.

## Key Themes Running Through the Book
- The institutional landscape of Indian statistics (CSO, NSSO, RBI, DGCI&S, DGE&T, etc.)
- The inter-linkage between data collection agencies and publications
- Coverage limitations and non-comparability issues in Indian datasets
- The distinction between organized and unorganized sectors in data availability
- The role of national and international data standards (SNA, SITC, NIC codes, ISCED)

## Concepts Introduced — All Chunks Processed

### Unit 21: National Accounts (Chunks 002–004)
- **CSO/NAS**: Apex source for national income, saving, and capital formation data
- **Types of Estimates**: Advance → Quick (First Revised) → Final
- **GVA at basic prices** (new headline measure since 2011-12 base); GDP at market prices = GVA + net taxes on products
- **Direct vs Indirect estimates**: organised sector uses direct data; unorganised sector uses benchmark surveys + extrapolation
- **IOTT (Input-Output Transactions Table)**: CSO (latest 2007-08) + Planning Commission (for each Five-Year Plan)
- **SDP**: Prepared by State DES using income originating approach (not income accruing); not comparable across states
- **Saving**: GDS = Public + Private Corporate + Household; household physical saving = residual
- **Capital Formation**: GDCF, GDFCF; two approaches — flow of funds and commodity flow
- **Other sources**: RBI Handbook, CMIE, EPWRF, Pre-Budget Economic Survey

### Unit 22: Agricultural & Industrial Data (Chunks 005–006)
- **Agricultural Census** (quinquennial, DES Ministry of Agriculture): operational holdings data
- **Cost of Cultivation** (DES): feeds into CACP and MSP recommendations
- **Annual Crop Production**: crop-cutting experiments; 5 advance estimates + final
- **Livestock Census** (quinquennial, AHSD): animal numbers and types
- **ASG** (Agricultural Statistics at a Glance): comprehensive annual reference for agricultural sector
- **BAHS** (Basic Animal Husbandry Statistics): comprehensive for livestock sector
- **ASI** (Annual Survey of Industries): factory sector; ~30 variables at 3-4 digit NIC; managed by CSO
- **IIP** (Index of Industrial Production): monthly; 6-week lag; base 2011-12; use-based classification
- **Unorganised Sector**: NSSO surveys (DE/NDE/OAE); 4th MSME Census (2006-07); Economic Census
- **Industrial Finance**: RBI Handbook + CMIE (EIS) volumes
- **Global sources**: FAO (agriculture), ILO (employment), UNIDO (TFP)

### Unit 23: Trade and Finance (Chunks 007–008)
- **DGCI&S**: Merchandise trade — FOB for exports, CIF for imports; 8-digit ITC(HS) classification
- **Trade Divergence (DGCI&S vs RBI BOP)**: 3 reasons — physical vs payment tracking; defence imports excluded; non-customs-crossing imports missed
- **Terms of Trade**: GTT (QI ratio), NTT (UVI ratio), ITT (NTT × QI exports / 100)
- **Services Trade**: captured as 'non-factor services' under 'Invisibles' in RBI BOP
- **Public Finances**: GFD, GPD, RD, PRD defined; Central + State + Combined data in RBI Handbook
- **FDI**: RBI + DIPP; data not comparable pre- and post-2000 (narrower vs broader coverage)
- **Monetary Aggregates**: M₀ → M₁ → M₃ → NM₂/NM₃ → L₁/L₂/L₃ (new aggregates from RBI Bulletin, Nov 2000)
- **Price Indices**: WPI (Econ Advisor), CPI-IW/CPI-AL (Labour Bureau), CPI Combined (CSO)
- **Financial Markets**: SEBI Handbook (capital markets, FII, stock exchanges, P/E ratios)

### Unit 24: Social Sector (Chunks 009–012)
- **Employment**: EMI/DGE&T (organised quarterly), Economic Census, ASI, Census (decennial), NSSO quinquennial
- **NSSO Measures**: UPS, UPSS, CWS, CDS — UPSS gives highest estimate; CDS captures underemployment
- **Labour Welfare**: Labour Bureau Yearbooks (injuries, disputes, trade unions); ESIC; EPFO
- **Education**: MHRD (school/higher), NUEPA (DISE — district level), NCERT (AIES), UGC, IAMR (NTMIS + Tracer Studies)
- **InSCED**: India's own education classification (developed 2014 by MHRD)
- **Health Infrastructure**: Rural Health Statistics (RHS) by MoH&FW; CBHI (NHP annual)
- **Vital Rates**: SRS by RGI — IMR, CDR, MMR, birth/death rates, life expectancy
- **NFHS**: 4 rounds (1992-93, 1998-99, 2005-06, 2014-15); comprehensive health and nutrition database
- **Child Malnutrition Indices**: Weight-for-Age (underweight); Height-for-Age (stunted); Weight-for-Height (wasted)
- **Shelter**: Census (1991+) and NSSO (49th, 58th, 69th rounds); includes slum data
- **Environment**: MOEF Annual Reports; CSO Compendium on Environment Statistics; CSO Green National Accounts (2013)
- **Displacement**: MAJOR DATA GAP — no systematic government data on displaced populations and resettlement
- **Weaker Sections**: Commission for SC/ST; Ministry of Social Justice; National/State Commissions for Women, Children, Aged, Backward Classes, Minorities
