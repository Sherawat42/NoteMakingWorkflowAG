## 22.2 Agricultural Data 🔴 HIGH YIELD

### 22.2.1 Agricultural Census 🔴

#### What is it?
- A **quinquennial (every 5 years)** census of all operational holdings in India.
- Latest completed census: **2010-11** (the 9th Agricultural Census).

#### Conducted by:
- **Directorate of Economics & Statistics (DES)**, Ministry of Agriculture & Cooperation

#### Data Collected
- **Size and structure** of agricultural holdings
- Area, land use, source of irrigation, tenancy, land utilization
- Socio-economic characteristics of operators (gender, social group, age, education)
- **Village-level and district-level** detailed data

#### Publication
- Results published in **Agricultural Census reports** by DES, Ministry of Agriculture
- Detailed village/district/state tables available on the Ministry's **website**

> 🔴 **Exam Tip**: Agricultural Census is the ONLY comprehensive, periodic source of data on all operational holdings including small, marginal, and sub-marginal holders.

---

### 22.2.2 Studies on Cost of Cultivation 🔴

#### Conducted by:
- **DES, Ministry of Agriculture** — ongoing studies since the 1960s

#### Coverage:
- **25-32 crops** studied across major crop-growing states
- Covers all: food grains, oilseeds, commercial crops, spices, etc.

#### Data Collected:
- **Cost of production** per unit of output (per quintal or per hectare)
- Input costs: seed, fertilizers, labour, irrigation, machinery
- **Comprehensive cost concepts**: A1, A2, B1, B2, C1, C2, C3 (increasingly inclusive)
- Output price, profit margin
- **Pricing recommendations** made to Commission on Agricultural Costs and Prices (CACP)

#### Publication:
- *"Cost of Cultivation in India"* — published by DES, Ministry of Agriculture
- CACP reports also refer to this data

> 🔴 **Exam Key**: The MSP (Minimum Support Price) is significantly influenced by cost of cultivation data.

### 22.2.3 Annual Estimates of Crop Production 🔴

#### Prepared by:
- **DES, Ministry of Agriculture** in collaboration with **State Directorates of Agriculture**

#### Method:
- Based on **crop-cutting experiments** (a sample survey method)
- **Five rounds** per year to provide a running picture of crop production:
  - 1st Advance Estimate (in September/October)
  - 2nd Advance Estimate (in December/January)
  - 3rd Advance Estimate (in February/March)
  - 4th Advance Estimate (in May/June)
  - Final Estimates (with a lag)

#### Data Available:
- **Area sown**, **area harvested**, **yield per hectare**, **total production**
- For both Rabi (winter) and Kharif (monsoon) crops
- Covers all **principal food and non-food crops**

#### Key Publication:
- *"Agricultural Statistics at a Glance" (ASG)* — comprehensive annual publication by DES, Ministry of Agriculture
- Also published in **Economic Survey** as summary tables

### 22.2.4 Livestock Census 🔴

#### Conducted by:
- **Animal Husbandry Statistics Division (AHSD)**, Ministry of Agriculture
- **Quinquennial** — every 5 years (the 19th Livestock Census was conducted in 2012)

#### Data Collected:
- **Number and type** of livestock and poultry animals (species, breed, sex, age)
- Implements and machinery used in agriculture
- Coverage down to the **village level**

#### Publication:
- Data available in *"Basic Animal Husbandry Statistics" (BAHS)* and on Ministry website

---

### 22.2.5 Data on Production of Major Livestock Products 🟡

#### Conducted by:
- **Integrated Sample Survey (ISS)** — an annual survey by the Directorate of Animal Husbandry
- State Governments send data to Central Government

#### Data Collected:
- Milk production by type (cow, buffalo, goat)
- Egg production
- Wool production
- Meat production

#### Publication:
- **BAHS** (Basic Animal Husbandry Statistics)

---

### 22.2.6 Agricultural Statistics at a Glance (ASG) 🔴

> The **go-to single reference** for all agricultural statistics in India.

- Published **annually** by DES, Ministry of Agriculture
- Comprehensive — covers all aspects:
  - Production, area, yield
  - Land use and land utilization
  - Fertilizer and pesticide consumption
  - Agricultural credit (also refer to RBI Handbook and NABARD publications)
  - Irrigation (also from Central Water Commission)
  - Cost of cultivation and CACP data
  - **Characteristics of land holdings** by size, across multiple Agricultural Censuses
  - Livestock and poultry statistics
  - Agricultural prices and indices

---

### 22.2.7 Another Source of Data on Irrigation 🟡

- **Central Water Commission (CWC)**, Ministry of Water Resources — publishes data on:
  - Potential and utilization of surface and ground water resources
  - Command areas of large and medium irrigation projects
- **Central Ground Water Board (CGWB)** — ground water resource data
- ASG also presents irrigation statistics from these agencies

## ✅ Self-Review
- Three quinquennial surveys: **Agricultural Census** (holdings), **Livestock Census** (animals), and (in Unit 24) **NSSO employment surveys**.
- The cost of cultivation studies feed directly into CACP recommendations and hence **Minimum Support Price (MSP)** policy.
- ASG + BAHS = the two key comprehensive agricultural publications.
- Never confuse Agricultural Census (holdings/land structure) with Crop Production Estimates (annual output).
