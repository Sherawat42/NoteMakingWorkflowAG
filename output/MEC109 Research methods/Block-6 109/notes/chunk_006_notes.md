### 22.3.2 Factory Sector — Annual Survey of Industries (ASI) 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT INDUSTRIAL DATA SOURCE

#### Coverage:
- All **factories registered** under:
  - Factories Act, 1948
  - Bidi and Cigar Workers (Conditions of Employment) Act, 1966
- Excludes: Defence establishments, factories under the Atomic Energy Act

#### Conducted by:
- **CSO (Central Statistical Organisation)**, MoSPI
- Data collected through a **census approach** for large factories and **sample approach** for smaller ones

#### Frequency:
- **Annual** survey — results available with a lag of approximately 2 years

#### Data Collected (~30 Variables per Factory):
- **Investment**: Fixed assets — land, buildings, machinery, equipment
- **Inputs**: Raw materials, fuels, electricity, other inputs
- **Output**: Total output at ex-factory prices
- **Value Added**: GVA (Gross Value Added) and NVA (Net Value Added)
- **Employment**: Mandays worked by workers, employees; male/female breakdown
- **Factor Shares**: Wages, salaries, employer contributions
- **Energy**: Fuel and electricity consumed

#### Classification:
- Data published at the **3 or 4-digit NIC code level** by States/Union Territories
- Enables derivation of technical ratios (capital-output ratio, labour productivity, etc.)

#### Key Database: ASICS (ASI Computer System)
- Allows users to retrieve specific data relating to ASI from an online database

#### Publication:
- **ASI Summary Results** published by CSO
- Available on MoSPI website

> 🔴 **Exam Tip**: ASI is the most detailed multi-variable source for **factory sector** data in India. It covers ~30 characteristics at 3-4 digit NIC code level.

### 22.3.3 Monthly Production of Selected Industries and IIP 🔴

#### Index of Industrial Production (IIP)

| Parameter | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Compiled by** | CSO (now MoSPI) |
| **Frequency** | Monthly |
| **Time Lag** | Released within **6 weeks** of reference month; revised in next 2 months |
| **Base Year** | Currently **2011-12 = 100** |
| **Coverage** | Selected industries representing about 72% of total manufacturing |

#### Structure of IIP:
- **Use-Based Classification**: Primary goods, Capital goods, Intermediate goods, Infrastructure/Construction goods, Consumer Durables, Consumer Non-Durables
- **Sector-wise**: Mining, Manufacturing, Electricity

#### Quick Estimates:
- **Monthly Production** of selected industries released as "Quick Estimates" within 6 weeks.
- State Governments are also developing **State-level IIPs** comparable to the national IIP.

#### Energy Statistics:
- **Energy Statistics** published by CSO provides data on energy sector production (not the same as IIP)

### 22.3.4 Data on Unorganised Industrial Sector 🔴

#### Why This Matters:
- The unorganised sector is the **largest employer** in the industrial sector.
- It is poorly covered by ASI, ASI only covers registered factories.

#### Key Sources:

| Source | Details |
|---|---|
| **NSSO Unorganised Sector Surveys** | Conducted every 5–6 years; covers all unorganised non-agricultural enterprises. Latest: NSS Report 549 (Unorganised Non-Agricultural Enterprises, 2010-11) |
| **Census of MSME (Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises)** | 4th Census (2006-07) is the most recent comprehensive national census of the MSME sector |
| **Economic Census** | Covers all enterprises including the unorganised sector; conducted in 1977, 1980, 1990, 1998, 2003, 2013 |

#### Types of Unorganised Establishments (DE/NDE/OAE):
- **Directory Establishments (DE)**: 6+ persons, at least 1 hired worker
- **Non-Directory Establishments (NDE)**: 1-5 persons, at least 1 hired worker
- **Own Account Enterprises (OAE)**: No regular hired worker

## ✅ Self-Review
- ASI = census/sample of **registered factories** only. Excludes the entire unorganised sector.
- IIP is the **monthly pulse** of industrial activity; ASI provides detailed **annual** factory-level data.
- The 4th MSME Census (2006-07) is the benchmark for unorganised small-scale industry data.
- RBI Handbook + CMIE (EIS) = comprehensive sources for industrial finance and market data.
- Know the three NSSO establishment types: DE, NDE, OAE — these come up in exam questions.
