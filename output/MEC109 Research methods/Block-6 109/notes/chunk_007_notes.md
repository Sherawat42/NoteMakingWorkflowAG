## 23.2.1 Merchandise Trade — DGCI&S 🔴 HIGH YIELD

### Primary Agency: DGCI&S
- **Directorate General of Commercial Intelligence and Statistics (DGCI&S)**
- Located in **Kolkata** (website: www.dgciskol.nic.in)
- Collects and compiles data on **imports and exports** through all recognized seaports, airports, land customs stations, and inland container depots.

### Scope of Merchandise Trade Data
- **Exports**: Indian merchandise + re-exports; valued at **FOB (Free on Board)**
- **Imports**: Foreign merchandise (whether for consumption, bonding, or re-export); valued at **CIF (Cost, Insurance, and Freight)**
- Based on **General System of Recording** (not Special System)

### Trade Classification System
| Period | Classification System |
|---|---|
| April 1977 – March 1987 | ITC-Rev. 2 (based on SITC-Rev.2) |
| April 1987 onwards | **ITC (HS)** — Indian Trade Classification based on Harmonised System (8-digit codes) |

> **ITC (HS)**: Developed by DGCI&S by subdividing 6-digit codes of the **Harmonised Commodity Description and Coding System** (Harmonised System) of the **World Customs Organization (WCO), Brussels**.

### Key DGCI&S Publications

| Publication | Frequency | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Foreign Trade Statistics of India (Principal Commodities & Countries) | Monthly | Provisional commodity-wise, country-wise, port-wise data |
| Monthly Statistics of Foreign Trade of India (Vol I: Exports, Vol II: Imports) | Monthly | Detailed 8-digit ITC(HS) data |
| Statistics of Foreign Trade by Countries | Quarterly | Country-wise exports and imports |
| Selected Statistics of Foreign Trade of India | Annual | Summary tables |
| Statistics of Customs & Excise Revenue Collections | Annual | Revenue data |
| Statistics of Foreign & Coastal Cargo Movements | Annual | Cargo movement data |

### DGCI&S Website Features:
- Static pages: History, summary data on principal commodities
- Dynamic pages: Online data dissemination (free and paid); at least 24 months final data at 8-digit level
- **PISS (Priced Information Service System)**: Provides information to private parties on payment basis; provides aggregate/detailed data to CMIE for trade intelligence

### Index Numbers of Foreign Trade 🔴

| Index | Formula | Measures |
|---|---|---|
| **Unit Value Index (UVI)** | `∑(P₁ × Qₜ) / ∑(P₀ × Qₜ)` | Price changes (unit value) |
| **Quantum Index (QI)** | `∑(P₀ × Qₜ) / ∑(P₀ × Q₀)` | Volume/quantity changes |

### Three Types of Terms of Trade 🔴

| Measure | Formula |
|---|---|
| **Gross Terms of Trade (GTT)** | 100 × [QI of Imports / QI of Exports] |
| **Net Terms of Trade (NTT)** | 100 × [UVI of Exports / UVI of Imports] |
| **Income Terms of Trade (ITT)** | [NTT × QI of Exports] / 100 = [UVI(exports) × QI(exports)] / UVI(imports) |

> Existing index base year: **1979-80** (old); DGCI&S is constructing new indices with base **1998-99**.

### Other Sources for Trade Data
- **RBI Handbook**: Presents DGCI&S data as time series; also covers BOP data
- **CMIE**: Volume on Foreign Trade and BOP
- **EPWRF**: Long time series data on foreign trade (from their 35 special statistics datasets)

### 🚨 Divergence: DGCI&S vs RBI BOP Data 🔴 VERY IMPORTANT

There is a known discrepancy between merchandise trade data from **DGCI&S** and **RBI's Balance of Payments (BOP)** data.

| Issue | DGCI&S | RBI BOP |
|---|---|---|
| **What is tracked** | Physical imports/exports at customs | Payment transactions for merchandise |
| **Government imports** | Does NOT capture (exempted from customs duty) | Captures (including defence imports) |
| **Non-customs imports** | Does NOT capture (e.g., oil rigs, some aircraft) | Captures (payments recorded in BOP) |

**Impact of Divergence (example from 2005-06)**:
- Trade deficit as % of GDP:
  - DGCI&S data: **5.2%**
  - RBI BOP data: **7.7%** — a difference of **2.5% of GDP**!
- Current Account Deficit (CAD):
  - DGCI&S based: **0.3% of GDP**
  - RBI BOP based: **2.9% of GDP**

> 🔴 **Exam Key**: Always specify the source when quoting trade deficit figures. DGCI&S and RBI BOP data are NOT directly comparable.

## 23.2.2 Services Trade 🟡

- India exports/imports many services: transport, travel, software, ITES, business services, professional services.
- Captured as **"non-factor services"** under **"Invisibles"** in RBI's BOP tables.
- **Invisibles** = Services + Transfers + Income
- Detailed breakdown in RBI Handbook (Table 140): Credit, Debit, and Net for:
  - (a) Services: Travel, Transportation, Insurance, Software, Business, Financial, Communication
  - (b) Transfers: Official, Private
  - (c) Income: Investment income, Compensation of employees
