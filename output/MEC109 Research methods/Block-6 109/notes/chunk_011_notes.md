## 24.4 Health 🔴 HIGH YIELD

### Primary Agencies for Health Data

| Agency | Publication |
|---|---|
| **Central Bureau of Health Intelligence (CBHI)**, Ministry of Health & Family Welfare | **National Health Profile (NHP)** of India — annual |
| **Office of the Registrar General of India (RGI)**, Ministry of Home Affairs | SRS Reports, SRS Compendium, SRS Bulletin, Census Social & Cultural Tables (C Series) |
| **Ministry of Health & Family Welfare** | Report on NFHS-4 (2014-15) |
| **WHO (India)** | Country statistics on health profile, nutrition, mortality, risk factors |
| **International Institute for Population Sciences (IIPS)** | NFHS field implementation and reports |

### Sources for District-Level Health Data
- **Annual Health Survey (AHS)** — conducted by RGI
- **District Level Health Survey (DLHS)** — conducted by MoH&FW
- **Rural Health Statistics (RHS)** — annual; published by MoH&FW from 2005 as part of **National Health Mission (NHM)**

### 24.4.1 Health Infrastructure 🔴

#### Rural Health Statistics (RHS) data — types:

| Data | Details |
|---|---|
| **Demographic indicators** | State-wise village count, population, growth rates/density; crude birth/death rates, IMR |
| **Rural health infrastructure** | Sub-centres, Primary Health Centres (PHCs), Community Health Centres (CHCs), Sub-divisional/District hospitals, Mobile medical teams; Shortfall data |
| **Health manpower** | ANMs (Auxiliary Nurse-Midwife), Health Workers (Male/Female), LHVs, Doctors at PHCs, Surgeons/Obstetricians/Physicians/Paediatricians at CHCs, Pharmacists, Lab technicians, Nursing staff |
| **Sub-centre facilities** | ANM quarters, water supply, electricity, all-weather road, IPHS norm compliance |
| **PHC facilities** | Labour room, OT, beds, water, electricity, road, telephone, computer, referral transport, RKS |
| **CHC facilities** | Four specialists, OT, laboratory, X-ray, 30+ beds, AYUSH drugs, allopathic drugs |
| **Training institutions** | ANM/HW (Female) training schools, MPW (Male) training centres |
| **Expenditure on health** | Overall + individual programme-wise (malaria, filarial, leprosy eradication) |

### 24.4.2 Public Health, Morbidity and Mortality Indicators 🔴

| Data Type | Source |
|---|---|
| Vaccination of children and pregnant women | RHS (MoH&FW) |
| Notified cases and deaths from communicable diseases (cholera, malaria, kala-azar, Japanese encephalitis) | CBHI (NHP) |
| Leprosy, tuberculosis detection, treatment, discharge | NHP |
| Cancer, mental diseases, communicable diseases (diphtheria, polio, tetanus, hepatitis, rabies) | NHP |
| National AIDS Control Programme progress | NHP |
| CGHS, ESI scheme utilization | NHP |
| Causes of Death statistics — by age, sex, disease groups | **RGI** (SRS/Medical Certification) |
| **Vital Rates** — Birth Rate (BR), Death Rate (DR), Natural Growth Rate | **SRS, RGI** |

### Key Mortality Indicators 🔴

| Indicator | Definition |
|---|---|
| **CDR** (Crude Death Rate) | Total deaths per 1000 population |
| **IMR** (Infant Mortality Rate) | Deaths before 1st birthday per 1000 live births |
| **NNR** (Nano-Natal Rate) | Deaths in first month of life |
| **PNR** (Post-Natal Rate) | Deaths after 1st month but before 1st birthday |
| **SBR** (Still Birth Rate) | Babies born dead per 1000 total births |
| **ASDRs** (Age-Specific Death Rates) | Death rate for specific age groups |
| **MMR** (Maternal Mortality Rate) | Deaths related to pregnancy/childbirth |
| **e₀ (Life Expectancy at Birth)** | Average number of years a person born today will live — also at ages 10, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60 |

> **Source for all vital rates**: **SRS (Sample Registration System)**, published by **RGI**

### 24.4.3 National Family Health Survey (NFHS) 🔴 VERY HIGH YIELD

#### Background
| NFHS Round | Year | Sample Size |
|---|---|---|
| NFHS-1 | 1992-93 | — |
| NFHS-2 | 1998-99 | — |
| NFHS-3 | 2005-06 | ~1,90,000 households |
| **NFHS-4** | **2014-15** | ~5,60,000 households |

- **NFHS-4** first covers all **35 States and UTs** as per Census 2011.
- Conducted by: **18 field agencies** (in NFHS-3); NFHS-4 through 11 agencies (AMS, DRS, GFK, IIHMR, NIELSON, etc.)
- **Coordinated by**: International Institute for Population Sciences (IIPS), Mumbai
- **Funded by**: USAID, DFID (UK), Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation, UNICEF, UNFPA, Govt. of India

#### What Makes NFHS Special
- Provides **State-level estimates** of demographic and health parameters
- Allows rural-urban disaggregation; NFHS-3 separately estimates for **8 cities** and **slum areas**
- Reports downloadable from: http://www.rchiips.org/nfhs/sub_report.shtml

#### Data Provided by NFHS 🔴

| Category | Key Data Points |
|---|---|
| **Household characteristics** | Education, housing, durable goods, standard of living, access to basic amenities (electricity, water, sanitation, fuel) |
| **Women's status** | Age at first marriage, exposure to mass media, employment, empowerment, domestic violence |
| **Fertility** | Current fertility, fertility trends, contraceptive use, birth intervals, sex preference |
| **Child mortality** | IMR, NNR, PNR, child mortality (1–4 years), under-five mortality |
| **Morbidity** | Disease prevalence, variation by State |
| **Child healthcare** | Vaccination, ARI (acute respiratory infection), diarrhoea treatment, ORS use |
| **HIV/AIDS awareness** | Knowledge and prevention |
| **Women's nutrition** | Food consumption, anaemia, BMI (Body Mass Index), iodised salt |
| **Child nutrition** | Breastfeeding, anaemia, acute/chronic malnutrition: Weight-for-Age, Height-for-Age, Weight-for-Height indices |
| **Maternal health** | Antenatal care, place of delivery, post-partum care |
| **Couple Protection Rate** | % of currently married women aged 15–49 using family planning |

#### Key Nutritional Indices for Children 🔴

| Index | Measures | Criterion |
|---|---|---|
| **Weight-for-Age** | Composite of acute + chronic malnutrition | <–2 SD from reference = "underweight" |
| **Height-for-Age** | Linear growth retardation (chronic undernutrition) | <–2 SD from reference = "stunted" |
| **Weight-for-Height** | Body mass relative to height (acute undernutrition) | <–2 SD from reference = "wasted" |

> Reference population: WHO/National Institute of Nutrition, Hyderabad standard

## 24.5 Shelter and Amenities 🟡

| Source | Scope |
|---|---|
| **Population Census** (from 1991) | Housing and amenities at village/urban block level; includes slums from Census 2001 |
| **Census 2011** (www.censusindia.gov.in) | Housing stock, amenities, assets at district level; separate slum tables |
| **NSSO Surveys** (49th/58th/69th rounds: 1993/2002/2012) | State-level data on housing conditions; more detail than Census |
| **NFHS** | Some housing and amenities data |

### NSS 69th Round Reports (July–December 2012):
1. "Key Indicators of Drinking Water, Sanitation, Hygiene and Housing Condition in India"
2. "Drinking Water, Sanitation, Hygiene and Housing Condition in India"
3. "Housing Condition and Amenities in India"

### Types of Data
- Distribution by housing type: pucca, semi-pucca, kutchha
- Toilet facility access by social group (SC/ST/other)
- Safe drinking water access by source
- Electricity for lighting
- Households with NO access to electricity, water, toilet (triple deprivation)
- Type of slum (notified/non-notified), infrastructure, tenure
- Reasons for migrating to slum; identification documents
- Durable goods ownership; workforce participation in slums

## 24.6 Social Consequences of Development 🟡

### The Problem: Data Gaps on Displaced Persons

- Development projects (dams, industries, wildlife sanctuaries) displace people — "oustees"
- They were supposed to be given jobs and compensation — but data on rehabilitation is severely lacking.

#### Available Data Sources (Limited)
- **DGE&T**: Data on oustees registered as job seekers and those placed (very partial coverage)
- **ICSSR study (1994)**: Estimated displacement between 1951–1990 due to mines, dams, industries, wildlife sanctuaries; estimated resettlement up to 1980
- **Walter Fernandes study** (West Bengal, 1947–2000): Displaced people, physically displaced, resettled, left to fend for themselves; proportion of Dalits/tribals
- **Govt of Orissa data** (quoted in EPW by Manipadma Jena): Villages affected by Hirakud Dam; families displaced; families resettled

### Key Conclusion 🔴
A **transparent, systematic database** on displacement and resettlement is urgently needed — covering: demographics, resettlement status, income levels, access to basic needs, time taken, expenditure.

## 24.7 Environment 🟡

### Key Agencies and Publications

| Agency/Source | Data |
|---|---|
| **Ministry of Environment & Forests (MOEF)** | Annual Reports — air/water quality, forest cover, biodiversity |
| **Central/State Pollution Control Boards** | Ambient air quality; water quality monitoring |
| **CSO** | "Compendium on Environment Statistics, India 2003" — comprehensive |
| **CSO** | **"Green National Accounts in India" (2013)** — first Green GDP framework |
| **Forest Survey of India (FSI)** | Forest cover assessment |

### Types of Environmental Data
- **Ambient Air Quality**: Concentration of SO₂, NO₂, SPM (Solid Particulate Matter) in major cities
- **Vehicle emissions**: % of petrol vehicles meeting CO emission standards
- **Water Quality**: Yamuna river (Delhi) — dissolved oxygen, BOD, faecal coliforms, total coliforms, ammonical nitrogen
- Noise pollution, soil degradation (mentioned but datasets limited)

### Green National Accounts
- The international system of national accounts (SNA) is too narrow — does not capture environmental costs.
- "**Green accounting**" = adjusting national accounts for environmental degradation.
- CSO published "**Green National Accounts in India — A Framework**" in 2013.

## ✅ Self-Review
- **SRS** (Sample Registration System) by RGI = primary source for vital rates (IMR, CDR, MMR, birth rates, death rates).
- **NFHS** is the comprehensive health and nutrition survey (4 rounds: 1992, 1998, 2005, 2014).
- Know the three child malnutrition indices: Weight-for-Age (underweight), Height-for-Age (stunted), Weight-for-Height (wasted).
- For environment data: MOEF Annual Reports + CSO Compendium on Environment Statistics.
- The displacement/resettlement data gap is a critical analytical issue — no comprehensive government database exists.
