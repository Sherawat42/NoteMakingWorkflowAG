## 24.2 Employment, Unemployment and Labour Force 🔴 HIGH YIELD

### 24.2.1 Magnitude of Employment and Unemployment 🔴

#### Source 1: Employment Market Information (EMI) Programme 🔴
| Parameter | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Managed by** | DGE&T (Directorate General of Employment & Training), Ministry of Labour |
| **Coverage** | Non-agricultural establishments in private sector with ≥25 persons; ALL public sector establishments (any size), except defence and Indian missions abroad |
| **Optional submission** | Establishments with 10–24 persons (voluntary) |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly (Quarterly Employment Reviews) and Annual (Annual Employment Reviews) |
| **Data Collected** | Employment levels, vacancies occurring and filled, occupational distribution (biennial returns) |
| **NIC code level** | 3-digit NIC code level |

**Limitations of EMI**:
- Excludes entire agricultural sector, self-employed persons, part-time workers
- Non-response in return submissions distorts data
- Organizational register may be incomplete

#### Employment Exchange Data 🟡
- DGE&T and State Directorates of Employment (DEs) maintain data on:
  - Number of job seekers by age, sex, education, social group on **live register**
  - Number of vacancies notified and placements made
- **Important caveat**: Live register should NOT be used as a measure of unemployment because:
  - Surveys show many registered are already employed
  - Not all unemployed are registered
  - Register overestimates AND underestimates simultaneously

#### Source 2: Economic Census 🔴
- Conducted in **1977, 1980, 1990, 1998, 2003, 2013**
- Covers all economic enterprises EXCEPT those in **crop production and plantation**
- Includes the unorganised sector outside crop production
- Data on employment in all these enterprises

#### Source 3: Annual Survey of Industries (ASI) 🔴
- Factory sector employment at **3-digit NIC 2008 code level**
- Along with investment, output, inputs, value added → enables derivation of technical ratios
- Quality tied to completeness of the factory frame (enforcement of Factories Act 1948)

#### Other Sector-Specific Sources 🟡

| Sector | Source |
|---|---|
| **Railways** | Railway Board, Ministry of Railways |
| **Banking** | Reserve Bank of India (RBI) |
| **Posts & Telecommunications** | Department of Posts; Ministry of Telecommunications |
| **Insurance** | Ministry of Finance |
| **Government Employees** | Census of Central Government Employees (DGE&T); State Government census |

#### Labour Bureau Publications 🟡
- **Indian Labour Yearbook**: Data on factory sector employment by industry groups; mines and quarries; shops/commercial establishments/restaurants
- Data collected via statutory returns (Factories Act 1948, Mines Act 1952, Shops & Establishments Act)
- Also covers: tea, coffee, rubber plantation employment (from Tea, Coffee, Rubber Boards)

### Comprehensive Sources: Population Census and NSSO Surveys 🔴

#### Population Census (Decennial)
- **Office of the Registrar General and Census Commissioner of India (RGI)**
- Every **10 years** — based on complete enumeration
- Workers classified as:
  - **Main Workers** (worked ≥183 days in a year)
  - **Marginal Workers** (worked <183 days)
  - **Non-Workers**
- Further classified into 4 economic activity categories:
  1. Cultivators
  2. Agricultural Labourers
  3. Household Industry workers
  4. Other workers

**Census 2011 Data published includes**:
- (a) **Primary Census Abstract (PCA)**: village/urban ward level — main workers, marginal workers, non-workers by sex, SC/ST, 4-fold economic activity classification
- (b) Main and marginal workers by single year of age, 4-fold economic classification, social group — at India/State/district level

### NSSO Quinquennial Employment & Unemployment Surveys 🔴

| Parameter | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Conducted by** | National Sample Survey Office (NSSO), MoSPI |
| **Frequency** | Every 5 years (quinquennial) |
| **Latest** | 68th Round (July 2011 – June 2012) |
| **Previous rounds** | Oct 1972–Sep 1973, Jul 1977–Jun 1978, Jan–Dec 1983, Jul 1987–Jun 1988, Jul 1993–Jun 1994, Jul 1999–Jun 2000, Jul 2004–Jun 2005 |

### 4 Measures of Employment/Unemployment in NSSO Surveys 🔴

| Measure | Type | Based on |
|---|---|---|
| **UPS** (Usual Principal Status) | Usual | Major activity in reference year (365 days) |
| **UPSS** (Usual Principal & Subsidiary Status) | Usual | Main + subsidiary activity together |
| **CWS** (Current Weekly Status) | Current | Activity in reference week |
| **CDS** (Current Daily Status) | Current | Activity on each day of reference week |

> 🔴 **Exam Tip**: UPS excludes subsidiary work; UPSS includes it — UPSS gives HIGHER workforce counts (especially for females). CDS is the most rigorous measure for underemployment.

### Types of Data from NSSO Employment Surveys 🔴

The surveys provide per-thousand distributions (R, U, R+U) at national and State levels:

| Category | Data Available |
|---|---|
| By Employment Status | Self-employed, regular wage/salaried, casual labour |
| By Age Group, Education Level | UPS/UPSS/CWS/CDS workforce |
| By Economic Activity | Up to 3-digit NIC code |
| By Occupation | Up to 3-digit NCO 1968 code |
| By MPCE classes | Shows employment among poor vs. non-poor |
| Underemployment | CWS unemployed among UPS employed; CDS working days |
| Gender Differences | Female workforce participation, occupational segregation |
| Casual Labour Earnings | Average daily wage by sex, age, operation, sub-round |

> Unit record data (microdata) from NSSO surveys available on CD/download from MoSPI on signing an undertaking and paying a fee.

## ✅ Self-Review
- **EMI Programme** = quarterly, organised sector only. Strong for government/public sector employment trends.
- **NSSO quinquennial surveys** = comprehensive, nationwide, includes self-employed and unorganised sector.
- **Census** = complete enumeration, every 10 years, down to village/ward level.
- Know all four NSSO measures (UPS, UPSS, CWS, CDS) — exam favourite. UPSS always gives maximum employment.
- The live register of employment exchanges is NOT a reliable measure of unemployment.
