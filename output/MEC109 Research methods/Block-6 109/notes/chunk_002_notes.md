## 21.2 National Accounts Statistics 🔴 HIGH YIELD

### 21.2.1 Structure of the Indian Statistical System

#### Primary Agency: CSO (Central Statistical Organisation)
- Under the **Ministry of Statistics & Programme Implementation (MoSPI)**
- Responsible for compiling **National Accounts Statistics (NAS)**
- Publishes NAS annually since **1956** (initially on a calendar-year basis, later financial-year)

#### Key Publication: NAS (National Accounts Statistics)
- Presents estimates on a **financial year** basis (April–March)
- Contains **advance/quick estimates** and **provisional/final estimates**
- Frequently revised — final estimates released with a lag

#### International Framework
- India's national accounts follow the **System of National Accounts (SNA)** — international standard maintained by UN, World Bank, IMF, OECD, and Eurostat.
- India adopted **SNA 1993** methodology; has since moved towards **SNA 2008**

---

### 21.2.2 National Accounts Aggregates and Concepts 🔴

| Concept | Definition |
|---|---|
| **GDP (Gross Domestic Product)** | Total value of goods and services produced within the domestic territory during a year |
| **GNP (Gross National Product)** | GDP + Net Factor Income from Abroad (NFIA) |
| **NDP** | GDP – Depreciation (Consumption of Fixed Capital) |
| **NNP** | GNP – Depreciation |
| **National Income (NI)** | NNP at Factor Cost |

#### Three Ways to Measure GDP

| Approach | Method | Measures |
|---|---|---|
| **Production Approach** | Sum of Gross Value Added (GVA) across all sectors | Output side |
| **Expenditure Approach** | C + I + G + (X – M) = GDP | Demand side |
| **Income Approach** | Sum of factor incomes (wages, profits, rent, interest) | Income side |

> 🔴 **Exam Tip**: GDP at Market Price = GDP at Factor Cost + Net Indirect Taxes
> NNP at Factor Cost = National Income

#### GDP vs GVA
- From 2015, CSO moved to **GVA at basic prices** as the primary headline indicator, with GDP derived by adding taxes and subtracting subsidies on products.

---

### Key CSO Publications on National Accounts 🔴

| Publication | Content |
|---|---|
| **National Accounts Statistics (NAS)** | Annual publication; GDP, GNP, NNP, sector-wise GVA, saving, capital formation |
| **Statement of National Product and Expenditure** | Embedded in NAS, detailed breakdown |
| **Quick Estimates** | Released January 31 of the reference year |
| **Advance Estimates** | Released February 7 of the reference year |
| **CSO Press Releases** | Monthly / quarterly GDP estimates |

---

### Institutional Architecture of National Accounts 🟡

- **CSO** coordinates with:
  - **State Directorates of Economics & Statistics (DES)** — for state-level accounts (GSDP)
  - **RBI** — for financial sector data (saving, investment)
  - **NSSO** — for household consumption and employment surveys
  - **Line Ministries** — for sector-specific data

---

### GDP Estimation by Sector 🔴

Data for different sectors comes from different primary sources:

| Sector | Primary Data Source |
|---|---|
| Agriculture | Directorate of Economics & Statistics (DES), Ministry of Agriculture |
| Industry (Factory) | Annual Survey of Industries (ASI) |
| Industry (Unorganized) | NSSO Unorganized Sector Surveys |
| Services | Administrative data, NSSO surveys |
| Public Administration | Budget documents |
| Finance | Reserve Bank of India (RBI) |
