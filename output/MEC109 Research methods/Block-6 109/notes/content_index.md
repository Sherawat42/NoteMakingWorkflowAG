## High-Yield Topics Summary (🔴 RED — Most Exam-Relevant)

| Topic | Key Points |
|---|---|
| CSO / NAS | Apex statistical organization; types of GDP estimates (Quick, Advance, Final); base year changes |
| Input-Output Table (IOTT) | Compiled by CSO and Planning Commission; shows inter-sectoral flows |
| SDP | Prepared by State DES; income originating approach (NOT income accruing) |
| Saving | GDS = Public + Private Corporate + Household; HH physical saving = residual |
| Investment | GDCF, GDFCF, NDCF; two approaches (flow of funds, commodity flow) |
| Agricultural Census | Quinquennial; operational holdings; DES, Ministry of Agriculture |
| Cost of Cultivation | Feeds into CACP and MSP recommendations |
| ASI | Factory sector; ~30 variables; 3-4 digit NIC code; managed by CSO |
| IIP | Monthly; 6 weeks lag; base 2011-12; use-based classification |
| DGCI&S | Merchandise trade; FOB (exports), CIF (imports); ITC(HS) 8-digit codes |
| DGCI&S vs RBI BOP Divergence | Physical customs tracking vs. payment tracking; 3 reasons for divergence |
| Terms of Trade | GTT = QI ratio; NTT = UVI ratio; ITT = NTT × QI(exports)/100 |
| Fiscal Deficits | GFD → GPD (minus interest) → NFD (minus net lending); RD, PRD |
| Monetary Aggregates | M₀ → M₁ → M₃ → NM₂/NM₃ → L₁/L₂/L₃ |
| Price Indices | WPI (Econ Advisor); CPI-IW & CPI-AL (Labour Bureau); CPI Combined (CSO) |
| Employment Measures (NSSO) | UPS, UPSS, CWS, CDS — UPSS always highest; CDS most sensitive |
| NFHS | 4 rounds (1992, 1998, 2005, 2014); Health and nutrition of women and children |
| Child Malnutrition Indices | Weight-for-Age (underweight), Height-for-Age (stunted), Weight-for-Height (wasted) |
| Vital Rates | SRS by RGI — IMR, CDR, MMR, Birth Rate, Death Rate, Life Expectancy |
