## 23.3 Finance 🔴 HIGH YIELD

### Finance Sector Overview
The Indian finance sector includes:
- **Public Finances**: Central and State Governments
- **Central Bank**: Reserve Bank of India (RBI)
- **Scheduled Commercial Banks (SCBs)**, Cooperative Banks
- **Financial Markets**: SEBI-regulated capital markets
- **Insurance & Pension Funds**: IRDA, PFRDA
- **Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs)**
- **Unorganised sector**: moneylenders, pawn shops, unregistered brokers

## 23.3.1 Public Finances 🔴

### Key Sources for Public Finance Data
| Source | Content |
|---|---|
| **Budget Documents** (Central/State) | Annual budget — receipts, expenditure, deficit |
| **Pre-Budget Economic Survey** | Summary overview of budgetary transactions |
| **RBI Handbook** | Comprehensive time series on Central/State/Combined finances |
| **RBI Monthly Bulletin** | Monthly updates |
| **CSO (NAS)** | Output, GVA, gross savings of the financial sector |
| **EPWRF website** | Time-series on public finances |

### Key Fiscal Deficit Concepts 🔴

| Term | Formula |
|---|---|
| **GFD** (Gross Fiscal Deficit) | Total expenditure (including loans net of recovery) – Revenue receipts (incl. external grants + non-debt capital receipts) |
| **NFD** (Net Fiscal Deficit) | GFD – Net lending of Central Govt |
| **GPD** (Gross Primary Deficit) | GFD – Interest Payments |
| **NPD** (Net Primary Deficit) | GFD – Net Interest Payments |
| **RD** (Revenue Deficit) | Revenue Receipts – Revenue Expenditure |
| **PRD** (Primary Revenue Deficit) | RD – Interest Payments |

> 🔴 **Exam Tip**: GFD is the most commonly cited fiscal deficit indicator. PRD eliminates interest payments to assess the "structural" deficit.

### RBI Handbook Time-Series Data on Central Government Finances
- **Key deficit indicators**: GFD, GPD, NPD, Revenue Deficit, PRD, Net RBI Credit from 1975-76
- **Receipts**: Tax revenue (direct & indirect taxes), non-tax revenue, capital receipts
- **Capital Receipts** breakdown: market borrowings, small savings, provident funds, disinvestment, external loans
- **Expenditure**: Revenue & capital expenditure; defence, interest payments, subsidies; share of economic and social services
- **GFD Financing**: External financing + internal financing (market borrowings, other borrowings, cash balance drawdown)
- **Plan Outlay**: Sectoral profile + financing sources

### State Government Finances
- **Key deficit indicators** for States: Fiscal deficit, revenue deficit, overall deficit
- **Receipts**: Tax receipts (sales tax, state excise), share of Central taxes
- **Capital Receipts**: Loans from Centre, market loans, State provident fund
- **Expenditure**: Developmental and non-developmental (interest, admin, pension)
- **States' GFD Financing**: Loans from Centre, market borrowings, NSSF securities

### Combined Central + State Finances
- Combined deficit, receipts, disbursements
- Market borrowings
- **Ownership of Government Securities** (11 categories: RBI, commercial banks, cooperative banks, primary dealers, insurance cos., FIs, mutual funds, provident funds, corporates, FIIs, others)

## 23.3.1(d) Transactions with the Rest of the World 🔴

India's external transactions (from RBI Handbook 2013-14):

| Data Type | Details |
|---|---|
| **India's BOP** | Current account + Capital account + Errors & Omissions + Monetary movements |
| **Exchange Rates** | Rs vs USD, GBP, DM/€, Japanese Yen (from 1975) |
| **REER & NEER** | 36-country bilateral weights, base 2004-05 |
| **External Assistance** | Loans + grants, authorisation, utilisation, debt service (from 1985-86) |
| **NRI Deposits** | All account types: NR(E)RA, FCNR(A), FCNR(B), NRO, FC(B&O)D, etc. (from 1996) |
| **Foreign Investment** | Gross FDI, repatriation, net FDI, net portfolio (from 2000-01) |
| **Foreign Exchange Reserves** | Total (from 1956-57) |
| **India's External Debt** | Multilateral, bilateral, IMF, trade credit, commercial borrowing, NRI deposits (from 1996) |

### FDI Data 🟡
- Available from **August 1991** onwards.
- **Collected by**: RBI (equity + reinvested earnings + other capital) and **DIPP** (Dept. of Industrial Policy & Promotion, Ministry of Commerce)
- FDI data pre-2000 = equity only; post-2000 includes (a) equity capital, (b) reinvested earnings, (c) other capital → **NOT comparable** with pre-2000 data.
- DIPP publishes monthly FDI inflow data by: investing country, sector, region, timeline.
- **SEBI Handbook**: FII (Foreign Institutional Investor) data

## 23.3.2 Currency, Coinage, Money and Banking 🔴 HIGH YIELD

### Primary Source: RBI Handbook

#### Monetary Aggregates Defined 🔴

| Aggregate | Components |
|---|---|
| **M₀ (Reserve Money)** | Currency in circulation + Other deposits with RBI + Bankers' deposits with RBI |
| **M₁ (Narrow Money)** | Currency with the public + Other deposits with RBI + Demand deposits |
| **M₃ (Broad Money)** | M₁ + Time deposits with banks |
| **NM₂ (New Monetary Aggregate)** | M₁ + Short-term time deposits |
| **NM₃ (New Monetary Aggregate)** | NM₂ + Long-term deposits + Call/term funding from FIs |
| **L₁ (Liquidity Aggregate)** | NM₃ + Postal deposits |
| **L₂** | L₁ + Liabilities of FIs |
| **L₃** | L₂ + Public deposits with NBFCs (compiled quarterly) |

> 🔴 **New Monetary Aggregates (NM₂, NM₃)** and **Liquidity Aggregates (L₁, L₂, L₃)** were introduced in the RBI Bulletin of **November 2000**.

#### Key Monetary Policy Rates
- **Bank Rate**, **Repo Rate (LAF)**, **Reverse Repo Rate**, **MSF Rate**
- **CRR** (Cash Reserve Ratio) and **SLR** (Statutory Liquidity Ratio)

### Price Indices 🔴

| Index | Compiled by |
|---|---|
| WPI (Wholesale Price Index) | Economic Advisor's Office, Ministry of Industry |
| CPI-IW (Consumer Price Index – Industrial Workers) | Labour Bureau, Shimla |
| CPI-AL (Consumer Price Index – Agricultural Labour) | Labour Bureau, Shimla |
| CPI-Rural, CPI-Urban, CPI Combined | CSO (released 12th of each month) |

### RBI Handbook — Banking Time Series Data

- **Liabilities and Assets of RBI** (from 1980): Notes/coins, balances abroad, loans/advances, investments
- **Money Stock** (M₀, M₁, M₃): Monthly composition and sources of change ("C" and "S" components)
- **Gold and Silver Prices**: Average monthly prices in domestic and foreign markets
- **Scheduled Commercial Banks (SCBs)**: Deposits, investments in Govt securities, bank credit (food/non-food), cash balances
- **Non-Food Credit Deployment**: Priority sector, industry groups, wholesale trade, export credit
- **Consolidated SCB Balance Sheets**
- **Gross and Net NPAs (Non-Performing Assets)** by bank groups
- **CRAR (Capital to Risk-Weighted Assets Ratio)**: Minimum 9% from 2000 (was 8% before)
- **Regional Rural Banks (RRBs)**, State Cooperative Banks, PACS data

### Other RBI Publications on Banking 🟡
- **Report on Currency and Finance** (annual)
- **Report on Trends in Banking** (annual) — profitability and operational efficiency of banks
- **RBI Online Database** (http://dbie.rbi.org.in) — searchable, downloadable

## 23.3.2 Financial Markets 🟡

### Key Regulators & Data Sources
| Regulator | Regulates | Key Publication |
|---|---|---|
| **RBI** | Banks, NBFCs, FIs | RBI Handbook |
| **SEBI** | Capital markets | Handbook of Statistics on Indian Securities Market |
| **Department of Company Affairs** | Companies Act | Company accounts data |

### SEBI Handbook Data — Key Series
- **Interest Rate Structure**: Call money rates, PLR of term lending institutions (IDBI), yields on Govt securities
- **Capital Issues**: Number and amount mobilized by non-Govt public Ltd companies
- **MF (Mutual Funds)**: Net resources mobilized by category (banks, FIs, UTI, private)
- **Primary Market**: Public issues, rights issues, equities at par/premium, CCPS, bonds
- **Secondary Market**: Annual/monthly trends in trading at stock exchanges
- **Share Price Indices**: BSE SENSEX (base 1978-79=100), BSE National, NSE indices
- **Market Capitalization**
- **FII Investment**: Gross purchases and sales, net investment (monthly and annual)
- **Liquidity Indicators**: Market cap-GDP ratio (BSE, NSE), turnover ratio, traded value ratio
- **P/E Ratio and P/B Ratio**: BSE SENSEX, BSE 100, S&P CNX NIFTY — monthly averages
- **Survey of Investor Households** (joint SEBI+NCAER): Estimates of investor/non-investor households by type of investment

## ✅ Self-Review
- **Public finances** = 3 levels: Central, State, and Combined. Each has distinct datasets in the RBI Handbook.
- **Deficit hierarchy**: GFD → GPD (subtract interest) → NFD (subtract net lending). Know the order.
- **Monetary aggregates**: M₀ (reserve money) → M₁ (narrow) → M₃ (broad) → NM₂ and NM₃ (new) → L₁, L₂, L₃ (liquidity).
- CRR and SLR are tools of monetary policy. Repo/reverse repo = LAF rates.
- WPI = Industry Ministry; CPI-IW and CPI-AL = Labour Bureau; CPI Combined = CSO.
- SEBI Handbook = detailed capital markets data; RBI Handbook = banking and monetary data.
- FDI data before and after 2000 is NOT comparable due to change in coverage.
