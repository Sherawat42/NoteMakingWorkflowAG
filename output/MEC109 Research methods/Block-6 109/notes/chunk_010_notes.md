## 24.2.2 Quality/Adequacy of Employment 🔴

### Key Dimensions of Employment Quality
1. **Intermittent/casual nature of work** — proportion of workforce in 'casual employment'
2. **Wage adequacy** — comparison of average earnings with statutory minimum wage and poverty line
3. **Employment status of poor household members**
4. **Gender discrimination** — access to employment across sectors, occupations

### Data Sources for Employment Quality

#### NSSO Employment Survey Data (from the tables already described)
- Proportion of workforce by employment status (self-employed, regular salaried, casual)
- Average daily earnings of male and female regular/casual workers by sector
- Pattern of employment by MPCE class (poor vs. non-poor)

#### Factory Sector (Wages):
- **ASI**: Total wages, total emoluments, employee compensation + mandays worked → derives average wage/manday
- **Labour Bureau Reports** (under Ministry of Labour, Shimla):
  - **Occupational Wage Surveys** (Successive Rounds — latest: 6th Round): Distribution of workers by earnings level and occupation for each industry; enables comparison by gender, region, industry
  - **Wage Rates in Rural India** (latest: 2004-05): Rural/unorganised sector wages vs. statutory minimum wages
  - **Report on Working of the Minimum Wages Act, 1948** (latest: 2003)

#### Unorganised Sector Wages:
- **DE, NDE, OAE Surveys** (CSO establishment surveys till early 1990s; now by NSSO)
- **NSSO Unorganised Sector Surveys**: Average annual earnings for men, women, children in establishments
- NSS Report 549 (2010-11): Latest unorganised non-agricultural enterprise survey

#### Child Labour:
- **Census and NSSO** tables on workforce by age group (5–9 and 10–14 years)
- These are dated and may not fully capture child labour due to survey limitations
- **NGOs** are often more effective at identifying and reporting child labour ground-level realities

## 24.2.3 Labour Welfare 🔴

### Labour Bureau, Shimla (Ministry of Labour) — Yearbooks of Labour Statistics

Data compiled on multiple aspects of labour welfare:

#### Industrial Injuries and Accidents
| Data | Source | Limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Fatal and non-fatal injuries in factories | Returns under Factories Act 1948 | Significant non-response |
| Injuries in mines | Returns under Mines Act 1952 (as amended 1983) | Non-response |

Classification of accidents (from 1984):
- **Fatal** accidents
- **Spot Serious** accidents (permanent loss/fracture/injury to sight/hearing)
- **Reportable Serious** accidents (enforced absence ≥72 hours)

#### Compensated Injuries (Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923)
- Data from annual returns from State Govts, Posts & Telegraph, Railway Board
- Covers injuries resulting in **death** or **disability** (permanent/temporary)
- Limitation: NOT all injuries are compensated; many establishments don't submit returns
- ESI-covered establishments compensated under **ESI Act**, not WC Act

#### Industrial Disputes
- Number of (a) disputes, (b) workers involved, (c) mandays lost, (d) cause of disputes:
  - Wages/allowances, Bonus, Personnel matters, Retrenchment, Leave/hours, Indiscipline, Others

#### Trade Unions (Trade Unions Act, 1926)
- Data on registered organizations submitting returns:
  - Workers' organizations: number, membership, income, expenditure, balance of funds
  - Employers' organizations: same
- Limitation: Registration not obligatory; response rate among registered < 50%
- Membership data by NIC 1987 available in Yearbooks

#### Welfare Funds & Social Security
- Coal Mines Welfare Fund, Mica Mines Welfare Fund
- **ESI Corporation (ESIC)** — number of beneficiaries, contributions, benefits
- **Employees Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO)** — members, fund balances

#### Unorganised Sector Welfare (Labour Bureau surveys):
- Working and living conditions of **Scheduled Caste/Tribe workers**
- Living conditions of **unorganised workers**
- **Contract labour** conditions

## 24.3 Education 🔴

### Why Education Data Matters
- Instrument for: empowerment (especially women), distributing benefits of growth, tackling inequalities
- Key policy goals: Universal primary education (MDG), Right to Education Act (2009)

### Primary Agencies for Educational Data
| Agency | Acronym | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Department of School Education | MHRD | School education statistics |
| Department of Higher Education | MHRD | University and higher education |
| National University of Educational Planning & Administration | NUEPA | DISE database (school-level) |
| National Council for Educational Research & Training | NCERT | All India Educational Survey (AIES) |
| University Grants Commission | UGC | University data |
| Institute of Applied Manpower Research | IAMR | Technical manpower (NTMIS) |

### Indian Standard Classification of Education (InSCED)
- Developed by MHRD in **2014** — India-specific adaptation of International ISCED classification
- 16 broad levels (A through O and X):
  - A–E: School Education
  - F–L: Higher Education
  - M, N, O: Certificate Courses, In-service training, Adult Education

---

### 24.3.1 Educational Infrastructure 🔴

Key data available from MHRD/UGC/NCERT/Labour Bureau (DGE&T):

| Data | Source |
|---|---|
| Literacy rates and progress of literacy campaigns | MHRD |
| Number of institutions (colleges, universities, schools — by type) | MHRD & UGC |
| Distribution of colleges: Arts, Science, Commerce, Law, Agriculture, Engineering, Medicine | MHRD & UGC |
| Teacher demographics, training status, attrition | All India Educational Survey (AIES) |
| College management patterns, recognition, availability of teachers and facilities | UGC |
| School management (Govt/local bodies/private/minority), medium of instruction, building type, facilities (water, sanitation — separate for girls) | Sixth AIES |
| Teachers in technical/professional institutions | MHRD & UGC |
| ITIs (Industrial Training Institutions), Advanced Training Institutes, Apprenticeship capacity | MoLE & MHRD |
| Vocational Rehabilitation Centres (VRCs) for physically handicapped | DGE&T |
| Expenditure on education: Sarva Shiksha Abhiyan, Total Literacy Campaign, Adult Education | MHRD |

### 24.3.2 Infrastructure Utilization & Access to Educational Opportunities 🔴

| Data | Source |
|---|---|
| Literacy rates (overall, age-specific, SC/ST, adult) | Census, NSSO, MHRD |
| Number of students (including female) in all educational institutions | MHRD |
| Male/female student enrollment by stage and course | MHRD |
| Working children (age 5–9, 10–14) as a % of that population vs. school enrollment | Census, NSSO |
| Enrollment in university-level programs by stage and faculty | MHRD & UGC |
| SC/ST and female enrollment at university level | MHRD & UGC |
| Teacher-pupil ratios at different levels | MHRD |
| Dropout rates at different stages | MHRD |
| Access to educational facilities in rural habitations (by distance) | Sixth AIES |
| Availability of special institutions for disabled children | Sixth AIES |
| Technical manpower utilization patterns and employment waiting time | IAMR (NTMIS, Tracer Studies) |
| Stocks of educated manpower by level, SC/ST, religious communities | NSSO, Census |

### DISE (District Information System of Education)
- Developed by **NUEPA** (accessible at www.dise.in)
- Records data from all schools and junior colleges in all **districts**
- Analytical reports: school facility indicators, enrollment indicators, teacher indicators, EDI (Education Development Index)
- Available from **2000-02 onwards**

### NSSO Education Surveys
- Conducted in 52nd Round (1995-96), 64th Round (2007-08), 71st Round (2014)
- Provide data on: literacy, educational composition of population, school attendance

### National Human Development Reports
- **NHDR 2001** (Planning Commission) and **State HDRs**
- Important for: synthesis of education, health, and other social sector data into HDI and GDI
- Available on the Planning Commission (now NitiAyog) website

## Key Points on Tracer Studies 🟡

- Follow up specific **cohorts** (batches) of graduates from institutions
- Track: employment absorption rates, waiting time for first employment, salary levels
- Conducted by **IAMR** through NTMIS (21 nodal centres across States)
- Also called **Cohort Studies**

## ✅ Self-Review
- **Labour Bureau (Ministry of Labour, Shimla)** = key source for wages, injuries, disputes, trade unions in the organised sector.
- MHRD publications are the primary source for school and higher education statistics.
- NUEPA's DISE = district-level school database (down to individual school level).
- Census = most disaggregated (village/ward) education and employment data.
- Know the distinction between **infrastructure data** (number of schools, teachers) and **access data** (enrollment, dropout, literacy rates).
