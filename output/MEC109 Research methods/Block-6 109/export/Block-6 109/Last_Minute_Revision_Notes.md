### ⚠️ Critical Gap: Displacement Data
> *"There is no data worth the name on people displaced from their livelihoods by development projects and the number among these who have been resettled in alternative livelihood."*
- A **growing consensus** that the burden of development costs falls mostly on the poor and voiceless.
- Urgent need for a **transparent database** on displaces persons and their resettlement.
