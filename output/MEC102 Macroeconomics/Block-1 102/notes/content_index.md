## Key Terms Glossary

| Term | Definition | First Appears |
|------|-----------|---------------|
| Aggregative analysis | Macroeconomics studies economy-wide aggregates; behaviour at aggregate ≠ scaled-up individual | Chunk 001 |
| School of macroeconomic thought | Group of thinkers sharing similar views on the behaviour of macroeconomic variables | Chunk 001 |
| Classical economics | Pre-Keynesian school holding that flexible wages/prices/interest enable markets to self-clear at full employment | Chunk 001 |
| Keynesian economics | School holding wages/prices are sticky; output can be below potential; activist fiscal/monetary policy | Chunk 001 |
| Stagflation | Simultaneous economic stagnation and inflation (1970s) | Chunk 001 |
| New Classical economics | Post-1970s revival adding rational expectations and micro-foundations; perfect competition | Chunk 001 |
| New Keynesian economics | Sticky prices + imperfect competition + rational expectations + micro-foundations | Chunk 001 |
| Mercantilism | Wealth = stock of precious metals; pre-classical doctrine displaced by Smith | Chunk 002 |
| Laissez-faire | Minimum state intervention; markets allocate efficiently on their own | Chunk 002 |
| Invisible hand | Self-interested individuals coordinated by price system to social welfare | Chunk 002 |
| Market clearing | Flexible prices/wages adjust until S = D in every market | Chunk 002 |
| Production function (Y = F(K,L)) | Output as function of capital (fixed in SR) and labour | Chunk 002 |
| Real wage (W/P) | Nominal wage deflated by price level — relevant for labour supply/demand | Chunk 002 |
| MPN | Marginal product of labour; firms hire until W/P = MPN | Chunk 002 |
| Quantity Theory of Money (MV=PY) | Money supply × velocity = price × output; classical price determination | Chunk 002 |
| Fisher equation | Identity MV = PT or MV = PY | Chunk 002 |
| Velocity of money | Average number of times a unit of money changes hands per period | Chunk 002 |
| Say's Law | "Supply creates its own demand" — production generates equivalent income/demand | Chunk 003 |
| Neutrality of Money | Changes in M affect only nominal variables, not real output/employment (classical LR) | Chunk 003 |
| Classical Dichotomy | Real and monetary sectors are independent; relative prices determined in real sector, P level by M | Chunk 003 |
| Saving-Investment Equilibrium | S = I via interest rate adjustment in the loanable funds market | Chunk 003 |
| Consumption function | C = a + bY (or C = a + b(Y−T)); a = autonomous, b = MPC | Chunk 004 |
| MPC (b) | Marginal propensity to consume = ΔC/ΔY, 0 < b < 1 | Chunk 004 |
| APC | Average propensity to consume = C/Y | Chunk 004 |
| Investment function | I = I̅ − ei; investment falls with interest rate | Chunk 004 |
| Aggregate Expenditure (AE) | C + I + G (+ NX); planned spending in the economy | Chunk 004 |
| Equilibrium output | Y = AE; intersection of 45° line and AE curve | Chunk 004 |
| Investment multiplier | k = 1/(1−b) = ΔY/ΔI; effect of autonomous investment on Y | Chunk 004 |
| Balanced budget multiplier | =1; equal rise in G and T raises Y by ΔG | Chunk 005 |
| Paradox of thrift | Higher savings desire reduces aggregate income, lowering actual savings | Chunk 005 |
| Transactions demand | Money held to pay for everyday transactions; depends on Y | Chunk 005 |
| Precautionary demand | Money held against unforeseen needs; depends on Y | Chunk 005 |
| Speculative demand | Money held as asset; inversely related to interest rate | Chunk 005 |
| Liquidity preference | Total demand for money = transactions + precautionary + speculative | Chunk 005 |
| Liquidity trap | Money demand becomes infinitely interest-elastic at very low rates; monetary policy ineffective | Chunk 006 |
| Crowding out | Government borrowing raises i, reducing private I (under flexible price setting) | Chunk 006 |
| IS curve | Locus of (Y, i) where real-sector (S=I) equilibrium holds; downward sloping | Chunk 006 |
| LM curve | Locus of (Y, i) where money-market equilibrium holds; upward sloping | Chunk 007 (full derivation) |
| IS-LM equilibrium | Simultaneous intersection determines unique (Y, i) for given P | Chunk 007 |
| AD curve | Locus of (Y, P) where IS-LM holds — downward in (Y, P) space | Chunk 007 |
| Aggregate Supply (AS) curve | Y as function of P; vertical (classical) or upward-sloping (Keynesian) | Chunk 007 |
| National income identity (open) | Y = C + I + G + NX where NX = X − M | Chunk 008 |
| Balance of Payments (BOP) | Systematic record of all transactions between residents and rest of world | Chunk 008 |
| Current account | Trade in goods/services + primary income + secondary income (transfers) | Chunk 008 |
| Capital account | Capital transfers and acquisition/disposal of non-produced non-financial assets | Chunk 008 |
| Financial account | Net acquisition of financial assets — FDI, FPI, other investment, reserve assets | Chunk 008 |
| Nominal Exchange Rate (NER) | Price of foreign currency in domestic currency (or vice versa) | Chunk 009 |
| Real Exchange Rate (RER) | RER = NER × (P*/P); relative price of foreign basket in domestic basket | Chunk 009 |
| NEER | Nominal Effective Exchange Rate — weighted index of bilateral NERs | Chunk 009 |
| REER | Real Effective Exchange Rate — NEER adjusted for relative inflation | Chunk 009 |
| Fixed exchange rate | Central bank pegs ER and intervenes via reserves to defend it | Chunk 009 |
| Flexible (floating) exchange rate | ER determined by demand and supply in FX market | Chunk 009 |
| Managed float | Hybrid: free floating with occasional CB intervention | Chunk 009 |
| IS* curve (open economy) | Goods-market locus (Y, e) under given i = i*; flexible ER context | Chunk 009 |
| LM* curve (open economy) | Money-market locus given i = i* — vertical in (Y, e) | Chunk 009 |
| Mundell-Fleming model | Open-economy IS-LM with perfect capital mobility; effects of policy depend on ER regime | Chunk 010 |
| Perfect capital mobility | Domestic interest rate = world rate (i = i*) by arbitrage | Chunk 010 |
| Policy effectiveness rule | Fiscal policy effective under FIXED ER; Monetary policy effective under FLEXIBLE ER | Chunk 010 |
| Policy trilemma (impossible trinity) | Cannot simultaneously have fixed ER, free capital mobility, and independent monetary policy | Chunk 010 |
