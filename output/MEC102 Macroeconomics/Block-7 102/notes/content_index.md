## Key Terms Glossary
| Term | Definition | First Appears |
|------|-----------|---------------|
| Fiscal policy | Government's use of taxes, transfers and expenditure to manage aggregate demand | Chunk 001 |
| Government Budget Balance (BB) | BB = NT − G = (tY − G) | Chunk 001 |
| Budget function | Plot of BB at different income levels for a given fiscal programme | Chunk 001 |
| Expansionary fiscal policy | ↑G or ↓T to cure recession | Chunk 001 |
| Contractionary fiscal policy | ↓G or ↑T to control inflation | Chunk 001 |
| Discretionary fiscal policy | Deliberate change in G and T | Chunk 001 |
| Recessionary / Deflationary gap | Shortfall of Y below full-employment Y_f | Chunk 001 |
| Inflationary gap | Excess of Y above Y_f | Chunk 001 |
| Inside lag | Time between shock and policy action | Chunk 001 |
| Outside / Response lag | Time between policy action and its impact | Chunk 001 |
| Recognition lag | Delay before policymakers detect a slump or boom | Chunk 001 |
| Implementation lag | Delay between recognising the problem and enacting policy | Chunk 001 |
| Automatic / Built-in stabilisers | Built-in tax and expenditure mechanisms that damp the cycle without deliberate action | Chunk 002 |
| Structural budget balance (SBB) | Budget balance evaluated at potential output Y_f | Chunk 002 |
| Fiscal sustainability | Government's ability to satisfy lifetime budget constraint without default | Chunk 002 |
| Government budget constraint | Identity: B_t = (1 + r)B_{t-1} − X_t | Chunk 002 |
| Primary balance | X_t = T_t − G_t (budget balance excluding interest) | Chunk 002 |
| Debt stabilisation | Keeping B constant via primary surplus = r | Chunk 002 |
| Debt-GDP ratio | B_t / Y_t | Chunk 002 |
| Debt-dynamics equation | Δ(B/Y) = (r − g)(B/Y)_{t-1} + (G − T)/Y | Chunk 002 |
| Interest-growth differential | r − g | Chunk 002 |
| Debt explosion | Self-reinforcing cycle of rising r → rising required surplus → recession → still higher (r − g) | Chunk 003 |
| Default | Failure to repay principal and accrued interest | Chunk 003 |
| Haircut | Percentage reduction creditors accept on their claim | Chunk 003 |
| Capital flight | Abrupt decline in demand for a country's assets in world financial markets | Chunk 003 |
| Debt monetisation / Money finance | Government bond issuance financed by central-bank money creation | Chunk 003 |
| Seignorage | Real revenue from money creation: ΔH/P = (ΔH/H)(H/P) | Chunk 003 |
| Hyperinflation | Self-defeating spiral of money-financing | Chunk 003 |
| Natural level of output (Y_n) | Output level the economy returns to in the medium run | Chunk 003 |
