# Running Context

## Key Concepts Introduced
- **Demand Management** (Chunk 001): Use of taxes, transfers and G to influence aggregate demand.
- **Government Budget & Budget Balance** (Chunk 001): BB = NT − G; balanced / surplus / deficit.
- **Expenditure (Keynesian) Multiplier** (Chunk 001): Net tax rate t affects multiplier; ΔY/ΔG > 1.
- **Discretionary Fiscal Policy** (Chunk 001): Deliberate change in G and T.
- **Recessionary / Deflationary Gap** (Chunk 001): Equilibrium Y below full-employment Y_f.
- **Inflationary Gap** (Chunk 001): Equilibrium Y above Y_f, generates rising prices.
- **Time Lags — Inside, Outside, Recognition, Implementation, Response** (Chunk 001): Delays plaguing discretionary policy.
- **Automatic / Built-in Stabilisers** (Chunk 002): Tax & transfer mechanisms that damp the cycle without deliberate action.
- **Structural Budget Balance (SBB)** (Chunk 002): Budget balance evaluated at potential output Y_f.
- **Fiscal Sustainability** (Chunk 002): Government's ability to satisfy lifetime budget constraint without default.
- **Government Budget Constraint** (Chunk 002): Identity linking ΔB to existing B (interest) and current G − T.
- **Primary Balance / Primary Surplus** (Chunk 002): X_t = T_t − G_t (excludes interest).
- **Debt Stabilisation** (Chunk 002): Keeping B constant via primary surplus = r.
- **Debt-GDP Ratio Dynamics** (Chunk 002): Δ(B/Y) = (r − g)(B/Y) + primary deficit / Y.
- **Interest-Growth Differential (r − g)** (Chunk 002): Key driver of debt-ratio path.
- **Debt Explosion / Vicious Cycle** (Chunk 003): ↑r → ↑surplus required → recession → ↑(r − g) → spiral.
- **Debt Default & Haircut** (Chunk 003): Partial non-repayment; creditors take percentage cut.
- **Capital Flight** (Chunk 003): Abrupt withdrawal of foreign demand for a country's assets.
- **Debt Monetisation / Money Finance** (Chunk 003): CB-financed deficit; revenue = seignorage.
- **Seignorage** (Chunk 003): ΔH/P = (ΔH/H)(H/P).
- **Hyperinflation** (Chunk 003): Self-defeating spiral of money-financing.
- **Effects of Deficit Reduction (AS-AD + IS-LM)** (Chunk 003): Short-run ↓Y, ↓P, ↓i; medium-run Y → Y_n, ↓i, ↑I.

## Definitions (⭐ exam-important)
- **Fiscal Policy** (Chunk 001): Government's use of taxes, transfers and expenditure to manage AD.
- **Government Budget Balance (BB)** (Chunk 001): BB = NT − G = (tY − G).
- **Discretionary Fiscal Policy** (Chunk 001): Deliberate change in G and T to influence Y and prices.
- **Recessionary / Deflationary Gap** (Chunk 001): Shortfall of Y below Y_f.
- **Inside Lag** (Chunk 001): Time between shock and policy action.
- **Outside Lag / Response Lag** (Chunk 001): Time between policy action and effect on economy.
- **Non-Discretionary / Automatic Stabiliser** (Chunk 002): Built-in mechanism that automatically increases AD in recession and reduces it in inflation.
- **Fiscal Sustainability** (Chunk 002): Government's ability to meet current and future obligations without default.
- **Government Budget Constraint** (Chunk 002): Identity linking ΔB to existing B and current G − T.
- **Primary Balance** (Chunk 002): X_t = T_t − G_t.
- **Haircut** (Chunk 003): Percentage reduction creditors accept on their claim during a partial default.
- **Capital Flight** (Chunk 003): Abrupt decline in demand for a country's assets in world financial markets.
- **Debt Monetisation** (Chunk 003): Government bond issuance financed by central-bank money creation.
- **Seignorage** (Chunk 003): Real revenue from money creation = ΔH/P.

## Key Data & Numbers
- Approx 1 year (Chunk 001): Time for a tax/spending change to have its full effect.
- Surplus to repay debt at year t (Chunk 002): (1 + r)^{t-1}.
- Surplus to stabilise debt (Chunk 002): r per year, forever.
- High-debt example (Chunk 003): r = 3%, g = 2%, B/Y = 100% → primary surplus 1% stabilises debt.
- Crisis example (Chunk 003): r jumps to 8% → required primary surplus = 6% of GDP.
- Ireland 2007–10 (Chunk 003): debt-GDP 25% → ~100%; deficit 0% → >30% of GDP.
- Greek-German yield spread (Chunk 003): +33 percentage points by January 2012.
- EU + IMF Greek aid since 2010 (Chunk 003): >€1 trillion in loan packages; ECB injected up to €1 trillion of liquidity.
- Venezuela hyperinflation (Chunk 003): up to ~300,000% per year.
