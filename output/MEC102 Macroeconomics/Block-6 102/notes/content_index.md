## Key Terms Glossary

| Term | Definition | First Appears |
|------|-----------|---------------|
| Barter system | Trading goods/services directly without money | Chunk 001 |
| Double coincidence of wants | Both parties must want what the other offers | Chunk 001 |
| Fiat money | Money with no intrinsic value, not redeemable for any commodity | Chunk 001 |
| Commodity money | Money whose material has intrinsic value | Chunk 001 |
| M0 / Reserve money / High-powered money | Currency in circulation + bankers' deposits with RBI + 'other deposits' with RBI | Chunk 001 |
| M1 / Narrow money | Currency with public + demand deposits + 'other deposits' with RBI | Chunk 001 |
| M3 / Broad money | M1 + time deposits with banking system | Chunk 001 |
| NM2 | NM1 + short-term time deposits of residents (≤ 1 year) | Chunk 001 |
| NM3 | NM2 + long-term time deposits + call/term funding from FIs | Chunk 001 |
| CRR (Cash Reserve Ratio) | % of deposits banks must keep as cash with central bank | Chunk 001 |
| SLR (Statutory Liquidity Ratio) | % of deposits banks must keep as cash, gold, or approved securities | Chunk 001 |
| Fractional reserve system | System where banks hold a fraction of deposits and lend the rest | Chunk 001 |
| Money multiplier (m) | M1/M0; multiple by which monetary base expands into broader money. Formula: m = (cd+1)/(cd+rd) | Chunk 001 |
| Currency–deposit ratio (cd) | C/D; chosen by non-bank public | Chunk 001 |
| Reserve ratio (rd) | R/D; set by central bank + banks' excess reserves | Chunk 001 |
| Currency–money ratio (cm) | C/M1; alternative parameter for the multiplier | Chunk 001 |
| Open Market Operations (OMO) | Central bank buying/selling government securities to alter monetary base | Chunk 001 |
| Expansionary monetary policy | Lowers rates / increases money supply | Chunk 001 |
| Contractionary monetary policy | Raises rates / decreases money supply | Chunk 001 |
| Repo Rate | Rate at which RBI lends to banks against securities (with repurchase) | Chunk 002 |
| Reverse Repo Rate | Rate at which banks park excess cash with RBI | Chunk 002 |
| Bank Rate / Discount Rate | Rate at which RBI lends to banks without collateral or repurchase | Chunk 002 |
| Overnight Rate | Inter-bank borrowing rate | Chunk 002 |
| Sterilisation | OMO used to neutralise the effect of forex intervention on money supply | Chunk 002 |
| Money neutrality | Doctrine that money does not affect real variables | Chunk 002 |
| Policy ineffectiveness proposition | New-Classical claim that systematic policy is ineffective | Chunk 002 |
| Quantitative credit control | Non-discriminatory monetary instruments (Bank Rate, OMO, CRR, SLR) | Chunk 003 |
| Qualitative credit control | Discriminatory instruments (margin requirements, credit rationing, direct action) | Chunk 003 |
| Margin requirement | Margin between collateral value and loan amount | Chunk 003 |
| Credit rationing | Deliberate restriction of credit volume by central bank | Chunk 003 |
| Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF) | RBI framework where repo/reverse repo are primary policy rates (ILAF 1999, RLAF 2011) | Chunk 003 |
| Monetary Policy Framework Agreement (MPFA) | 2016 GOI–RBI agreement formalising inflation targeting | Chunk 003 |
| Transmission Mechanism | Channels through which monetary policy affects economic variables | Chunk 003 |
| Tobin's q | Ratio of market value of firm's shares to replacement cost of physical assets | Chunk 003 |
| Asymmetric information | Lender/borrower information mismatch (basis of adverse selection, moral hazard) | Chunk 003 |
| Loss function (central bank) | L = x² + (y - y'')²; welfare cost rising with inflation and deviation from output target | Chunk 004 |
| Natural output (y') | Output produced when labour force is fully employed | Chunk 004 |
| Bliss point B | Zero inflation + output at target — minimum welfare cost | Chunk 004 |
| Dynamic inconsistency | Having announced a rule, the bank finds it optimal to violate it | Chunk 004 |
| Cheating outcome | Violating an announced rule to exploit anchored expectations | Chunk 004 |
| Delegation to conservative central banker | Independent banker with α > 1 in loss function (Rogoff) | Chunk 004 |
| Taylor's Rule | r = r' + a(x - x') + b(y - y') | Chunk 004 |
| Neutral rate of interest | r' = c/d; rate at which output gap = 0 | Chunk 004 |
| Accelerationist Phillips Curve | Δx = e(y - y'); adaptive-expectations form of PC | Chunk 004 |
| Nominal anchor | Monetary policy's role of providing a credible inflation target | Chunk 004 |
| Crowding out (consensus version) | Expansionary fiscal policy raises neutral rate, displacing private spending | Chunk 005 |
| Path dependence (hysteresis) | Long-run equilibrium depends on path; natural output depends on actual output | Chunk 005 |
| Knife-edge property of natural unemployment | Friedman's claim that any deviation triggers rapid acceleration/deceleration | Chunk 005 |
| Learning-by-doing | Productivity improvement from cumulative production experience | Chunk 005 |
