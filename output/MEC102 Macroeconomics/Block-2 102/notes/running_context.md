# Running Context

## Key Concepts Introduced
- **Inflation–Unemployment relationship** (Chunk 001): classical dichotomy vs. Keynesian linkage.
- **Three types of unemployment** (Chunk 001): frictional, structural, cyclical.
- **Labour Force Participation Rate (LFPR)** (Chunk 001): labour-force ÷ population aged 16–64.
- **Phillips Curve** (Chunk 001): inverse u–π relation; convex to origin.
- **Natural Rate of Unemployment ($u^*$)** (Chunk 001): frictional + structural; floor of unemployment.
- **Stagflation** (Chunk 001): high inflation + high unemployment; oil crises 1973/1979.
- **Expectation-Augmented Phillips Curve** (Chunk 001): SRPC shifts when expectations change.
- **Adaptive vs. Rational expectations** (Chunk 001): gradual past-based updating vs. full-information forward looking.
- **NAIRU** (Chunk 001): unemployment rate at which inflation is stable.
- **Short-Run vs. Long-Run Phillips Curve** (Chunk 001): SRPC downward sloping; LRPC vertical at $u^*$.
- **AS-AD Model & non-neutrality of money** (Chunk 002): short-run output deviation when $P \neq P^e$.
- **Fooling models** (Chunk 002): workers fooled by ↑P, firms not — asymmetric information.
- **Wage equation & price-setting equation** (Chunk 002): $W = P^e f(u, z)$, $P = (1+m)W$.
- **Lucas Critique** (Chunk 002): past statistical relationships break when policy regime changes.
- **Perfect Foresight** (Chunk 002): special case with $P^e = P$ exactly; money fully neutral.
- **Stochastic true-model RE** (Chunk 003): $Y_t = \alpha + \beta X_{t-1} + \gamma Y_{t-1} + \eta Z_{t-1} + \nu_t$.
- **Microfoundations** (Chunk 003): macro relations built from optimizing micro decisions.
- **Lucas Supply Function** (Chunk 003): $y_t - y_p = f(p_t - E_{t-1}p_t)$; price surprise drives output.
- **Signal extraction problem** (Chunk 003): distinguish relative vs. aggregate price changes.
- **Local vs. global information** (Chunk 003): agents know own prices better than aggregate prices.
- **Policy Ineffectiveness Theorem** (Chunk 003): only unanticipated money moves output (Sargent–Wallace).

## Definitions (⭐ exam-important)
- **Inflation** (Chunk 001): persistent rise in the general level of prices.
- **Involuntary Unemployment** (Chunk 001): willing-to-work but cannot find work at prevailing wage.
- **Labour Force** (Chunk 001): persons aged 16–64 willing to work; employed + unemployed.
- **LFPR** (Chunk 001): labour-force / population aged 16–64.
- **Phillips Curve** (Chunk 001): empirical inverse u–π relationship.
- **Natural Rate of Unemployment** (Chunk 001): frictional + structural; Friedman & Phelps.
- **NAIRU** (Chunk 001): rate at which inflation neither accelerates nor decelerates.
- **Adaptive Expectations** (Chunk 001): updated from past observed values.
- **Rational Expectations** (Chunk 001): all info + true model; errors random, mean zero.
- **Long-Run Phillips Curve (LRPC)** (Chunk 001): vertical line at $u^*$.
- **AD function** (Chunk 002): $Y = f(G, T, M/P)$.
- **AS function** (Chunk 002): $P = P^e(1+m)f(u, z)$.
- **Lucas Critique** (Chunk 002): policy-regime invariance fails.
- **Perfect Foresight** (Chunk 002): $P^e = P$ exactly; money neutral.
- **REH (formal)** (Chunk 002): expectations from true data-generating process.
- **Rational expectational error** (Chunk 003): $\nu_t = Y_t - E_{t-1}Y_t$, mean 0, variance $\sigma^2$.
- **Lucas Supply Function** (Chunk 003): $y_t - y_p = f(p_t - E_{t-1}p_t)$.
- **Signal extraction problem** (Chunk 003): relative vs. aggregate price decomposition.
- **Policy Ineffectiveness Theorem** (Chunk 003): only unanticipated money moves output (Sargent–Wallace 1976).
- **Money supply rule** (Chunk 003): $M_t = \alpha y_{t-1} + \epsilon_t$, $E(\epsilon_t \mid I_{t-1}) = 0$.
