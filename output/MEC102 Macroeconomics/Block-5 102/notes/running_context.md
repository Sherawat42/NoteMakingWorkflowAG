## Key Data & Numbers
- **Romer's elasticity example** (Chunk 001): demand elasticity = 5, labour-supply elasticity = 0.1, output falls 3% → re-pricing gain ≈ ¼ of revenue
- **US average job tenure** (Chunk 002): ~10 years
- **Worked u\* example** (Chunk 003): δ=0.05, (F/G)·k=0.95 → u*=5%; ↑δ to 0.10 → u*≈9.5%
- **US 1990s benefits restructuring** (Chunk 004): benefits made harder to obtain, lowered search-unemployment rate
- **EU vs US unemployment gap** (Chunk 004): partly explained by more generous EU benefits
