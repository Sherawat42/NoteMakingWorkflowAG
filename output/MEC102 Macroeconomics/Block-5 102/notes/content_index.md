## Key Terms Glossary

| Term | Definition | First Appears |
|------|-----------|---------------|
| Nominal rigidity | Money prices/wages don't change when conditions call for it | Chunk 001 |
| Real rigidity | Goods/labour/credit market imperfections preventing real wages and relative prices from adjusting | Chunk 001 |
| Menu costs | Costs of changing prices (printing, reprogramming, customer-irritation) | Chunk 001 |
| Mark-up | Price minus marginal cost in imperfect competition | Chunk 001 |
| Countercyclical mark-up | Mark-up falls in booms, rises in recessions | Chunk 001 |
| Thick-market effects | Higher activity reduces information costs, increasing competition | Chunk 001 |
| Asymmetric information (credit) | Borrowers know more about project quality than lenders | Chunk 001 |
| Credit channel | Monetary policy transmission via loan availability, not interest rate | Chunk 001 |
| Credit rationing | Quantity restriction of credit at prevailing rate | Chunk 001 |
| Magnification of shocks | Credit imperfections amplify real disturbances | Chunk 001 |
| Pro-cyclical real wages | Real wages mildly rise in booms, fall in recessions (empirical fact) | Chunk 002 |
| Efficiency wage | Voluntarily-paid above-market real wage to raise efficiency | Chunk 002 |
| Reservation wage | Minimum wage at which a worker will supply labour | Chunk 002 |
| Effort function | e = e(w), per-worker effort as increasing function of real wage | Chunk 002 |
| Solow condition | w·e'(w)/e(w) = 1 — elasticity of effort w.r.t. wage = 1 at optimum | Chunk 002 |
| Per-unit cost of effective labour | w/e(w), minimised at the optimum efficiency wage | Chunk 002 |
| Extended effort function | e(w, w', u), captures long-run dynamics with alternative wages and unemployment | Chunk 002 |
| Anti-shirking logic | High wage deters shirking when monitoring is imperfect | Chunk 002 |
| Fixed-wage contract | Pre-specified wage, employment chosen by firm — Pareto inefficient | Chunk 002 |
| Implicit contract | Wage and employment specified for each state of economy — Pareto efficient | Chunk 002 |
| Insiders | Currently-employed workers, with bargaining power | Chunk 002 |
| Outsiders | Unemployed workers, without bargaining power | Chunk 002 |
| Pareto efficiency | Cannot make one party better off without harming the other | Chunk 002 |
| Walrasian labour market | Perfectly competitive labour market that always clears via wage adjustment | Chunk 003 |
| Voluntary unemployment | Unemployment of those unwilling to work at the going wage rate | Chunk 003 |
| Involuntary unemployment | Unemployment of those willing to work at going wage but unable to find work | Chunk 003 |
| Search and matching model | Model with heterogeneous workers/jobs paired through costly search-and-match | Chunk 003 |
| Frictional unemployment | Unemployment of workers moving between jobs | Chunk 003 |
| Separation rate δ | Fraction of employed workers laid off each period | Chunk 003 |
| Marginal value of a worker q_t | Expected present value of marginal products over the worker's tenure | Chunk 003 |
| Bargaining share ξ | Fraction of the match surplus going to the worker | Chunk 003 |
| Natural rate of unemployment u* | u* = δ / {δ + (F/G)·k}; equilibrium unemployment with constant productivity | Chunk 003 |
| Matching difficulty G | Parameter in cost-of-hiring function; higher G = harder to locate workers | Chunk 003 |
| Hiring externality | A firm's hiring lowers aggregate u, raising MC of hiring for other firms | Chunk 003 |
| Bargaining wedge | Difference between social and private benefit of hiring (worker share ξ) | Chunk 003 |
| Sectoral shock | A change in relative productivity or relative demand across sectors | Chunk 003 |
| Inter-period mobility | Lilien's setup: labour can move between sectors across periods but not within | Chunk 003 |
| Search unemployment | Unemployment from quitting to find a better job or rejecting offers | Chunk 004 |
