## Key Data & Numbers
- Kuznets (1940s): aggregate APC roughly constant in long-run time-series data.
- Quadratic utility example: $u(C_t) = C_t - (a/2)C_t^2$.
- Population growth rate: $n$ (exogenous, RCK and OLG models).
- Subjective discount rate: $\rho > 0$.
- Modified golden rule: $f'(k^*) = \rho + n$.
- Golden rule: $f'(k_g) = n$.
- Cobb-Douglas/log utility example (OLG): $\alpha = 1/4$, $n = 1$ → dynamically inefficient for sufficiently large $\beta$.
- OLG savings function (log utility, CD): $s_t = \beta w_t/(1+\beta)$.
