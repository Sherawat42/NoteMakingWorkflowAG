## Definitions (⭐ exam-important)

- **Direct Finance** ⭐ (Chunk 001): Lender provides funds directly to borrower via markets (bonds, IPOs)

- **Credit (Default) Risk** ⭐ (Chunk 001): Risk that deposits/credit not repaid in part or full
- **Liquidity Risk** ⭐ (Chunk 001): Inability to meet sudden withdrawals or liability payments

- **Exchange Rate Risk** ⭐ (Chunk 001): FX volatility hitting foreign-currency assets/liabilities
- **Asymmetric Information** ⭐ (Chunk 001): One party has more info than the other
- **Adverse Selection** ⭐ (Chunk 001): Pre-transaction; risky borrowers self-select in
- **Moral Hazard** ⭐ (Chunk 001): Post-transaction; reckless behaviour with another's funds
- **Financial Crisis** ⭐ (Chunk 001): Upset in financial markets — wide asset-price fluctuations, credit-flow disruptions
- **Asset-Price Bubble** ⭐ (Chunk 001): Asset prices far above fundamental values
- **Credit Boom / Bubble** ⭐ (Chunk 001): Rapid credit expansion intensifying asset bubble
- **Leveraging** ⭐ (Chunk 001): Banks borrow from other institutions to expand lending capacity
- **Fire Sale** ⭐ (Chunk 001): Distressed asset selling at depressed prices, accelerating decline
- **Debt Deflation** ⭐ (Chunk 001): Real debt burden rises when prices fall
- **Insolvency** (Chunk 001): Assets fall below liabilities

## Chunk 008 — Trade Balance, Real Exchange Rate, Measures, Unit 28 Summary

### Key Concepts Introduced (Chunk 008)
- **Small Open Economy** (Chunk 008): takes world interest rate r* as given
- **S̄ − I(r*) = NX** (Chunk 008): net exports as saving–investment gap at world rate
- **Trade Surplus / Deficit determination** (Chunk 008): r* > r → surplus; r* < r → deficit; r* = r → zero TB
- **Real Exchange Rate α = e × θ** (Chunk 008): nominal rate × domestic/foreign price ratio
- **NX = f(θ) — negative function** (Chunk 008): real ER ↑ → imports ↑, exports ↓
- **Inflation–Interest Rate–FX nexus** (Chunk 008): higher rates → currency appreciates; high inflation → depreciation
- **9 Measures for FX Stability** (Chunk 008): CB intervention, fixed ER, managed float, fiscal/monetary, reforms, ER bands, transparency, reserves, international cooperation

### Definitions ⭐ (Chunk 008)
- **Small Open Economy** ⭐ (Chunk 008): Cannot influence world prices/interest rate
- **Real Exchange Rate (α)** ⭐ (Chunk 008): α = e × θ
- **Trade Surplus** ⭐ (Chunk 008): NX > 0; arises when r* > r
- **Trade Deficit** ⭐ (Chunk 008): NX < 0; arises when r* < r

### Named Models / Theories (Chunk 008)
