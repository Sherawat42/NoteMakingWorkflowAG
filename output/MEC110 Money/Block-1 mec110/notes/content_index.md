## Key Terms Glossary

| Term | Definition | First Appears |
|------|-----------|---------------|
| Economic agents | Entities participating in economic activities — households, firms, govt, FIs | Chunk 002 |
| Financial System | Integrated set of FIs, markets, instruments, services that transfer/allocate funds | Chunk 002 |
| Financial institution | Organisation acting as mobiliser/depositor of savings and purveyor of credit | Chunk 002 |
| Financial market | Mechanism enabling participants to deal with financial claims | Chunk 002 |
| Financial instrument | Claim against a person/institution for future payment of money and/or periodic interest/dividend | Chunk 002 |
| Derivative | Contract whose value derives from an underlying asset/index | Chunk 002 |
| Cryptocurrency | Decentralised digital assets based on blockchain | Chunk 002 |
| Money market | Trades highly liquid short-term (<1 yr) instruments | Chunk 002 |
| Capital market | Trades medium/long-term securities (≥1 yr) | Chunk 002 |
| Primary market | Deals in new issues; supplies fresh capital | Chunk 002 |
| Secondary market | Deals in already-issued securities; provides liquidity | Chunk 002 |
| Sayers' distinction | Banks = creators of credit; non-banks = purveyors | Chunk 002 |
| Forex market | Market in currency pairs; most liquid market in the world | Chunk 002 |
| Cash instrument | Instrument whose value is determined directly by markets | Chunk 003 |
| Primary security (direct) | Issued directly by ultimate borrower to ultimate user | Chunk 003 |
| Secondary security | Securities purchased from other investors | Chunk 003 |
| Direct finance | Funds flow from saver to borrower without intermediary | Chunk 003 |
| Indirect finance | Funds flow via a third-party intermediary | Chunk 003 |
| Financial intermediary | Entity that takes funds from savers and lends to borrowers; issues own securities | Chunk 003 |
| Spread (banking) | Lending rate − deposit rate; bank's profit margin | Chunk 003 |
| Broker | Mediator that matches buyers and sellers; doesn't take a position; earns commission | Chunk 003 |
| Commercial bank | Accepts deposits, lends to individuals/businesses/govts | Chunk 003 |
| Development bank | Provides medium/long-term finance for industry/agriculture | Chunk 003 |
| Insurance company | Collects premiums for risk coverage; invests them | Chunk 003 |
| Pension fund | Manages retirement savings; invests in diversified portfolio | Chunk 003 |
| Credit union | Member-owned cooperative non-profit bank-like institution | Chunk 003 |
| NBFI | Non-banking financial intermediary (mutual funds, insurance, pension funds) | Chunk 002 |
| Financial Intermediation | Process channelling funds between surplus and deficit parties | Chunk 004 |
| NPA (Non-Performing Asset) | High overdue lender exposure; major weakness post-1991 | Chunk 004 |
| Organised sector (Indian FS) | Under regulatory purview of RBI/SEBI; commercial/development/cooperative banks, stock market, NBFIs | Chunk 004 |
| Unorganised sector (Indian FS) | Informal credit market — money lenders, indigenous bankers | Chunk 004 |
| Balance Sheet | Statement of assets, liabilities, net worth at a point in time | Chunk 005 |
| Net Worth | Assets − Liabilities; value of the firm | Chunk 005 |
| Depreciation | Loss of asset value from wear and tear | Chunk 005 |
| GAAP | Generally Accepted Accounting Principles | Chunk 005 |
| Cash Flow Statement | Statement of cash inflows/outflows (operating/investing/financing) | Chunk 005 |
| Income Statement | Accrual-based statement of revenues recognised and expenses incurred | Chunk 005 |
| Real assets | Tangible assets (real estate, land, machinery) with intrinsic value | Chunk 005 |
| Financial assets | Intangible assets (stocks, bonds, MFs, currencies) representing claims | Chunk 005 |
| PPE | Property, Plant, Equipment; long-term real assets at cost less depreciation | Chunk 005 |
| REITs | Real Estate Investment Trusts; financial assets investing in real estate | Chunk 005 |
| National Income | Total income earned by a country's residents in a year | Chunk 005 |
| National Product | Total value of goods/services produced in a country in a year | Chunk 005 |
| GDP | Compilation of value-added output | Chunk 005 |
| GNP | GDP at factor cost + foreign income | Chunk 005 |
| NNP | GNP − depreciation | Chunk 005 |
| Flow of Funds Accounts (FFA) | Macro tool tracking funds across sectors and instruments | Chunk 005 |
| Goldsmith's deepening ratios | FR, FIR, NIR, IR (1969) | Chunk 005 |
| Finance Ratio (FR) | Total Financial Claims / National Income | Chunk 005 |
| FIR | Financial Assets / Physical Assets (NDCF) | Chunk 005 |
| NIR | Primary Issues / NDCF | Chunk 005 |
| Intermediation Ratio (IR) | Total Secondary Issues / Total Primary Issues | Chunk 005, 006 |
| Stock | Quantity/value at a point in time | Chunk 006 |
| Flow | Rate of change over a period | Chunk 006 |
| Exchange rate | Value of one currency in terms of another | Chunk 006 |
| Gold Standard | 1875 system fixing currency values in gold | Chunk 006 |
| Bretton Woods | Post-WWII system fixing currencies to USD; collapsed early 1970s | Chunk 006 |
| Floating exchange rate | Rate determined by supply/demand in markets | Chunk 006 |
| Barter system | Exchange of goods/services for goods/services without money | Chunk 006 |
| Mortgage | Cost-of-purchase loan; usually 15–30 years | Chunk 006 |
| Government borrowing | Govt raising money by issuing bonds/securities | Chunk 006 |
| Budget deficit | Govt expenditure − revenue in a year | Chunk 006 |
| Crowding out | Higher interest rates and reduced private borrowing due to heavy govt borrowing | Chunk 006 |
| Nominal interest rate | Stated accounting rate; ignores inflation | Chunk 006 |
| Real interest rate | Inflation-adjusted true return | Chunk 006 |
| Fisher Effect | (1+i) = (1+R)(1+h); simplified i = R + h | Chunk 006 |
| Monetary Policy | Tools used by central bank to control money supply | Chunk 006 |
