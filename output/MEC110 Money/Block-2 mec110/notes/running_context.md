# Running Context

## Key Concepts Introduced
- **Money** (Chunk 001): Anything generally acceptable as a means of payment in the settlement of transactions, including debt.
- **Near Money** (Chunk 001): Highly liquid assets that are not cash but can easily be converted into cash.
- **Liquidity** (Chunk 001): Ease and speed with which an asset can be converted to cash without loss of value.
- **Barter Economy** (Chunk 001): Economy without money where goods/services are exchanged directly; suffers from "double coincidence of wants".
- **Four Functions of Money** (Chunk 001): Medium of Exchange, Unit of Account, Store of Value, Standard of Deferred Payment.
- **Monetary Aggregates M0–M4** (Chunk 001): Successively broader measures of money supply (M0 = monetary base; M4 = broadest).
- **High-Powered Money / Monetary Base** (Chunk 001 & 004): Fully liquid claims on the central bank: currency in circulation + bank reserves.
- **Payment Systems (India)** (Chunk 001): RTGS, NEFT, IMPS, UPI, BBPS, NETC.
- **CBDC / Digital Rupee (e₹)** (Chunk 001): Legal tender issued by a central bank in digital form.
- **Cryptocurrency** (Chunk 001): Decentralized digital currency on blockchain (e.g., Bitcoin, Ethereum).
- **Credit Channel of Monetary Policy** (Chunk 002): Bank Lending Channel + Balance Sheet Channel — Bernanke (1993).
- **Money Demand** (Chunk 002): Desire to hold cash/liquid assets instead of less liquid investments.
- **Quantity Theory of Money** (Chunk 002 & 003): MV = PT (Fisher) and MV = PY (income version).
- **Cambridge / Neoclassical Theory** (Chunk 003): Md = k(PY); Marshall, Pigou, Robertson, Keynes.
- **Liquidity Preference Theory** (Chunk 003): Keynes' three motives — transaction, precautionary, speculative.
- **Baumol-Tobin Model** (Chunk 003): EOQ-style cash management; A = √(bY/2i).
- **Tobin's Speculative Demand Theory** (Chunk 003): Portfolio of cash + bonds based on risk-return optimization.
- **Friedman's Quantity Theory** (Chunk 003): Money demand as demand for an asset; Md = f(Yp, W, im, ib, ie, πe, u)P.
- **Money Multiplier** (Chunk 004 & 005): m = (cd + 1)/(cd + rr + er).
- **Endogenous Money Supply Theory** (Chunk 005): Money supply determined by credit demand and bank lending behaviour, not solely by central bank.

## Definitions (⭐ exam-important)
- **Money** (Chunk 001): Anything that functions as means of payment, unit of account, and store of value.
- **Near Money** (Chunk 001): Highly liquid non-cash assets convertible to cash (e.g., money-market funds, T-bills, foreign currencies).
- **Money Supply** (Chunk 001): Total amount of money available in an economy at a particular point in time (a stock).
- **Liquidity Trap** (Chunk 003): Situation where interest rates reach minimum, further fall is unexpected, and monetary policy is ineffective.
- **Capital Gain** (Chunk 003): Profit due to price appreciation of an asset (e.g., bond price rise).
- **Velocity of Money** (Chunk 003): Number of times a unit of money changes hands in a given period.
- **High-Powered Money (H)** (Chunk 004): H = C + R; foundation of broader money supply.
- **Money Multiplier (m)** (Chunk 004): m = M/H = (cd + 1)/(cd + rr + er).
- **Required Reserves (RR)** (Chunk 004): Mandated fraction of deposits banks must hold.
- **Excess Reserves (ER)** (Chunk 004): Reserves held by banks above the required amount.

## Named Models / Laws / Theories
- **Fisher's Quantity Equation** (Chunk 002 & 003): MV = PT.
- **Cambridge Equation** (Chunk 003): Md = k(PY).
- **Keynes' Liquidity Preference Theory** (Chunk 003): Md = αY + βY + (P×W)·f(i).
- **Baumol-Tobin Model** (Chunk 003): Q* = √(iY/2b); Average holding A = √(bY/2i).
- **Tobin's Portfolio Theory** (Chunk 003): R = βδ; bond–cash combination via indifference curves.
- **Friedman's Modern Quantity Theory** (Chunk 003): Md = f(Yp, W, im, ib, ie, πe, u)P.
- **Money Multiplier Formula** (Chunk 004): m = (cd + 1)/(cd + rr + er); simple deposit multiplier = 1/rr.
- **Bernanke's Credit Channel** (Chunk 002): Bank Lending Channel + Balance Sheet Channel.
