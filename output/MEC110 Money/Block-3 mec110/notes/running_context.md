- **Goals of monetary policy** (Chunk 001): high employment, growth, price stability, interest-rate stability, financial-market stability, FX-market stability.
- **Three criteria for targets/indicators** (Chunk 001): measurability, controllability, predictable effect.
- **Money-supply vs interest-rate target rule** (Chunk 001): IS shock → money supply; LM shock → interest rate.
- **Conventional instruments** (Chunk 001): policy rate, reserve requirements, OMO; tightening vs loosening.
- **Unconventional instruments** (Chunk 001): QE, FG, NIR, OMF, DM; macro-prudential.
- **Lender of Last Resort (LoLR)** (Chunk 001): rediscovered role post-GFC.
- **Helicopter money** (Chunk 001): money-financed fiscal transfer; needs CB-govt cooperation.
- **Macro-prudential policy** (Chunk 001 / 002): system-wide stability, beyond individual institutions.
- **Transmission mechanism** (Chunk 001): dynamic (lagged) + stochastic (uncertain).
- **Zero Lower Bound (ZLB)** (Chunk 001): floor on nominal rate; trigger for unconventional tools.
- **Financial stability** (Chunk 002): necessary but not sufficient for CB mandate.
- **G-20 coordination** (Chunk 002): post-GFC forum for regulatory response.
- **Inflation Targeting (IT)** (Chunk 002): primacy of price stability; ±2% band around 4% CPI.
- **Multiple Indicator Approach** (Chunk 002): pre-IT RBI framework.
- **Multi-indicator approach (post-IT)** (Chunk 002): money supply + other variables as guides.
- **Monetary Policy Committee (MPC)** (Chunk 002): 6 members, 4 meetings/yr, Governor's casting vote.
- **Tolerance band** (Chunk 002): 4% ± 2%; breach for 3 consecutive quarters = failure.
- **LAF (Liquidity Adjustment Facility)** (Chunk 002): June 2000; principal operative instrument; daily liquidity.
- **Quantitative vs Qualitative Instruments** (Chunk 002): volume vs direction of credit.
- **Fiscal dominance / FRBM Act** (Chunk 002): phasing out monetisation of fiscal deficit.
- **Forecasting and Policy Analysis System (FPAS)** (Chunk 002): RBI's disaggregated inflation model.

## Definitions (⭐ exam-important)
- **Goal / Objective** (Chunk 001): ultimate outcome variable mandated to the central bank.
- **Target / Intermediate Target** (Chunk 001): observable variable with predictable impact on goals.
- **Indicator / Operational Target** (Chunk 001): variable directly responsive to instruments, used to influence targets.
- **Instrument / Tool** (Chunk 001): direct lever (OMO, reserve reqts, bank rate, etc.).
- **Conventional monetary policy tools** (Chunk 001): policy rate, reserve requirements, OMO.
- **Unconventional policy instrument** (Chunk 001): does not depend for operation on changing risk-free nominal interest rate now or in future.
- **Quantitative Easing (QE)** (Chunk 001): large-scale CB asset purchase; unsterilised swap of CB money for privately held assets, long-term/uncertain duration.
- **Lender of Last Resort (LoLR)** (Chunk 001): traditional emergency liquidity role.
- **Monetary policy transmission mechanism** (Chunk 001): instrument → target relationship, mediated by macro variables.
- **Dynamic transmission** (Chunk 001): delayed effect from instrument to target; medium-term horizon (≈2 years).
- **Financial stability** (Chunk 002): persistent robust functioning of markets/institutions/infrastructure absorbing shocks with minimal disruption.
- **Macro-prudential policy** (Chunk 002): prudential tools for systemic, not individual, stability.
- **Inflation Targeting (IT)** (Chunk 002): strategy maintaining price level at a specified value/range.
- **Multiple Indicator Approach** (Chunk 002): pre-IT framework using inflation, growth, employment, banking, FX jointly.
- **Monetary Policy Committee (MPC)** (Chunk 002): six-member statutory committee deciding rates by majority vote.
- **Tolerance band** (Chunk 002): ±2% around 4% CPI; 3 consecutive quarters of breach = failure.
- **Quantitative / General Instruments** (Chunk 002): bank rate, repo, reverse repo, CRR, SLR, OMO.
- **Qualitative / Selective Instruments** (Chunk 002): margin reqts, consumer credit regln, publicity, credit rationing, moral suasion, directives, direct action.
- **Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF)** (Chunk 002): June 2000; principal operative instrument for daily liquidity.
