## Section: Unit 9 Terminal Questions 🔴

### Core Idea
Twelve terminal questions span Unit 9's full scope — FIT, NBFIs, capital markets, credit channel, interest-rate channel, yield-curve dynamics, asset-price channel, and fiscal-monetary coordination.

### Examples
1. How does the **Flexible Inflation Targeting framework** impact monetary policy in India?
   *Pointer:* repo rate as policy rate (Chunk 005), 4% ± 2% CPI band (Chunk 002), MPC governance (Chunk 002).
2. What role do **non-banking financial institutions** play in transmitting monetary policy?
   *Pointer:* fixed-income returns + money-market liquidity participation; insurance/pension/mutual funds (Chunk 005).
3. How do central-bank actions affect **capital markets** and the broader economy?
   *Pointer:* bond pricing, equity valuation, cost of capital, signalling effects, OMO/asset-purchase impact (Chunk 005).
4. What is the influence of the **credit channel** on borrowing costs?
   *Pointer:* policy rate → bank cost of funds → lending rates; regulatory tools (capital adequacy, LTV) (Chunk 005).
5. How do changes in central-bank policy rates affect **credit availability**?
   *Pointer:* lending-rate adjustments, liquidity provision, regulatory tools (Chunk 005).
6. What impact does **monetary-policy tightening** have on investment decisions?
   *Pointer:* higher borrowing costs, tighter lending standards, reduced credit supply, slower growth, falling asset prices (Chunk 005).
7. How do central-bank actions influence **short-term interest rates**?
   *Pointer:* policy-rate pass-through, forward guidance, liquidity provision (Chunk 005-006); call money rate as proxy.
8. What are the effects of **lowering interest rates** on consumption and investment?
   *Pointer:* cheaper consumer loans → spending ↑; cheaper business loans → capex ↑ (Chunk 006).
9. Why is understanding **yield-curve dynamics** important for policymakers?
   *Pointer:* expectations of future policy, inflation, economic outlook; steepening/flattening signals (Chunk 006).
10. How do central-bank actions influence **asset prices**?
    *Pointer:* discount-rate effect, direct purchases (QE), expectation channels; cuts → asset prices ↑ (Chunk 006).
11. How are the **wealth effects** associated with rising asset prices?
    *Pointer:* three sub-channels — portfolio value, perceived security, collateral; reverse on declining prices (Chunk 006).
12. Why is **coordination between monetary and fiscal authorities** crucial for policy effectiveness?
    *Pointer:* aligned policies support recovery, stabilise markets, boost credibility, reduce uncertainty, address structural imbalances (Chunk 006).

> **Quick Recall:**
> - **Q1-Q3** = framework + intermediaries.
> - **Q4-Q6** = credit channel.
> - **Q7-Q9** = interest-rate channel + yield curve.
> - **Q10-Q11** = asset-price channel.
> - **Q12** = fiscal-monetary coordination.
