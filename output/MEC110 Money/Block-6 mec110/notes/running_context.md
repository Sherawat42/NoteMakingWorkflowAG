## Key Concepts Introduced
- **Diversification** (Chunk 001): spreading investments across assets/asset-classes to reduce risk and optimise returns.
- **Portfolio** (Chunk 001): collection of investments owned by an entity (stocks, bonds, MFs, real estate, etc.).
- **Portfolio management** (Chunk 001): overseeing and optimising investment collections; balances risk-return; ongoing process.
- **Stages of portfolio management** (Chunk 001): policy → strategy → selection → monitoring → review.
- **Risk-aversion** (Chunk 001): most investors accept risk only with adequate reward; degrees vary.
- **Total risk** (Chunk 001): variability of an asset's returns; = diversifiable + non-diversifiable.
- **Diversifiable / unsystematic / unique risk** (Chunk 001): firm-specific; eliminable via diversification.
- **Non-diversifiable / systematic / market risk** (Chunk 001): macro-level; cannot be diversified away.
- **Variance & Standard Deviation as risk measures** (Chunk 001): σ² = ΣP·[Rᵢ−E(R)]²; σ = √σ².
- **Expected return** (Chunk 001): E(R) = ΣRᵢPᵢ.

## Definitions (⭐ exam-important)
- **Diversification** (Chunk 001): spreading investments across assets/asset-classes to mitigate risk and optimise returns.
- **Portfolio management** (Chunk 001): practice of overseeing and optimising investment collections — allocating resources, balancing risk-return, adapting to market.
- **Total risk** (Chunk 001): overall variability of returns; combines diversifiable + non-diversifiable.
- **Diversifiable risk** (Chunk 001): firm-specific risk affecting individual stocks; reducible by diversification.
- **Systematic risk** (Chunk 001): market-wide risk from macroeconomic / geopolitical factors; non-diversifiable.
- **Variance σ²** (Chunk 001): Σ Pᵢ · [Rᵢ − E(R)]².
- **Standard deviation σ** (Chunk 001): √variance.
- **Expected return** (Chunk 001): Σ Rᵢ Pᵢ.
