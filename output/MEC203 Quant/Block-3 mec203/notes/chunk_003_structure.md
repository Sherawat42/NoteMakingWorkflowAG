## Section: §10.3 Geometrical Interpretation of Derivatives 🟡

## Section: §10.4 Differentials 🔴
- df(x) = f'(x) Δx; for independent variable, dx = Δx but Δy ≠ dy in general.
- Rules: d(c)=0, d(u±v)=du±dv, d(uv)=u dv+v du, quotient rule for differentials.
- Used heavily in integration.

## Section: §10.5 Higher Order Derivatives 🟡
- Second/third/n-th derivatives. Example: y = 4x⁵+7x⁴+3x+9 → derivatives.

## Section: §10.6 Application of Simple Derivatives in Economics 🔴
- §10.6.1 Average Revenue (AR = p) vs Marginal Revenue (MR = dR/dq = f(q) + q f'(q)). Relation MR = AR + q f'(q); MR < AR if AR slopes down.
- §10.6.2 Average Cost (AC = c/q) vs Marginal Cost (MC = dc/dq). Relation: d/dq(AC) = (1/q)(MC − AC); MC < AC if AC falling.
- §10.6.3 Elasticity of Demand: arc vs point; eₐ = (p/q)(dq/dp); classification (perfectly inelastic, inelastic, unitary, elastic, perfectly elastic).
- Income elasticity (Engel curve); luxury/necessities/inferior categorization.
- §10.6.4 Constant elasticity demand q = Apᵅ ⇒ ε_d = α everywhere; α = −1 ⇒ rectangular hyperbola.

## Section: §10.7 Slope and Curvature 🔴
- f'(x) sign → monotonic increasing/decreasing.
- f''(x) > 0 convex, f''(x) < 0 concave, f''(x) = 0 linear.
- Tabular cross of f' & f'' signs (six cases).
- Worked examples (5x²+6 convex; demand p = aq²+bq+c elasticity; q = aq^b MR analysis).
