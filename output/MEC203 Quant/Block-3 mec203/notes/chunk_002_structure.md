## Section: Unit 10 Front Matter & Derivative Definition 🔴
- §10.0 objectives: derivatives; geometric interpretation; economic applications; slope/curvature; Taylor, MVT, L'Hôpital.
- §10.2 Derivative defined as `f'(x) = lim_{h→0} [f(x+h)−f(x)]/h`.
- Increment notation Δx, Δy.
- Differentiability ⇒ continuity (not converse). Example: |x| at 0 (continuous, not differentiable).
- Right/left derivatives Rf'(x), Lf'(x); both must exist & be equal.
