## Section: §12.2 Indefinite Integrals 🔴
- Reverse of differentiation; ∫ f(x) dx = F(x) + C.
- F(x) not unique (constant of integration C).
- §12.2.1 Integral sign as elongated S (sum of infinitesimal areas).

## Section: §12.3 Properties of Indefinite Integrals 🔴
- (1a) ∫ k f(x) dx = k ∫ f(x) dx (constant factors out).
- (1b) Power rule: ∫ xⁿ dx = x^{n+1}/(n+1) + C, n ≠ −1.
- (2) Linearity over sum: ∫(f+g) dx = ∫f dx + ∫g dx.
- §12.3.1 Worked examples (Examples 12.2–12.8).
- §12.3.2 Method of Substitution: substitute u = g(x), integrate, back-substitute.
- Worked Examples 12.9–12.13 (e.g., (2x⁴−5)⁵ x³ → u = 2x⁴−5).
