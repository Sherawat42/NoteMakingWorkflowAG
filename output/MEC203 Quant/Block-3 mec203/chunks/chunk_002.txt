The derivative of a function measures the function's rate of change. To
understand the concept, consider the increment of a variable (x) in changing

If in a functiony = f(x), the independent variable x takes an increment Ax(or
h), then Ay(or k) denotes the corresponding increment of y, i.e., of f(x).

Thus, we have, y + Ay = f(x + Ax) ie., Ay = f(x + Ax) — f(x)
