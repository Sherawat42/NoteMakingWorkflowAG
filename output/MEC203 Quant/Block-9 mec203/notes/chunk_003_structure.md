## Section: Three Fundamental Distributions Derived from Normal — Chi-square 🔴
<!-- Reason: foundational distribution; used in tests; derivation typically asked -->

### Key Concepts
- χ² with 1 df = square of standard normal (Z²)
- χ² with n df = sum of n independent Z²
- p.d.f formula
- Shape: positively skewed; for n>2, mode at n-2; for n≤2 monotonically decreasing
- Additive property: χ²(n₁) + χ²(n₂) = χ²(n₁+n₂)
- Derivation by mathematical induction (Jacobian polar transformation)

### Definitions ⭐
- Chi-square variate
- Degrees of freedom
- Additive property of χ²
