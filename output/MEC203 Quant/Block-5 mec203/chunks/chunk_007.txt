18.6 LET US SUM UP

The crucial concepts of convexity and concavity were thoroughly covered in
this unit. Uses of concave and convex functions are common in economics,
and they serve as a basis for the concept of optimisation, a fundamental and
often occurring concept in economics. Further, the usage of second
derivatives in relation to these functions was provided. Certain requirements
and conditions of convex and concave functions along with the idea of quasi-
concavity and quasi-convexity was also highlighted. The other part of the unit
focused on the fundamentals of restricted optimisation, which can be broken
down into two primary categories: constrained optimisation under equality
constraints and constrained optimisation under inequality constraints. The
complimentary slackness requirements that demonstrate that the primal and
dual values are the same have also been presented in this unit. In addition,
Kuhn-Tucker requirements for the general mathematical programming
problem, such as non-negative and free variables, equality and inequality
constraints, are included.
