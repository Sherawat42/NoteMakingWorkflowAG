24.2 DISCOUNTING

Perhaps we all have heard the maxim — Money received today is more than

money received tomorrow; and this message is true in the world even without
inflation. The question therefore begs, why that-discounting happens. The answer
lies with the fact, that money is attached with interest it can accrue. Consider the
case of simple interest for one year being 10%. If we invest € 1 today, we will get
1.1 after a year from now. In other words, one can be indifferent between 7 1
today and € 1.1 a year after one year from today, given market interest rate 10%.

We use present values to compare cost and benefits that occur at different points

in time.
