22.3. THE CALCULUS OF VARIATIONS

The origin of calculus of variations can be traced back to John Bernoulli who in
year 1696 posed a problem called brachistochrone problem: (If a small object
moves under the influence of gravity which path between two fixed points
enables it to make the trip in the shortest time). The problem was solved by him
and independently by his brother James in 1697. Other eminent mathematician
who contributed to this branch of calculus include Euler, Lagrange, Legendre,
Jacobi, Hamilton, Weierstrass, Hilbert.

Next, we discuss fundamental result, of Calculus of Variations, viz, Euler-
Lagrange Equation, also known as Euler-Lagrange Condition, or even E-L
condition, EL equation etc.)
