22.0 OBJECTIVES

After going through this unit, you should be able to:

e distinguish between static and dynamic models as well as between
continuous and discrete dynamic models

e enumerate various dynamic optimization approaches, state the essential idea
of each approach, state the theoretical results which form the foundations of
the approach, and use each one for solving dynamic optimisation problems.
More explicitly,

e explain the Calculus of Variations approach, state the foundational theorem
justifying the basis of the approach, and use it in solving appropriate
problems.

e Explain the dynamic programming approach for dynamic optimization, and
using it derive optimality criteria of discrete dynamic optimisation.
