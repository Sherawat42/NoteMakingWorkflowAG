24.1 INTRODUCTION

In this concluding unit of Block 7, by using the formula derived as well as
explained in Unit 23, we exemplify the beauty of dynamic optimisation.
Therefore, a grasp of contents of Unit 23 is prerequisite for reading Unit-24.
Sourced from established literature, the Unit 24 presents six applications-3 each
from microeconomics and macroeconomics, which demonstrate model
formulation, optimal solution & necessary analysis. The concept of dynamic
optimisation is well founded and its application in economics is ever expanding.
Unit 24 is a humble yet solid endeavour to innervate students for exploring the
vast area of applied dynamic optimisation in economics.
