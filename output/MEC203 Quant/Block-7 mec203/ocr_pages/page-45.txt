23.1.4 Optimal control theory: Summary

We start our discussion of ‘optimal control theory’ with mentioning the fact that
the essential difference between ‘Calculus of Variations’ and ‘Optimal control
Theory’ is the additional use of ‘control variable’ in the latter.

Combined with Example 5 above, in this context, let us recall the recent
news (dated: June 04, 2023): OPEC+ member countries consider more

production cuts as oil prices slump.

The above is the case of use of control variable: Despite the availability of
technology and capacity for production, the oil will not be produced according
to maximum capacity because of the above decision which stipulates the upper
limit on the production over a period of time.

A control variable is external to the production-consumption system.
