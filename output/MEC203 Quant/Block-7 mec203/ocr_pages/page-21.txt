Suppose x is a single case then the solution for Euler-Lagrange equation is a and
order differential equation. We have two integration constants and these
integration constants can be found from initial and final condition of problem.
Suppose x is n variable case then we shall get 2n constants and those can be got

from initial and final conditions.

In conclusion Euler-Lagrange equation is necessary condition for optimisation
of a functional.

Remarks:

1) Euler-Lagrange equation is non-linear differential equation since product of
x”(t), x’(t) and x(t) may appear in equation.

2) Solution involve Two-point boundary value problem.

3) Solution through numerical technique and solution through analytic

methods are very difficult.

4) If Euler-Lagrange equation is not satisfied for any function, this indicates
the optimum does not exist for that functional.
