23.1 INTRODUCTION

In the previous unit, we have discussed the problem of optimisation of a
functional under integration sign by using calculus of variations. Moreover, we
have noted various end-point conditions with their transversality conditions. We
derived necessary and sufficient conditions for extremum. Here we apply
calculus of variations to find solutions to optimal control problems.

Before we go into details of ‘Optimal Control Theory’, we summarise our
discussion, so far, of optimisation, and in the process, introduce some concepts,
essential for any further discussion on Optimisation.
